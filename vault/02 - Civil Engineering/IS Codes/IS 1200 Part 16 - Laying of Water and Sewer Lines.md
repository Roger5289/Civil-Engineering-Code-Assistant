# IS 1200 (Part XVI):1979 — Method of Measurement: Laying of Water and Sewer Lines Including Appurtenant Items

Part of the [[IS 1200 - Method of Measurement Index|IS 1200]] series. General series rules in [[IS 1200 Part 1 - Earthwork]]. Source: `...\1200_16.pdf` (9 pages). Extracted text: `<local extracted text>\IS 1200\IS-1200-Part16-fulltext.txt`. In-building water supply/plumbing/drains fixtures are covered by [[IS 1200 Part 19 - Water Supply Plumbing and Drains]], not here — this Part is the *trunk infrastructure* (mains, sewers, manholes) side.

## General rules for this Part (Cl 2)
- Work-measured-separately list matches the series default (water/mud/foul/tides) but **drops snow** and specifically calls out **special pumping (causes other than rain/subsoil water) measured in kilolitres of water** against a separate provision.
- **Excavation/earthwork for pipelines: per [[IS 1200 Part 1 - Earthwork]].** **Beds, benchings, covering: per [[IS 1200 Part 2 - Concrete Works]].**

## Water lines (Cl 3)
- **Pipes classified** by diameter, length-per-pipe, material, quality, jointing method — **running metres inclusive of all joints**, measured along the pipe/fitting centreline. **All fittings/specials enumerated separately as extra-over** the pipe run; cutting+jointing to fittings/specials deemed included in the fitting's own item.
- **Alternative method:** classify by diameter/material/quality only, measure running metres **between** fittings/specials (not through them); all joints/fittings/specials then fully described and enumerated separately, with cutting-for-jointing deemed included in the fitting item.
- Suspended/vertical pipes — described as such; supports/fixings measured separately (Cl 3.4.2).
- Testing — running metres, unless stated otherwise.
- **Miscellaneous:** valve cisterns, public fountain platforms, fire hydrants — fully described, enumerated. Supports (hangers, pillars), railway/culvert crossings, pavement cutting/reconditioning, pipeline/cable deviation, masonry dismantling/reconstruction — measured per the relevant IS standard for that specific work.

## Sewer lines (Cl 4)
Same running-metre + extra-over-fittings structure as water lines (Cl 3), classified by diameter/length/material/quality/jointing.
- **Manholes/inspection chambers:** measured in detail per relevant IS standards, **or alternatively** described+enumerated, **classified by depth band** (≤0.5 m, 0.5–1 m, 1–2 m, 2–3 m, etc.) — **depth = distance from manhole-cover top to the main drain's invert level.**
- **Appurtenant items** (ventilating shafts, pumping mains, etc.) — described+enumerated, or measured in detail per relevant IS standard.
- Testing — running metres, **between manholes**.
