# IS 1200 (Part 14):1984 — Method of Measurement: Glazing

Part of the [[IS 1200 - Method of Measurement Index|IS 1200]] series. General series rules in [[IS 1200 Part 1 - Earthwork]]. Source: `...\1200_14.pdf` (8 pages, incl. Amendment 1 — May 2002, which rewrites Cl 3.1.1's last sentence and all of Cl 3.2, both reflected below in their amended form). Extracted text: `<local extracted text>\IS 1200\IS-1200-Part14-fulltext.txt`.

## General rules (Cl 2)
Sheet/glass material kind described, measured separately. Wired glass states reinforcement design/pattern; frosted glass states one-side or both-sides. Wood/metal/concrete surrounds measured separately. **Glazing method** described and measured separately by classification, type+putty stated:
a) front-and-back putty, sprigged or fixed with glazing pins;
b) bedded in putty, fixed with beads;
c) bedded in rubber/velvet, fixed with beads (where required).

## Measurement (Cl 3)
- **Base unit: m², thickness stated.**
- **Pane dimensions = clear opening dimensions + width of the window/door frame's rebates.** A non-rectangular pane is measured as the **smallest rectangular area it could be cut from**. **(As amended 2002) Cutting to make the circumscribing rectangle is deemed included in the item** — this replaced the original wording which separately deemed "straight cutting" included; the amendment folds any cutting needed to reach the bounding rectangle into the item.
- **(As amended 2002) Cutting required for non-rectangular/non-square panes is also deemed included in the item — the required shape must be detailed in the drawings or otherwise specified.** This **replaced** the pre-2002 rule that measured "circular cutting" (defined broadly to include any curve) as an **extra**, running metres — a genuine substantive change: circular/curved cutting used to be billed as extra-over and now is not, so **always check which edition a live contract cites** before assuming curved-pane cutting is chargeable separately.
- Glass and sheet louvres — described, enumerated.
- Hacking out old broken glass + preparing for new — m².
- Holes drilled — enumerated, diameter + glass/sheet type&thickness + pane size stated.
- Grinding, polishing, rounding off glass/sheet edges — described, running metres.
