# IS 1200 (Part II):1974 — Method of Measurement: Concrete Works

Part of the [[IS 1200 - Method of Measurement Index|IS 1200]] series. General series rules (clubbing, booking order, measurement precision, work measured separately, stages) are in [[IS 1200 Part 1 - Earthwork]] — this note only covers what's specific to concrete. Source: `...\1200_2.pdf` (15 pages, includes Amendments 1 & 2). Extracted text: `<local extracted text>\IS 1200\IS-1200-Part2-fulltext.txt`.

**Note:** road, piling, well-foundation and tunnelling concrete are covered by IS 1200 Parts XVII/XXIII/XXIV/XXV respectively, not here. Slab thickness is measured to the nearest **0.005 m** (tighter than the series-default 0.01 m).

## Classification (Cl 4.1.1)
Plain, reinforced and prestressed concrete are each measured **separately**; precast and cast-in-situ are kept **separate**. By structure type: bridges; dams/spillways; barrages/weirs; canal works; tunnels/shafts; harbour/dock/marine works; special structures (power houses, overhead reservoirs, chimneys, towers, silos); buildings; other.
- **Unit: cubic metres** unless stated otherwise.
- **Formwork measured separately** (→ [[IS 1200 Part 5 - Formwork]]).
- **Reinforcement measured separately** (→ IS 1200 Part 8) unless a composite concrete+reinforcement item is explicitly described — in which case items identical except for reinforcement must still be split out separately.
- **Special concrete** (cooled, heated, cellular, expansive, heat-resisting) — fully described, measured separately.
- **No deductions for:** dissimilar-material ends (beams, posts, girders, rafters, purlins, trusses, corbels, steps) ≤500 cm² cross-section; openings ≤0.1 m²; reinforcement volume; pipes/conduits/sheathing ≤100 cm² cross-section each; small voids ≤40 cm² each; moulds/drip-mouldings/chamfers/splays/coved angles/beds/grooves/rebates ≤10 cm wide or ≤15 cm girth; stops/mitres/returns/rounded ends/junctions.

## Cast-in-situ concrete (Cl 4.2)
Classified: foundations/footings/column bases; walls (any thickness, incl. pilasters/buttresses/plinth courses); slabs on wall/beam/column (incl. landings, balconies, canopies, bridge slabs); other slabs; CHAJJAS; lintels/shelves/beams/bressumers; columns/piers/abutments/pillars/posts/struts; staircases (stringer beams incl., landings excl.); balustrades/newels/railings; spiral staircases (landing incl.); arches; domes/vaults; shell roofs/arch ribs/folded plates; chimneys/shafts; canal lining; retaining/ballast/return walls; precast-filling concrete; kerbs/steps; string/lacing courses/parapets/copings/sills; cornices/moulded sills; louvers/fins/fascia.
- **Columns:** measured top of base → underside of first floor slab, then floor-slab-top → underside of slab above. Flat-slab column flares included with the column.
- **Beams:** face-to-face of columns, including haunches. Depth = bottom of slab to bottom of beam (top-of-slab to top-of-beam for an inverted beam).
- **CHAJJA:** measured inclusive of bearing (or as clear projection if combined with lintel/beam/slab). **A projecting RCC member ≤100 mm average thickness is a CHAJJA; >100 mm is measured as a slab.** Where fins/facias combine with a CHAJJA, the CHAJJA is measured clear between them and the fins/facias measured through.
- **Cavity walls:** cavity-forming measured in m² stating width/tie material/size/spacing; measured along the cavity's centre plane, deducting openings/solid portions; closing cavities at jambs/sills/heads — separate, running metres.
- **Casing to steel joists/stanchions:** m³; joist volume not deducted (boxed stanchions/girders: only the boxed portion is deducted).
- **Surface channels:** m³, channel volume deducted; round/elliptical/oval sections use ¾-width-at-top × average centre depth. Forming the channel — running metres, inner girth stated.

## Precast concrete (Cl 4.3, Table 1)
Includes mould use, face finishing, and reinforcement supply (or reinforcement measured separately, stated). Hoisting/setting-in-position included unless stated otherwise. **Table 1 units by component:** wall panels/floor-roof slabs → m²; beam units/columns/trusses → running m or number; channel units/purlins → running m or number; string courses/copings/bed plates/anchor blocks/sills/shelves/louvers/steps/staircases → running m or number; kerbs/edgings → running m or number; solid/hollow blockwork → m³ or m²; light-weight partitions → m² stating thickness; door/window frames → running m stating size; waffle units → m² or number; water tanks → number; JALLIES → m² of opening filled, stating thickness; fencing posts → number or m³; folded slab → m³.
- Fencing posts classified by average sectional area: ≤100 cm², 100–250 cm², >250 cm².

## Prestressed concrete (Cl 4.4–4.5)
- **Cast in situ:** structural members (columns/beams/slabs) measured separately; members cast in sections (not one continuous pour) fully described.
- Duct/groove forming and grouting/sealing — running metres, stating sleeve/sheathing size, temporary supports, grout composition. Air-hole forming/grouting — included in the duct item. Jacking/anchoring recess filling — m³, separately, stating exposed finish.
- **Wires/strands/cables:** measured in **kilograms** (between anchorages), stating ultimate strength, proof stress, wire/cable size — each size separate. No allowance for extra length at anchorages. De-greasing, straightening, cutting, assembly, cones/wedges/anchor-plates/spacers are deemed included.
- **Precast prestressed:** enumerated stating number/size/length/fixing method; hoisting/transport/assembly/setting included unless stated. Classified by tensioning method: pre-tensioned in mould; post-tensioned on ground after casting; cast-in-sections-assembled-then-post-tensioned; post-tensioned after hoisting (self-supporting or needing temporary support); cast-in-sections-assembled-in-situ-then-post-tensioned.

## Miscellaneous items (Cl 4.6)
- **Expansion joints:** running metres (depth/width stated) or m² (width stated); filling/covering material — separate, running metres; metal sheet strips (copper/brass/aluminium) — separate, running metres.
- **Damp-proof course:** m², stating thickness; includes formwork, fair-finished edges, substrate prep. Horizontal and vertical DPC measured separately.
- **Waterproofing:** admixture quantity — litres or kg, separate; surface-applied treatment — m², stating coats and material:water ratio.
- **Guniting:** finished surface — m².
- **Pack/pressure grouting:** grout holes — running metres (holes through steel liner plates are enumerated instead); grout pipes/fittings — kg (by weighment or known unit weight×length); water-pressure testing — enumerated per hole; grout quantity — by weight of cement actually forced in; stone dust/other additions — measured loose/dry by approved-size boxes, separately.
- **Stanchion-base grouting:** m², mix stated. **Holding-down bolts:** enumerated, stating hole depth/mix, grouting included.
- **Cutting in concrete:** grooves/chases — running metres, girth stated; holes/mortices/openings — per cm of cutting depth, classified ≤250 cm² and 250 cm²–0.1 m² (net required area, not actual cut area); openings >0.1 m² — m³, incl. temporary/permanent support provision. Exposing reinforcement without damage — m², depth stated.
- **Toothing and bonding** (new concrete wall to existing) — m².
