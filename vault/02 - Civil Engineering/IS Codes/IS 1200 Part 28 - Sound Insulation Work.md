# IS 1200 (Part 28):1992 — Method of Measurement: Sound Insulation Work

Final Part of the [[IS 1200 - Method of Measurement Index|IS 1200]] series. General series rules in [[IS 1200 Part 1 - Earthwork]]. Source: `...\1200_28.pdf` (5 pages). Extracted text: `<local extracted text>\IS 1200\IS-1200-Part28-fulltext.txt`. Like [[IS 1200 Part 26 - Acid Resistant Lining]], this Part explicitly adopts "the simplest type of measurement" after surveying major organizations, since no consistent prior convention existed for this niche trade.

## General rules (Cl 2–3)
Items may be clubbed together (this Part's version of the clubbing rule doesn't add the usual "on the basis of detailed description" qualifier — genuinely terser than the rest of the series). Bills of quantities fully describe materials+workmanship, accurately represent the work.

## Sound insulation treatment (Cl 4)
Classified by material used, each classification measured separately, stating: material specification; coat count+thickness per coat; surface-treatment nature; base nature; curved/conical/special/elliptical work (radius stated).

## Measurement (Cl 5)
- Dimensions to nearest 0.01 m — **but >25 m dimensions to nearest 0.1 m** (same relaxation as [[IS 1200 Part 27 - Earthwork Done by Mechanical Appliances]]). Areas to nearest 0.01 m².
- **Ceiling/false ceiling work, flooring work, roof work** — each measured on the basis of **finished work.**
- **Wall work** — actual finished area.
- **Curved walls, curved ceilings** — each measured **separately**, on the basis of finished surface measurement.
- Isolated-width work (bands, corners, window sills, door/window jambs, sunk panels) and rounded angles — measured separately.

## General rules, continued (Cl 6–7)
- Description includes rises and other roundings/shapes.
- Repairs — described, dubbing thickness stated if any.
- **Ceilings, walls, floors — always measured separately from each other** (never combined into one item).
- Isolated areas ≤1 m² each — described, nature stated.
- Curved/conical/spherical work — described separately, radius stated.
- **No deduction for openings ≤0.3 m²** (note: this Part's opening-deduction threshold is 0.3 m², distinct from the 0.4 m²/0.5 m² thresholds used elsewhere in the series — e.g. [[IS 1200 Part 10 - Ceiling and Linings]]'s 0.4 m² and [[IS 1200 Part 12 - Plastering and Pointing]]'s 0.5 m² — a genuine numeric difference worth checking before assuming any one threshold applies series-wide).
- **Height >10 m above ground/datum, no floor in between:** measured separately in **5 m stages.** **If a floor exists at ≤5 m height:** measured separately **per storey** instead — the same dual convention as [[IS 1200 Part 12 - Plastering and Pointing]]'s height rule.
