# IS 1200 (Part 24):1983 — Method of Measurement: Well Foundations

Part of the [[IS 1200 - Method of Measurement Index|IS 1200]] series. General series rules in [[IS 1200 Part 1 - Earthwork]]. Source: `...\1200_24.pdf` (8 pages, incl. Amendment 1). Extracted text: `<local extracted text>\IS 1200\IS-1200-Part24-fulltext.txt`. Covers caisson/well-sinking foundations (bridge piers etc.) — a niche but genuinely distinct trade from piling.

## General rules for this Part (Cl 2)
- Work-measured-separately: dry soil; **wet soil (30 cm above subsoil water)**; foul positions; tide-interrupted work. High/low water levels stated. *(Note: these conditions also cover removing obstructions other than those needing mechanical/special methods — see Cl 2.7 below.)*
- **Desired additional info for this trade:** general site/work description + river-bed cross-section showing low-water and high-flood levels; water-logging cause; strata through which wells will be sunk, or bore records.
- **Obstruction removal** met during sinking (other than the Cl 2.5 conditions) — measured separately **on a quantum-meruit basis** (i.e. billed for what was actually reasonably necessary, not a pre-fixed rate).

## Well sinking (Cl 3)
- Item includes use of **kentledge** (dead-weight ballast) to the extent needed for sinking + correcting the well's position.
- **Island provision** (if required) — measured separately.
- **Sinking — running metres, shape+size stated.** Measured from the level where the cutting edge is first pitched to its final resting level. *(Cutting-edge level = the plane joining the well curb's lowest portion, which cuts into the soil during sinking / acts as the penetration face.)*

## Steining and curb (Cl 4)
Concrete/brickwork/stone masonry in the steining, and concrete in the top plug+curb — described, **m³**, formwork included. **Concrete in the bottom plug (incl. sump, if any) — measured on the basis of cement bags consumed** (not volume). Filling in the well — m³, filling type stated. Reinforcement (incl. bars, links, binders, steel flats) — measured separately (→ [[IS 1200 Part 8 - Steelwork and Ironwork]]). Steel cutting edge + steel armouring (if done) — measured separately, also per Part 8. Cutting off extra steining height (where required) — m³.

## Pneumatic sinking (Cl 5)
- Works under different **working-pressure ranges** (per IS 4138-1977, Safety Code for Compressed Air) — measured separately.
- **Sinking — running metres, size+shape stated.** Depth = level where air is introduced to the level where air is stopped.
- **Measured separately:** bringing+removing the pneumatic plant; plant use when fitted on the well vs not fitted (two separate items); fixing/removing the adapter+airlock; corbel slab; keeping the well under pressure during plugging/guniting/repairing/inspection/testing (excluding sinking itself).
