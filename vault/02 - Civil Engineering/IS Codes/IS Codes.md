# IS Codes

Bureau of Indian Standards codes, built the same way as [[NBC 2026]] — page-anchored reference notes from the source PDFs, one note per code. Started 2026-09-11 after Code Design Assistant's Phase 1 locked IS 456 as the first IS code (NBC's own structural sections defer to it for real design values, so it was prioritized ahead of finishing NBC's Part C).

Source PDFs live under `<your codes folder>\IS CODES\`, organized by BIS committee (CED2 Cement & Concrete, CED37 Loads, CED39 Earthquake Engineering, CED7 Structural Steel, CED43 Foundations, CED44 Measurement, etc.) — that folder holds the whole BIS civil engineering library, not just what's been converted here.

## Notes in this folder

- [[IS 456 - Plain and Reinforced Concrete]] — IS 456:2000, the core RCC design code. Materials, durability (Table 5 is the daily-use core), general design (deflection control, stability), reinforcement & detailing (Cl 26 — development length, cover, spacing, min/max steel %), special members (walls/stairs/footings/flat slabs), limit state design (flexure, shear Table 19/20, torsion, cracking). **Audited 2026-09-11** (matching NBC's Phase 0 rigor): found and fixed 1 real error (Table 11), 1 confusing/self-contradictory table rewrite (Table 18), and 1 major completeness gap (all of Cl 26 was missing from the first draft). Two Table 15 cells remain unresolved even after checking the PDF page directly — flagged in place.
- [[IS 875 Part 1 - Dead Loads]] — unit weights of building materials (Table 1) and assembled components — ceilings, floors, roofs, walls (Table 2). Mostly a reference lookup table; commonly-used values pulled out, rest is alphabetical in the source.
- [[IS 875 Part 2 - Imposed Loads]] — occupancy floor loads (Table 1, the most-used table in the whole IS 875 set), roof loads (Table 2), parapet/balustrade loads (Table 3), load reduction rules, impact/vibration/crane allowances.
- [[IS 875 Part 3 - Wind Loads]] — the full V_z = V_b·k1·k2·k3 → design pressure → pressure/force coefficient methodology, internal pressure coefficient rules, dynamic-effects trigger. Shape-specific external pressure coefficient tables (dozens, by building/roof geometry) kept as reference-by-page, not transcribed — genuinely too dense and geometry-dependent to compress safely.
- [[IS 875 Part 4 - Snow Loads]] — niche (only J&K/HP/UP hill districts); S=μS₀ formula and shape coefficients by roof type. The code itself doesn't supply ground snow load values — that requires contacting DRDO Manali or IMD Pune directly.
- [[IS 875 Part 5 - Special Loads and Load Combinations]] — temperature, soil/hydrostatic pressure, fatigue, accidental loads (vehicle/crane impact, explosion, fire, air-raid shelters), and the general 12-combination load-combination list that IS 456's Table 18 supplies factors for.

**IS 875 (all 5 parts) completed and audited 2026-09-11.** The audit found real problems, worse than IS 456's: **Part 2's entire Table 1 (occupancy floor loads) had to be rebuilt** — the source PDF's two-column (UDL/concentrated-load) table layout doesn't survive linear text extraction, so values were swapping between adjacent rows and roughly half the rows were missing outright. Rebuilt row-by-row from word-level PDF coordinates (and one page rendered as an image) rather than the linear extraction. **Part 3's risk-coefficient table (k1) was missing an entire row** (25-year-life, low-hazard buildings) and had a backwards claim about which wind-speed zones get the bigger safety discount — both fixed. **Part 5's fire-load formula** had a guessed coefficient and wrong units, corrected against the source (Q = 1.2×t_b in kJ/m², not 1.25×t_b in MJ/m²). Part 1's data and Part 5's air-raid-shelter table were spot-checked and confirmed accurate — the pattern is specifically multi-column numeric tables, not single-value lists.

- [[IS 1893 Part 1 - Earthquake Resistant Design]] — IS 1893 (Part 1):2002, seismic design. Zone factors (Table 2), importance factor (Table 6), response reduction factor (Table 7), the A_h design-coefficient formula, fundamental period formulas, base shear distribution, torsion, soft-storey rules, storey-drift limit (0.004×height). Used the 2002 edition, not the superseded 1984 one that's also in the source folder. Annex E (zone factors by town) deliberately not reproduced — same multi-column extraction risk the IS 875 audit found, flagged in the note rather than risked. **Audited 2026-09-11** — much cleaner than IS 875: Table 7 verified perfect (all 11 rows), Table 1 had one row missing (now restored), everything else already read clean.

- [[IS 13920 - Ductile Detailing for Seismic Forces]] — IS 13920:1993 (1993 original, amended 2002 — **need to source the 2016 revision**, tracked in [[Active Priorities]]). Beam/column/joint/shear-wall ductile detailing: minimum steel ratios, capacity-design shear formulas, special confining reinforcement, boundary elements, the full Annex A shear-wall flexural-strength procedure. **Audited 2026-09-11** — cleaner than IS 875/1893 from the start (clause-based prose, not multi-column tables); confirmed ρ_min, the column shear coefficient, and the A_sh formulas (two independent hand cross-checks against the code's own worked examples, both matched exactly); resolved all formulas that hadn't survived the first draft's extraction by rendering the pages as images. Nothing was found wrong — the audit completed gaps rather than fixing errors.

- [[SP 16 - Design Aids for Reinforced Concrete]] — SP 16:1980, IS 456's design-chart/table companion (92 charts, 98 tables). Built 2026-09-11 as a **navigation guide** rather than full transcription — it's fundamentally a lookup book, not a rulebook. Captures the compact formulas/small tables that are safe to transcribe (x_u,max/d, P_u formula, e_min, τ_c derivation + τ_c,max Table J, hook/bend anchorage — all confirmed consistent with [[IS 456 - Plain and Reinforced Concrete]]) plus a full index mapping design scenario → chart/table number. Large numeric tables (development length by diameter×grade, slab moment-of-resistance grids) deliberately not transcribed — spot-checked and found to show the same multi-column scrambling risk as IS 875. **Audited 2026-09-11** — nothing wrong found; every transcribed formula/table re-verified against a page-image render, all correct.
- [[SP 34 - Handbook on Concrete Reinforcement and Detailing]] — SP 34:1987, the detailing-practice companion (how to draw/schedule reinforcement, not just calculate it). Built 2026-09-11 covering Sections 1 (steel types), 4 (cover, development length, Table 4.1 hook/bend anchorage, splicing) and 5 (bar-bending-schedule methodology) in depth, plus sampled Sections 7 (columns) and 9 (slabs) for standout practical rules not already in IS 456/13920. Bar-shape-code tables (Sections 5, and similar graphical content elsewhere) deliberately left as reference-by-page — inherently sketch-dependent. **Found a real edition-currency conflict:** SP 34's column tie-diameter minimum (Cl 7.2.6.2: ≥1/4 largest bar or 5mm) is superseded by IS 456:2000's verified 16mm minimum — flagged prominently in the note; IS 456:2000 governs. **Audited 2026-09-11** — confirmed the 5mm tie-diameter figure really is what the 1987 source prints (not an extraction error). Found one more genuine source-printing anomaly: Table 4.1's 90° bend value for an 8mm bar prints as 6.2cm, inconsistent with the 8φ rule (6.4cm) that every other row in the table follows exactly — corrected in the note with a flag rather than silently changed.

- [[IS 1200 - Method of Measurement Index]] — the 28-part method-of-measurement series (BOQ/billing side, distinct from the design codes above), built in full 2026-09-11 after the maintainer confirmed full-depth coverage over a core-trades-only or consolidated-note alternative. Covers every trade from earthwork and concrete through piling, tunneling, and sound insulation. See the index note for the full part list, the shared-general-rules pattern, and the numeric thresholds (opening deductions, dimension precision) that genuinely vary trade-to-trade rather than being one series-wide constant. **Audited 2026-09-11** — targeted the series' two genuinely tabular tables (Part 15, Part 22) via page-image verification rather than every part, given the rest is prose that's audited clean throughout this session; found and fixed two real errors in Part 22's materials table, Part 15's table was already fully correct.

- [[IS 800 - General Construction in Steel]] — IS 800:2007 (Third Revision, limit-state design), built and audited in full 2026-09-12, all 17 Sections + Annexes A–H. Right edition confirmed up front — the source folder also holds the superseded 1984 working-stress edition under a different filename, a real edition trap caught before building rather than after (unlike IS 13920, where the edition gap was only noticed and flagged, not resolved). Includes Amendment 1 (Jan 2012) applied inline. Covers materials/steel grades, general design requirements, limit-state safety factors, tension/compression/bending/combined-force member design, connections (bolted + welded), working stress design, seismic ductile detailing (OCBF/SCBF/OMF/SMF), fatigue, durability, fire resistance, and fabrication. **Audit found and fixed real errors** — bolt hole clearance table had columns swapped, the fire-resistance temperature formula had a division where the source uses multiplication, bolt combined shear+tension was logged as linear instead of quadratic and the bearing-bolt tension clause was missing entirely — and recovered three formulas (bolt prying force, Annex D column effective length, Annex C floor vibration) originally flagged as unrecoverable from the extraction.
