# IS 1200 (Part 17):1985 — Method of Measurement: Road Work Including Airfield Pavements

Part of the [[IS 1200 - Method of Measurement Index|IS 1200]] series. General series rules in [[IS 1200 Part 1 - Earthwork]]. Source: `...\1200_17.pdf` (11 pages). Extracted text: `<local extracted text>\IS 1200\IS-1200-Part17-fulltext.txt`. Third revision aligned this Part with IRC (Indian Roads Congress) codes, especially for earthwork and cement concrete pavements.

## General rules for this Part (Cl 2)
- Length/width to nearest 0.01 m; **thickness to nearest 0.005 m or the specified tolerance, whichever is less** (tighter, matching Part 8's steelwork precision, not the series default).
- **Finished thickness of sub-base/base/bituminous courses:** computed from levels taken **before and after construction**, on a grid **5 m centre-to-centre longitudinally** in straight reaches, **2.5 m on curves**. Transversely: 2-lane roads — 4 positions at 0.75 m and 2.75 m from each carriageway edge; single-lane roads — 2 positions at 1.25 m from each edge, plus crown+edges. Reference points for the transverse grid line are left embedded at both ends so the same grid points can be re-measured after each successive course. Widened-portion payment courses need at least one level line per widening strip; thickness there = the before/after level difference in that specific area.
- **Base unit: m²** unless stated. Length = between payment ends / expansion-joint centrelines; width = between payment edge and construction-joint centreline, or between joint centrelines.
- **Excavation/earthwork** for road alignment/embankment/drains/site-clearance → [[IS 1200 Part 1 - Earthwork]]. Rolling/watering formations — included with the item, or separate, m².
- **Demolition of roadwork** → IS 1200 Part 18 (Demolition and Dismantling). **Road-work materials** → IS 1200 Part 22 (Materials, not yet built in this vault).
- **Cut-formation prep, by unit:** loosening/recompacting subgrade — m²; removing unsuitable material — m³; replacing with suitable material — m³; preparing rocky subgrade — m².
- Stripping/storing/re-applying topsoil — m³.

## Roadwork generally (Cl 3)
Soling/sub-bases — thickness stated. Levelling course — m³ compacted-in-position volume, computed as surface area × average thickness (prismoidal formula per Cl 2.5.1's level data). Stripping/topsoil — m³. Loosening/recompacting original ground — m². Removing unsuitable embankment-foundation material + replacement — m³.

## Tar and bitumen roads (Cl 4)
- **Binder** (tar or bitumen): type/grade/penetration value stated; different binder types measured separately.
- **Priming** (water-bound macadam before surface dressing) — separate, primer type+quantity/m² stated.
- **Edging** — running metres, material+placing method described; excavation for it is deemed included, not separate.
- **Tack coat** — m² of application area, per the specified spread rate.
- **Bituminous macadam base/binder course** — finished work, **m³**. Filling potholes ≤0.005 m² — deemed included, not separate.
- **Bituminous penetration macadam base/binder course** — finished work, **m²**.
- **Built-up spray grout base course** — finished work, m².
- **Surface dressing** — each coat, finished work, m².
- **Open-graded premix carpet** — finished work, m².
- **Mix seal surfacing** — finished work, m².
- **Semi-dense carpet** — finished work, **m³**.
- **Asphaltic concrete** — finished work, **m³**.
- **Seal coat** — finished work, m².
- **Pre-fabricated bitumenized surfacing** — described; single-layer vs double-layer measured separately, m².

## Miscellaneous (Cl 5)
Screening (road metal/stone chips etc.) — m³ of total quantity to be screened. Breaking (stone/brick/road materials to size) — m³ of stacked material **after** breaking, before/after sizes stated, tracking included. Scarifying — m², depth+surface type stated; hand vs machine scarifying measured separately. Berms — running metres, average filling+width stated. Sub-bases/bases/shoulders (granular, stabilized-soil, or water-bound macadam) — each finished-work-in-position, m³. Potholes >0.005 m² — m³ (≤0.005 m² deemed included per Cl 4.5.1). Shoulder construction — finished work in position, m³. Pre-splitting rock excavation slopes — m² of specified presplit slope surface. Turfing with sods — finished work, m². Seeding and mulching — finished work, m². Sub-surface drain pipe — linear metres between installation extremes (jointing, hessian wrapping, upgrade-end plugging, outlet grating/screen, impervious clay covering all deemed included, not separate). Aggregate-drain backfill/aggregate — laid-in-position, m³. Trench-bed unsuitable-material removal+replacement — m³ of suitable material laid. **RCC pipe culverts** — running metres along centreline, inlet to outlet; concrete bedding per [[IS 1200 Part 2 - Concrete Works]].

## Cement concrete pavements (Cl 6)
Concrete strength described; measured in **m³ or m²**.
- Mechanical-spreader/vibratory-compaction-equipment concrete — measured separately, described as such.
- Reinforced (bar or fabric) concrete — stated + measured separately; reinforcement itself → [[IS 1200 Part 8 - Steelwork and Ironwork]].
- Formwork for pavings → [[IS 1200 Part 5 - Formwork]], measured separately.
- Special surface finishes — described, separate, m² (or included with the main item).
- Construction/dummy joints — running metres (if not in the main item), thickness+depth stated. Expansion joints — running metres, thickness+depth stated.
- Steel dowel bars (incl. ferrules) — described (length+diameter), enumerated.
- Steel plate reinforcement to joints — described (incl. metal sheath thickness/width, sheath gauge), running metres of joint.
- Tongued-and-grooved longitudinal butt joints — running metres.
