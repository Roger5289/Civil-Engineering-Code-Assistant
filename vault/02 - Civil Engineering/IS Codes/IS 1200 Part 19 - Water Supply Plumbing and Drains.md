# IS 1200 (Part XIX):1981 — Method of Measurement: Water Supply, Plumbing and Drains

Part of the [[IS 1200 - Method of Measurement Index|IS 1200]] series. General series rules in [[IS 1200 Part 1 - Earthwork]]. Source: `...\1200_19.pdf` (10 pages). Extracted text: `<local extracted text>\IS 1200\IS-1200-Part19-fulltext.txt`. This is the **in-building** fixtures/plumbing counterpart to [[IS 1200 Part 16 - Laying of Water and Sewer Lines]] (trunk mains/sewers).

## General rules for this Part (Cl 2)
Standard series items (clubbing, booking order, description content, dimensions to nearest 0.01 m, bills of quantities); work-measured-separately list matches series default minus snow, plus the same Cl 16-style **special-pumping-in-kilolitres** provision. Excavation for pipe trenches → [[IS 1200 Part 1 - Earthwork]].

## General pipe/fitting rules (Cl 3)
- **All pipes/fittings classified** by nominal diameter, material, quality, jointing method — **running metres**, unless otherwise specified; laying/jointing/fixing method fully described. Item includes all cutting/waste of pipes and thread-cutting where needed.
- Unequal-diameter fittings — designated by the **largest** diameter.
- Testing of water supply/drains/plumbing — included in the item description (not separate).
- **Lead-caulked joints** — enumerated separately.
- Pipes in ducts/chases/trenches, embedded in floor, or fixed to walls/ceilings with supports — each measured separately.
- Concrete beds/haunchings/coverings (incl. formwork) — running metres, pipe size + concrete dimensions/mix stated.
- Cutting through walls/floors + making good — included with the item, **except concealed pipework**, where chase-cutting + making good is measured separately, running metres.
- **Short lengths** (≤1 m, other than running lengths) — measured separately, running metres, described as "short length."

## Water supply (Cl 4)
Standard fittings (elbows, bends, tees, connectors, unions, diminishing sockets) — included with the pipes (not separate). Caps, collars, plugs, stopped ends — described+enumerated. Sluice valves/hydrants/stop-cocks/covers/surface boxes/water meters — described+enumerated, measured **separately by diameter**; jointing to pipe on either side described. Connection to the water main — described+enumerated.
- **Boilers/cisterns/cylinders** — enumerated, stating type/size/location/fixing method/working+test pressure/pipe-connection type+size; perforations for connections — enumerated.
- **Insulating coverings** (boilers/pipes) — m² for boilers/cylinders/tanks, **running metres stating diameter** for pipes; **no deduction for manholes/hand-holes/pipes passing through insulation**, nor extra for finishing around such openings. Insulation to bends/elbows/tees/valves — enumerated as extra-over the pipework insulation.

## Plumbing (Cl 5)
**Plumber's work** (locks, taps, valves, pillar-cocks, stop-cocks, ball valves, caps+linings, cleaning eyes with screw caps, ferrules, thimbles, unions, waste washers, perforated gratings, etc.) — described+enumerated, joints+fixing included.
- Bends/elbows/tees/branches/inspection-or-access doors/swan necks/enlarged sockets etc. for soil/waste/vent pipes — enumerated as extra-over.
- Wire guards + ventilating cowls over pipe tops — described+enumerated, measured separately **by pipe bore**.
- Stack clamps — described+enumerated, stay length + wall/roof fixing method stated.
- Brass pipes — classified by external diameter + metal thickness, jointing/fixing method described. Standard fittings — enumerated.
- Traps — described+enumerated, both-end joints included.
- **Fixtures** (a long enumerated list — captured as a class rather than transcribed item-by-item since it's simply "describe and enumerate"): water closets (washdown/squatting/siphonic types), wash basins, laboratory sinks, urinals (bowl/half-stall/squatting-plate types), partition slabs, siphonic toilets, foot rests, shower roses, squatting-pan traps, universal WCs, flushing cisterns (urinal and WC, automatic/siphonic types), brackets+supports, bath tubs, cast-iron drainage gratings, mixing valves, WC seats, self-closing taps, kitchen sinks, urinal water spreaders, half-round channels, wash-basin/sink waste fittings+plugs+accessories — **all enumerated, fully described, fixing included.**

## Drains (Cl 6)
Drain pipes — same running-metre + extra-over-fittings structure as [[IS 1200 Part 16 - Laying of Water and Sewer Lines]]'s sewer-line method (classify by diameter/quality/material/jointing; centreline measurement; fittings/specials enumerated separately as extra-over; alternative method classifies without diameter-inclusive fittings and measures between them instead).
- **Manholes/inspection chambers:** measured in detail per relevant Parts, or alternatively described+enumerated, **classified by the same depth bands** as Part 16 (≤0.5 m, 0.5–1, 1–2, 2–3 m, etc.; depth = cover-top to main-drain-invert distance).
