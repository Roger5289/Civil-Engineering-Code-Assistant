# IS 1200 (Part 26):1987 — Method of Measurement: Acid Resistant Lining

Part of the [[IS 1200 - Method of Measurement Index|IS 1200]] series. General series rules in [[IS 1200 Part 1 - Earthwork]]. Source: `...\1200_26.pdf` (6 pages). Extracted text: `<local extracted text>\IS 1200\IS-1200-Part26-fulltext.txt`. Niche industrial-building trade (acid-resistant tank/floor/drain linings); the standard itself notes it deliberately adopted "the simplest type of measurement" after surveying major organizations' practices, given no consistent prior convention existed.

## General rules for this Part (Cl 2)
Standard clubbing/booking/description rules; dimensions to nearest 0.01 m, areas to nearest 0.01 m² (no separate volume/weight precision — this trade is purely area-measured). Expansion/dummy joints + filler — included in the item description, not separate. Repairs described, old-surface prep included. Isolated areas ≤1 m² each — described, nature stated. Patterned/multi-colour work — described, nature stated. Curved/conical/spherical work — described separately, radius stated, its labour also measured separately.

## Method of measurement (Cl 3) — the core content
All finished-surface measurement, no separate substrate/backing item:
- **Floor lining** — finished-surface measurement.
- **Drain lining** — finished surface, **width = perimeter at the finished level.**
- **Tanks, with or without coping** — same method as drain lining (finished surface, width = perimeter at finished level) — both cases measured identically.
- **Rectangular pedestals/foundations** — finished surface, width = perimeter of the finished surface.
- **Circular pedestals/foundations** — finished surface, width = the finished circumference.
- **Conical portions** — superficial area of each side, based on finished-surface dimensions, **width = the curved portion's length along the curvature.**
- **Hemispherical areas** — finished-surface superficial area, width = the finished surface's perimeter.
