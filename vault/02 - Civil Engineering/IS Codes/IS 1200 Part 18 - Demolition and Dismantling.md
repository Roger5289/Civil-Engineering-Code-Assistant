# IS 1200 (Part XVIII):1974 — Method of Measurement: Demolition and Dismantling

Part of the [[IS 1200 - Method of Measurement Index|IS 1200]] series. General series rules in [[IS 1200 Part 1 - Earthwork]]. Source: `...\1200_18.pdf` (11 pages). Extracted text: `<local extracted text>\IS 1200\IS-1200-Part18-fulltext.txt`. Third revision added dismantling of masonry/concrete met during excavation.

**Definitions (Note to Cl 1):** **"Dismantling"** = carefully taking up/down and removing *without damage* — articles passed by hand and lowered (never thrown); nailed/screwed/bolted items taken out with proper tools, not torn/ripped off. **"Demolition"** = taking up/down or breaking up (no preservation requirement).

## General rules for this Part (Cl 2)
- **Measurement taken before demolition/dismantling** (except hidden work), no bulking allowance.
- Work-measured-separately list matches the series default (water/mud/foul/tides/snow).
- **Precautions (Cl 2.7):** necessary public/owner-property protection precautions must be flagged; **temporary shoring for portions not being pulled down (or adjoining property), plus temporary enclosures/partitions, are included in the main item** — not separate. Dust/nuisance-control precautions stated if required.
- **Demolition and dismantling works are always measured separately from each other.**
- **Lead:** description includes separating serviceable from unserviceable material, stacking within 100 m, and debris disposal. **Removal beyond 100 m measured separately**: 100 m–1 km in 100 m units; beyond 1 km in 500 m units. Shortest practicable route, not the actual route taken.
- Dry vs mortar-laid work measured separately, mortar type stated; framed vs unframed measured separately.
- Any necessary disconnecting/maintaining of services (temporary or permanent) flagged, with a making-good item provided if required.

## Walls and piers (Cl 3)
Walls, independent piers, columns + their footing/foundation (brick/stone/concrete) — described, **m³**; copings/corbels/cornices/other projections included with the wall measurement. Plaster thickness excluded when measuring wall thickness. Ashlar face stones/dressed stonework/precast concrete, if taken down **intact**, stated and measured separately, m³. Honeycomb/hollow-block walling — measured **as solid**. Cleaning bricks (incl. stacking, extra handling, rubbish disposal) — enumerated **in thousands of cleaned bricks**. Cleaning stone (from any demolished masonry, incl. ashlar facing/dressed stonework/slabs/flagging/precast blocks, incl. extra handling+disposal) — m³ of cleaned stone.

## Reinforced concrete and reinforced brickwork (Cl 4)
RCC structures and reinforced-brick roofs/walls (incl. footing/foundation) — m³; reinforcement-cutting stated if required. **Where reinforcement is to be separated/scraped/cleaned, that work is stated separately and measured in kilograms of salvaged steel.**

## Roofs, ceilings, floors (Cl 5–8)
- **Roof coverings** (incl. battens, boarding, mats, bamboo JAFFARI, subsidiary supports) — m², thickness+size/gauge stated. Ridges/hips/valleys — girthed, included with the roof area. Corrugated/semi-corrugated surfaces — flat, not girthed.
- Mud on roofs — m³. Lead sheets in roofs — **kg**, hips/valleys/flashings/gutter linings included in the weight.
- Supporting members (rafters, purlins, beams, joists, trusses) — wood: m³; steel/iron: kg. Span >10 m stated.
- **Ceilings:** stripping — m², described. Supporting joists/beams — m³ or kg per the roof-support rule above.
- **Concrete/brick roofs and floors** — m³, incl. beams/cantilevers/similar supports.
- **Floors/pavings** (except concrete pavings) — m³, fixing method described. **Concrete pavings — m², thickness stated.**

## Partitions, trellis, woodwork (Cl 9–10)
Partitions/light walls (lath+plaster, JAFFARI trellis, expanded metal, thin concrete/terracotta slabs, incl. framework) — m², thickness stated.
- **Woodwork:** ballies — running metres. Other woodwork <40 cm² section — running metres; ≥40 cm² section — m³. Boarding (incl. wooden CHAJJAS/sunshades with supports) — m², thickness stated.

## Steel/iron, doors/windows, posts, fencing, glazing (Cl 11–15)
- **Steel/iron work** — **kg**, weight from standard tables unless readily weighable. Riveted work with rivets to be cut — measured separately. Structural steel to be re-erected — measured separately. Framed steel gates: covering/filling material weight (iron sheets, expanded metal) added to the main article's weight unless ordered out separately.
- **Doors/windows/clerestory windows/ventilators** (wood or steel), whether removed during wall dismantling or via recesses in standing walls — enumerated; **>3 m² opening size measured separately.** CHOWKHAT/architrave/holdfast removal included in the item.
- **Posts or struts** (wood/steel/RCC, any section, incl. embedded-portion removal) — running metres.
- **Fencing wire mesh** (any type, with framework) — m²; if framework is to be separated and mesh rolled, stated as such.
- **Glazing:** taking out serviceable glass (except polished plate) from old sashes/skylights (any thickness/weight/size), incl. old-putty removal — m². Irregular/circular panes measured as rectangular/square.

## Services (Cl 16–18)
- **Water/sewer lines:** described by internal diameter+length, running metres inclusive of fittings, measured along the pipe centreline (fittings separated and enumerated if required). Pucca drains — m³, described. Valves/cisterns/fountain platforms/fire hydrants — enumerated. Manholes/inspection chambers — enumerated, size+depth stated, classified by depth band (same convention as [[IS 1200 Part 16 - Laying of Water and Sewer Lines]]: distance from cover top to main-drain invert). Ventilating shafts/gully traps/flushing cisterns/other appurtenant items — enumerated.
- **Oil/gas pipe lines** — measured the same way as water/sewer lines above.
- **Road surfaces:** different types measured separately. Road paving — m², described. Concrete paving — as per Cl 8.1 (m², thickness stated); reinforced concrete stated+measured separately. Soling/sub-base — m³, separately per material type.
