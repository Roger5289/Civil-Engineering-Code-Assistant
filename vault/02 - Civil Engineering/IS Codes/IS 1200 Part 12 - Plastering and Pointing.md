# IS 1200 (Part XII):1976 — Method of Measurement: Plastering and Pointing

Part of the [[IS 1200 - Method of Measurement Index|IS 1200]] series. General series rules in [[IS 1200 Part 1 - Earthwork]]. Source: `...\1200_12.pdf` (10 pages). Extracted text: `<local extracted text>\IS 1200\IS-1200-Part12-fulltext.txt`. This Part carries the **opening-deduction rule** referenced by Part 13 (painting) almost verbatim — read the two together.

## Plastering (Cl 3)
Classified by material, each measured separately, stating: mortar mix; coat count+thickness per coat; surface-treatment nature; base nature; curved/conical/spherical/elliptical work (radius stated); any special base treatment. **Arrises, internal rounded angles, external chamfers/rounded angles ≤80 mm girth are included in the description** (not separate). Fibrous plaster additionally states application method + joint treatment. Repairs — described, dubbing thickness stated if any.
- Roofs, ceilings, walls — each measured separately. Removing plaster (scraping etc.) — m², separate.
- **Isolated-width plastering** (bands, cornices, sunk panels) and **chamfers/rounded angles >80 mm girth**: ≤30 cm width/girth → running metres; >30 cm → m².
- **Height >10 m above ground/datum: measured separately in 5 m stages** — **except** interior plastering in buildings, which is instead measured separately **per storey**.
- Base unit: m² unless stated. Cutting to edges — running metres, separate (or included in the item, described).

### Deductions (Cl 3.8) — the core rule, shared near-verbatim with Part 13's painting deductions
For jambs/soffits/sills of openings ≤0.5 m² each, and for dissimilar-material ends (joists/beams/posts/girders/steps etc.) ≤0.5 m² each, and for openings **0.5–3 m²** each:
- **≤0.5 m² openings and dissimilar-material ends: NO deduction, and NO addition** for reveals/jambs/soffits/sills or for finish around the ends.
- **0.5–3 m² openings:** deduction made, **no addition** for reveals/jambs/soffits/sills, per this logic:
  1. Both wall faces plastered with the **same** plaster → deduct **one face only**.
  2. Both faces plastered with **different** plaster types, or one plastered + one pointed → deduct from the side with the **smaller reveal width**; no deduction on the other side. **Equal reveal widths on both sides → deduct 50% of the opening area from each face's plaster/pointing.**
  3. **Only one face plastered, other untreated** → full deduction from the plastered face if its reveal is narrower than the untreated side's; if reveals are equal or the plastered side's reveal is wider, **no deduction** at all.
  4. Door-frame width = wall thickness (or frame projects beyond it) → **full deduction from each** plastered face.
- **Openings >3 m²:** deduct on **each** face, but jambs/soffits/sills **are** measured (unlike the smaller bands). *(Rebated-reveal extra width always excluded from the opening-area calc, across all bands.)*

### Location-specific rules
Ceilings — measured between walls/partitions, pre-plaster dimensions, cornice/cove width deducted. Stair soffits — measured as ceiling plastering; raking soffits measured separately. Ribs/mouldings on ceilings — measured as cornices (Cl 3.4 rule), deducted from plastering if >15 cm width/girth. Wall plastering — length between walls/partitions (pre-plaster), height floor/skirting-top to ceiling, cornice/cove depth deducted; pilaster/projection sides added to the wall plaster; mouldings/architraves/ceiling-ribs/cornices on pilasters or around openings measured separately per Cl 3.4. Length always at the girth centreline; girth measured along the moulding curve. Moulded cornices/coves — m² (length × girth). Letters/figures in plaster — enumerated, height stated. Plastering on lathing — separate, coat count+thickness stated; wood vs steel lathing measured separately (→ Part 21 / Part 8); steel lathing states laps/gauge/mesh (no lap/cutting allowance); wood lathing states lath size/spacing/timber kind; connector lathing measured separately. Honeycomb-work plastering — m² overall, **no opening deduction**.

## Pointing (Cl 4)
Material proportions described. Each pointing type measured separately; pointing on different wall/floor/roof types measured separately; surface type+material stated. Single detached joints (flashings) — running metres. Brick/tile pointing with matching-shade mortar — measured separately. Base unit — m². Removing pointing (raking or otherwise) — m², separate.
- **Deductions (Cl 4.6):** identical structure to plastering's Cl 3.8 (same 0.5 m²/3 m² bands, same same-finish/different-finish/one-side-only/door-frame logic) — substitute "pointing" for "plaster" throughout.
- Raking-out joints — m² (or included in the item description). Single detached joint raking-out — running metres, separate.
- Honeycomb-work pointing — m² overall, no opening deduction.
