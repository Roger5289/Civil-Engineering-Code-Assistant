# IS 1200 (Part 8):1993 — Method of Measurement: Steelwork and Ironwork

Part of the [[IS 1200 - Method of Measurement Index|IS 1200]] series. General series rules in [[IS 1200 Part 1 - Earthwork]]. Source: `...\1200_8.pdf` (7 pages, incl. Amendment 1). Extracted text: `<local extracted text>\IS 1200\IS-1200-Part8-fulltext.txt`. Roof covering, water/sewer pipelines, and gas/oil pipelines are covered by Parts 9/16/20 respectively, not here. This is the Part referenced repeatedly by earlier Parts (2, 3, 6) for reinforcement/steel measurement.

## Series-rule variations for this Part
- Length/breadth measured to the nearest **0.001 m** (tighter than the series default 0.01 m) — **except reinforcement, which stays at 0.005 m**. Areas to nearest 0.001 m².
- Item description covers **both fabrication and erection** together (conveyance, handling, hoisting, finishing labour) — or, alternatively, fabrication and erection may each be described and measured as separate items.
- Cross-sections/thicknesses and unit masses are taken from the relevant IS standards for the section type, not measured directly. **Final mass per category, to the nearest 1 kg.**
- Priming coat is included in the fabrication item.
- **Riveted structures: unless stated otherwise, add 2.5% to the structure's mass for shop+site rivet heads.** **Welded structures: no allowance for weld metal.**
- Wedging-up under stanchion bases/steel grillages — described and enumerated.

## Steelwork classification (Cl 4.1)
Measured **separately by category**, each bolted/riveted/welded construction stated as such (suffix B/R/W in the BOQ): independent rolled sections (joist/channel/angle/tee, no connecting plates); rolled sections with connecting plates/angle cleats (main+cross beams, hip/jack rafters, purlins to common rafters); rolled joists in grillages (stiffener weight added to joist mass); compound girders; plate girders (type + overall height stated); lattice girders/aerial masts/tank staging (member details + overall height stated); single stanchions (rolled joists/channels with caps/bases/splices/brackets); compound stanchions (same, compound); trusses/trussed purlins (span + overall height stated); cladding/glazing framing; crane gantry rails+fastenings; staircases (stringers/treads/landings/handrails); plates (plain/chequered, cut/notched/holed, no attachments) vs the same **with** riveted/bolted/welded attachments (separate category); running rails/girders for sliding doors; platework (bunkers, chutes, chimneys+ladders, tanks incl. stays but excl. staging, gutters/downpipes, furnace shells); anchor/holding-down bolts+fittings+sag rods; wind ties, wooden-bridge strakes, handrail cores (straight), door/fencing running bars/posts/struts; framed work (grills, gratings, guard bars, ladders, walkways, railings, brackets); straps/hooks/clamps/holdfasts/wall ties/insets/knee pieces; ornamental work (grills, balustrades, curved/ramped/scroll handrail cores); door/hydraulic-gate/caisson-and-well-curb steelwork; purpose-made butt hinges for stone/concrete door-window frames.
- **Composite construction:** shear connectors count as structural steelwork.
- **Unit: mass**, unless stated otherwise.
- **Included in item mass (Cl 4.3):** cleats, brackets, packing pieces, bolts/nuts/washers, distance pieces, separators, diaphragms, gussets (overall rectangular dimensions), fish plates; riveted-work rivet-head allowance per Cl 3.8.
- **No deduction for rivet/bolt holes** (except anchor/holding-down bolt holes) **unless the hole exceeds 0.02 m²**; notches — deduct only if >0.05 m²; other openings (service pipe holes etc.) — deduct only if >0.1 m².

## By-category rules (Cl 5–13)
- **Tubular structures** — by weight.
- **Cables/guy wires** — running metres (diameter stated) or by weight.
- **Bearings** (roller/rocker/sliding) — fully described, enumerated.
- **Flue pipes** (steel sheeting) — overall running metres, incl. short lengths/cutting/waste; diameter, sheet thickness, black-or-galvanized (+ galvanization class per IS 1239 Part 1) stated; supports separate. Bends/elbows/cowls/tapered pipes/roof-plates-with-sleeve — enumerated as **extra over**.
- **Duct metalwork** — m² surface area; supports separate.
- **Expanded metalwork/weld meshwork** — m², described incl. laps/mesh/weight/strands/fixing method; **openings >0.2 m² deducted**; cutting/waste included.
- **Steel reinforcement (Cl 11 — the clause most other Parts point to):** running metres, mass per Cl 3.7.1; item includes rust removal, straightening, cutting to length, hooked ends, cranking/bending (straight or spiral); authorized overlaps and chairs/separators measured. **Welded joints:** butt-welded — by number; lap-welded — running metres of weld length. **Fabric reinforcement:** m², including meshes/strands, authorized laps measured. **Wire-netting encasement:** m², same lap rule. Raking/circular cutting+waste included. **Binding wire is never measured separately** — always deemed included in the reinforcement item.
- **Hoop iron** — fully described, running metres.

## Miscellaneous (Cl 12)
Bolts+nuts+washers (not already in Cl 4.1(t)) — kg. Plain/barbed wire fencing — running metres per line of wire. Patent plain wire fencing — m². Wire mattresses/nets — m², incl. tying method, laps. Collapsible gates — m² as fixed, stating opening size/pickets/pivoted-bar/mesh size when extended; runners/pulleys/locking lugs/handles + erection included. **Steel rolling shutters/grills** — m²; **width = outer distance between backs of the two guide channels; height = sill to hood-cover centre**; gauge/type/interlock-centre-distance/bridge depth stated; spring mechanism, jamb guides, bottom rail, locking described; wicket gate (if any) described; manufacturer's protective treatment (paint/hot-dip galvanizing) described. Steel doors/windows/ventilators/glazing frames — m² as fixed, IS-type stated, fixing/hanging/fastenings included. Compound-wall/railing gates — enumerated or m², hangings/guide rails/fastenings included. Steel louvres — m² of opening covered. Site-perforated pipes — kg (deduct perforations only if >0.02 m²); manufactured perforated pipe — running metres. Metal sheet wall cladding — m², gauge/type/connection method stated.

## Cast ironwork (Cl 13)
Flue/smoke pipes — overall running metres incl. short lengths/cutting/waste; jointing method, diameter, mass/standard-length stated. Bends/inspection-doors/cowls — enumerated as extra over. **Spiral staircases** — enumerated, stating overall diameter+height, total treads/risers/sleeves-in-one-piece, central shaft/poles incl. base plate+handrail+balusters; exit landings described+enumerated; stays (if any) stated. Stanchions/columns/lamp posts — by number, mass specified. Grid flooring/grills — m² overall area, size+mass/piece described. Everything else — by mass, classified: unmachined (brackets, frames, gully traps, manhole covers, gratings, fire/soot doors+frames, balls, stop-cock boxes) vs machine-turned (pulleys and similar).
