# IS 1200 (Part XX):1981 — Method of Measurement: Laying of Gas and Oil Pipe Lines

Part of the [[IS 1200 - Method of Measurement Index|IS 1200]] series. General series rules in [[IS 1200 Part 1 - Earthwork]]. Source: `...\1200_20.pdf` (8 pages). Extracted text: `<local extracted text>\IS 1200\IS-1200-Part20-fulltext.txt`. Near-identical structure to [[IS 1200 Part 16 - Laying of Water and Sewer Lines]] — this note flags what's genuinely gas/oil-specific rather than repeating the shared method.

## General rules for this Part (Cl 2)
Same as Part 16: standard clubbing/booking/description/dimension rules; work-measured-separately list (water/mud/foul/tides, no snow) plus special-pumping-in-kilolitres provision; measurement in below/above-datum stages.

## Method of measurement (Cl 3)
- **Pipes classified** by diameter, length-per-pipe, material, quality, jointing method — running metres inclusive of all joints, centreline measurement; **fittings/specials enumerated separately as extra-over**, cutting+jointing deemed included with the fitting item. (Alternative classify-without-fitting-inclusive method, same as Part 16, also available.)
- Pipe supports (hangers, chairs, pillars, etc.) — fully described, enumerated separately.
- Pipes in ducts/trenches/chases — described as such in each case. Pipes embedded in floor screed — described as such.
- Trench excavation/refilling → [[IS 1200 Part 1 - Earthwork]].
- Concrete beds/haunchings/coverings (incl. formwork) — running metres, pipe size + concrete dimensions/mix stated.

## Gas/oil-specific items (Cl 3.7–3.11) — the substance not shared with water/sewer lines
- **Heat treatment** — fully described, enumerated separately unless stated otherwise.
- **Tests:** hydrostatic and radiographic tests **described and measured separately** — hydrostatic in running metres; **radiographic per centimetre length, for each pipe size.**
- **Pigging, pickling, purging** (with inlet gases) — each separately enumerated.
- **Insulation:** all pipework insulation — fully described, running metres (unless stated otherwise), type+pipe size stated, measured along the centreline. **No separate measurement for bevelling insulation at ends/flanges.** Insulation around valves/ancillaries/fittings — fully described, enumerated separately as extra-over the pipework insulation.
- **Miscellaneous:** railway/culvert crossings, pavement cutting/reconditioning, pipeline/cable deviation, dismantling/reconditioning — measured per the relevant standard for that work. Connection to sub-mains/other pipelines — fully described, enumerated.
