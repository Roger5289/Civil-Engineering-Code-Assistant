# IS 1200 (Part 1):1992 — Method of Measurement: Earthwork

Part of the [[IS 1200 - Method of Measurement Index|IS 1200]] series (Method of Measurement of Building and Civil Engineering Works), built 2026-09-11 to feed Code Design Assistant's roadmap and BOQ/billing work. Source: `...\CED44-Methods of measurement of works of civil engineering\1200_1.pdf` (7 pages). Extracted text: `<local extracted text>\IS 1200\IS-1200-Part1-fulltext.txt`.

## General rules common to the whole IS 1200 series (Cl 2, this Part — read once, applies everywhere)
These same rules (near-verbatim) open every part of IS 1200. Stated here in full; every other part in this series just links back rather than repeating them.
- **Clubbing:** items may be clubbed if the clubbed items' break-up is agreed on the basis of this standard's detailed descriptions.
- **Booking of dimensions:** always in the sequence length → breadth/width → height/depth/thickness.
- **Measurement precision:** net, decimal system — dimensions to the nearest 0.01 m, areas to the nearest 0.01 m², volumes to the nearest 0.01 m³ (Part 2/concrete tightens slab thickness to the nearest 0.005 m).
- **Measured separately, always:** work in/under water, in/under foul situations, under tidal conditions (state high/low tide levels), in snow.
- **Not measured/paid separately** (deemed included in the main item): setting out, site clearance (grass/vegetation), unauthorized battering/benching/excavation, dead-men/tell-tales in borrow pits, temporary steps in excavation sides, excavation for planking/strutting insertion, removing slips/falls in excavation, ordinary bailing/pumping of rainwater.
- **Bills of quantities** must fully describe materials and workmanship and accurately represent the work.
- **Measured in stages**, stating height/depth, as below/above a stated ground or datum level.

## Classification of excavated material (Cl 3)
- **Soft/loose soil** — yields to ordinary pick/shovel/spade/rake (vegetable/organic soil, turf, gravel, sand, silt, loam, clay, peat).
- **Hard/dense soil** — needs close application of picks/jumpers/scarifiers (stiff clay, gravel, cobblestone, water-bound macadam, road soling). *Cobblestone = rounded/semi-rounded fragment, 80–300 mm max dimension.*
- **Mud** — soil/water mixture, fluid or weak-solid state.
- **Soft/disintegrated rock (no blasting)** — quarried/split with crowbars; includes laterite and hard conglomerate.
- **Hard rock (blasting required)** — any rock/boulder needing blasting to excavate. *Boulder = weathering-rounded fragment >300 mm max dimension.*
- **Hard rock, blasting prohibited** — otherwise blasting-grade rock, but blasting banned (noise/vibration/safety) — excavated by chiselling/wedging/other agreed method instead.

## Method of measurement (Cl 4)
- **Base unit: cubic metres**, measured from authorized dimensions, no allowance for bulking.
- **Uniform ground:** measure the cut trench/borrow pit directly; dead-men/tell-tales left at intervals to determine average depth.
- **Non-uniform ground:** take levels before start, after site clearance, and after completion; compute volume from the level differences.
- **Mixed strata (soft rock + hard rock, or soil + rock combinations):** measure the total by the trench/level method, then separate hard rock by stacking and measuring it in stack with a pre-agreed void-deduction percentage; the softer material is found by subtracting the hard-rock quantity from the total. Where three or four strata types are mixed, the same logic cascades — separate each category in turn (hard/dense soil via tell-tale cross-sections, then hard rock by stacking) working from the total down.
- **1.5 m staging:** all excavation measured in successive 1.5 m stages, stating the commencing level (not required where there's no lift, e.g. hillside cutting).
- **"Excavate and get out"** is the standard description — includes throwing spoil ≥1 m or 1/3 depth clear of the excavation edge (whichever is more); disposal of surplus beyond that is a separate item or a stated lead. A reduced berm (min 600 mm) is allowed only where disposal area is genuinely limited and the material/shoring can take the extra load.
- **Working-space allowance (Cl 4.2.5):** for excavation needing formwork, external damp-proofing, external protective masonry, post-tensioned ground-beam trenches, guniting, or outside-access work — measure the *lesser* of authorized working-space volume or actual excavated volume. Default authorized working space (if not otherwise specified): **600 mm from the face of the substructure** at its lowest level; **+1.5 m extra length at each end** for post-tensioned ground-beam trenches specifically.
- **Surface dressing** (trimming natural/excavated/filled ground, inequalities ≤150 mm) — m².
- **Rough excavation** (no dressing of sides/bottom needed — borrow pits, hillside cutting) — m³.
- **Surface excavation** (>1.5 m wide and >10 m² on plan, ≤300 mm deep) — m².
- **Excavation over areas** (>1.5 m wide, >10 m² on plan, >300 mm deep) — m³.
- **Trenches (foundations, pipes, cables) and shafts/wells/cesspits** (≤1.5 m wide / ≤10 m² on plan) — m³. For pipe/cable trenches, take the lesser of authorized-width or actual-excavated quantity. **Foundation trenches >1 m deep: add 50 mm/m depth per side** to the specified width.
- **Post holes** (≤0.5 m³ each) — enumerated; description deems to include return/fill/ram and spoil removal.
- **Return, fill and ram** (where not already in the excavation item) — m³; includes spreading in ≤200 mm layers, watering, ramming, levelling.
- **Embankments** — m³, includes slope formation; layer thickness and consolidation method must be stated; measured in 1.5 m stages. Special soils (e.g. marine clay) need a stated allowance for settlement under embankment load.
- **Filling under floors** — m³, ≤200 mm layers, watering, ramming, levelling.

## Lead and lift (Cl 5)
- **Lead** = shortest practicable route, not necessarily the route taken. ≤250 m measured in 50 m units. 250–500 m is one separate item. **Beyond 500 m: 500 m unit bands** (500–1000, 1000–1500, … up to 5 km). **Beyond 5 km: 1 km bands** (≥half a km rounds up to 1 km, <half a km is ignored). Loading/unloading is deemed included. Consolidated spoil heaps needing re-handling are stated and measured separately. For measurement purposes the excavated area is divided into blocks; lead = distance from block centre to the centre of that block's deposited earth.
- **Lift** = measured from ground level. Excavation to 1.5 m below ground, with spoil deposited on the ground, is included in the base earthwork item. **Extra lift beyond that: 1.5 m unit bands.** Only *obvious* lifts count (not lifts merely inherent to lead from ground slope) — except leads up to 250 m, where slope-inherent lift is still counted. Lift over a bank/obstruction = the level difference between the excavated material's centre of gravity and the top of the bank.

## Planking and strutting (Cl 6)
Use/waste of all necessary timber (wales, struts, open/close poling boards) plus fixing/removal is deemed included in the description. **Measured separately** for: trenches; areas (incl. raking shores' use/waste); shafts/wells/cesspits/manholes. Tightly-driven close-butt sheeting (running sand conditions) and any cavity packing behind it is its own separate item. Planking/strutting **left permanently in place** is measured separately from temporary work.

## Removing trees and hedges (Cl 7)
- Clearing shrubs/brushwood/small trees (girth ≤300 mm, measured 1 m above ground) — **m²**, includes removal/disposal.
- Cutting hedges, removing fences — **running metres**, includes removal/disposal.
- Trees 300 mm–1000 mm girth — **enumerated** as one item (per tree). Trees >1000 mm girth — enumerated **stating the girth**. Both include lopping branches and removal/disposal.
- Digging out roots (incl. stacking) — measured and **enumerated separately**.
