# IS 1200 (Part 13):1994 — Method of Measurement: White Washing, Colour Washing, Distempering and Painting of Building Surfaces

Part of the [[IS 1200 - Method of Measurement Index|IS 1200]] series. General series rules in [[IS 1200 Part 1 - Earthwork]]. Source: `...\1200_13.pdf` (6 pages, incl. Amendment 1). Extracted text: `<local extracted text>\IS 1200\IS-1200-Part13-fulltext.txt`.

## General rules (Cl 2)
Decorative treatment fully described, stating **number of coats**. Preparatory work (brooming, steel-wire brushing, scraping, washing, rubbing down) — on new surfaces (incl. primer coats) or on old treated surfaces — always **included in the main item**, not billed separately.
- **Classified and measured separately** by treatment type: whitewash/colour wash; non-washable distemper; washable distemper; waterproof paint (coloured/colourless); chalk whiting to cloth/hessian; linseed oil+cement to steel/iron; cement slurry wash.
- Priming/alkali-neutralizing treatments, surface scraping, smoke-soot washing, oil/grease removal, disinfection treatment (efflorescence, mould, moss, fungi, algae, lichen) — each measured **separately**, materials described.
- Walls, ceilings, sloping roofs — measured separately. Old treated surfaces — measured separately, described as such.

## Measurement (Cl 3)
- **Base unit: m²**, net, dimensions to nearest 0.01 m, areas to nearest 0.01 m².
- **Deductions (Cl 3.2)** — **the same structure as [[IS 1200 Part 12 - Plastering and Pointing]]'s Cl 3.8/4.6**, with one addition: for openings ≤0.5 m² and dissimilar-material ends ≤0.5 m², no deduction/addition; 0.5–3 m² openings follow the same-finish/different-finish/one-side-treated logic (deduct from the narrower-reveal side, 50% split if equal); door-frame-equals-wall-thickness → full deduction each face; **plus a fifth case unique to this Part: reveal present on only one side → full deduction on the no-reveal face, 50% deduction on the reveal face.** Openings >3 m² — deduct each face, but jambs/soffits/reveals **are** measured.
- **No deduction for attachments** (casings, conduits, pipes, electric wiring, etc.).
- **Corrugated surfaces measured flat as fixed, not girthed** — but the measured quantity is then **increased** by a stated percentage and folded into the general area:
  - Corrugated steel sheets: **+14%**
  - Corrugated asbestos-cement sheets: **+20%**
  - Semi-corrugated AC sheets: **+10%**
  - Nainital-pattern roofs (plain sheeting with rolls): **+10%**
  - Nainital-pattern roofs with corrugated sheets: **+25%**
- Cornices/wall features not picked out in a different finish — girthed and folded into the general area (not billed separately).
- Painting kept as its own separate category from other finishes; state whether measured flat or girthed. **Alternatively**, different surfaces may be grouped into one general item by converting uneven-surface areas to equivalent plain area via these multipliers:
  - External plain brickwork, recessed/raised/weather-struck pointing: **+20%**
  - Sand-face plaster, grain ≤4 mm: **+50%**
  - Rough-cast plaster, stone aggregate ≤10 mm: **+100%**
  - Pebble-dash finish, aggregate >10 mm: **+275%**
  - Sponge-finished plaster: **+25%**
- **RCC JALLIES:** area increased by: one side painted → **+100%**; one side + through-thickness → **+150%**; both sides + through-thickness → **+200%**.
