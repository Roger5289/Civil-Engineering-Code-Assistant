# IS 1200 (Part X):1973 — Method of Measurement: Ceiling and Linings

Part of the [[IS 1200 - Method of Measurement Index|IS 1200]] series. General series rules in [[IS 1200 Part 1 - Earthwork]]. Source: `...\1200_10.pdf` (7 pages, incl. Amendments 1 & 2). Extracted text: `<local extracted text>\IS 1200\IS-1200-Part10-fulltext.txt`. Short and largely standard — false ceilings, panelling, board linings.

## Measurement (Cl 3)
- Material, thickness, fixing method described. **Supporting members measured separately** under the relevant Part (steel → Part 8, wood → Part 21) unless stated otherwise.
- Work to a specific pattern is stated as such. **Circular-surface work** measured separately (screw-fixing stated if applicable); all straight/raking cutting+waste included with the item.
- **Base unit: flat, m²**, unless otherwise described.
- **No deduction for openings ≤0.4 m²** (no extra measurement for forming them either). **Openings >0.4 m²** — full deduction, with any opening-formation labour measured separately.
- Boarding fixed to curved surfaces ≤15 cm width — measured separately, includes shooting edges to proper splays.
- Circular cutting+waste — running metres, thickness+material type stated.
- Cover fillets over joints — running metres, separately, material/width/thickness stated (chamfered/rounded/moulded edges stated); mitring at junctions included.
- Sealing joints of plaster/fibre boards — running metres, sealing method described.
- Chamfering/rounding fibre-board edges, cutting V-grooves — running metres.
- **Insulation boards/slabs** fixed to surfaces — m², type/layer-count/layer-thickness/fixing method stated. **Hollow insulation blocks** — m², thickness specified, setting method + mortar type described.
