# IS 1200 (Part XXV):1971 — Method of Measurement: Tunneling

Part of the [[IS 1200 - Method of Measurement Index|IS 1200]] series. General series rules in [[IS 1200 Part 1 - Earthwork]]. Source: `...\1200_25.pdf` (9 pages, incl. Amendments 1–4, latest Jan 1996, reflected in the current text). Extracted text: `<local extracted text>\IS 1200\IS-1200-Part25-fulltext.txt`.

## General rules for this Part (Cl 2)
- **Weight measured to the nearest 0.1 kg** (finer than the series-default 1 kg).
- Work-measured-separately: **in/under water; in compressed air** (a much shorter list than most Parts — no mud/foul/tide/snow categories here).
- Springs likely to be encountered — measured against a separate specific provision (Cl 2.5).
- **Pumping** (incl. bore-well/well-point dewatering) — measured separately for all stages, in **kW-hours or hp-hours**. **Pneumatic pumping** — measured by the compressor's rated capacity, **m³ of air delivered per hour**.

## Excavation (Cl 3)
- Classified: (a) hard rock not requiring supports; (b) all soil/soft-or-hard-rock classes requiring temporary or permanent supports (per [[IS 1200 Part 1 - Earthwork]]'s soil classification) — **supports measured separately (Cl 4)**.
- Item deemed to include: drilling, blasting, ventilation, lighting, scaling, hauling+depositing excavated material in the earmarked area, final surface clearing.
- **Quantity = the tunnel's solid volume within the hypothetical "B" line (pay line)** set by the engineer-in-charge, **regardless of whether actual excavation falls inside or outside that line** — subject to excavation reaching at least the minimum "A" line. *A/B lines defined per IS 4880 (Part 2):1976 (tunnel geometric design).*
- **If the minimum excavation line is later increased** (requiring re-enlargement of an already-excavated tunnel) — measured separately, as the volume **between the original B line and the revised B line.**
- Soft/disintegrated rock layers bedded with hard rock, or seams/faults, excavated beyond the pay line by hand/pneumatic/other implements **without continuous systematic blasting** — measured separately.
- Cement consumed for guniting/shotcreting (weak-rock protection) — measured separately, **by weight.**

## Supports (Cl 4)
- **Temporary supports:** item includes furnishing, installing, maintaining, removing (+ connected materials/labour/equipment). **Measured by area supported** (= peripheral length × the tunnel's linear dimension).
- **Permanent supports:** item = furnishing+installing the supports complete with all bolts/nuts/butt plates/feather plates/dowels/wedges/tie rods/spikes/drift pins/temporary timber spreaders/concrete pedestals (if any), logging, blocking, back-packing with excavated material. **All structural steel (riveted, welded, nuts, bolts) measured in kilograms.**
- **Grouting behind supports + concrete lining** — measured separately, by **weight of cement consumed**, material stated.
- **Precast concrete blocks as lagging** (forming part of the concrete lining) — measured separately (→ [[IS 1200 Part 2 - Concrete Works]]); their volume is **deducted** from the concrete-lining volume if placed within the B line.
- **Space between the B line and precast-concrete/structural-steel lining, filled by concrete** — measured separately, **by weight of cement consumed**, split into: (a) manually-placed concrete (ledge beams, curbs, etc.); (b) mechanically-placed concrete (pumps, placers, etc.).
- **Rock bolts** — by weight, including wedges/nuts/butt plates.
- Drainage pipes in lining — measured separately, running metres.

## Concrete lining (Cl 5)
Measured as the quantity of concrete placed **between the formwork (or the steel shell's outside surface, if used) and the pay line** (the B line/pay line adopted for that location's excavation). **Item includes formwork.** No deduction for reinforcement volume, **but the volume of permanent steel supports (Cl 4.2), where provided, IS deducted** from the total lining volume.

## Pressure grouting over concrete lining (Cl 6)
- **Grout holes** (pack or pressure grouting, through concrete and rock) — running metres. Holes drilled through plate steel liners — measured **in numbers**, separately.
- **Grout pipes/fittings** — kilograms, by actual weighment or known weight×length.
- **Water pressure testing** — separately, per hole, enumerated.
- **Grouting quantity** — by weight of cement actually forced into the holes. Stone dust/other additions — measured loose/dry before mixing, by **volume**, approved size+design.
