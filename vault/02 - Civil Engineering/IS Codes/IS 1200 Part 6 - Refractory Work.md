# IS 1200 (Part VI):1974 — Method of Measurement: Refractory Work

Part of the [[IS 1200 - Method of Measurement Index|IS 1200]] series. General series rules in [[IS 1200 Part 1 - Earthwork]]. Source: `...\1200_6.pdf` (8 pages, incl. Amendments 1 & 2). Extracted text: `<local extracted text>\IS 1200\IS-1200-Part6-fulltext.txt`. Niche — furnace/kiln/boiler linings, not typical building BOQ work, but included for completeness per the locked full-series scope.

## Series-rule variations for this Part
- Dimensions to nearest 0.01 m, areas to nearest 0.01 m², volumes to nearest 0.01 m³ **(same as the series default) plus weight to the nearest 0.001 tonne** — the one Part so far adding a weight-precision rule.
- **Work to be measured separately:** here it's specifically hot-condition refractory work, with the temperature range stated — not the water/mud/tide/snow list from Part 1.
- Description deemed to include: conveyance/delivery/handling/unloading/storing/waste/pack-return, scaffolding/platforms/walkways/tools/tackle, item-wise stacking, unpacking, wood/straw disposal, plus (refractory-specific) safety equipment, lighting and ventilation at the work location.

## Method of measurement (Cl 3)
- **Deemed included** in the item: dressing bricks/blocks (cutting/grinding/chipping for joint thickness and alignment); dressing for expansion/sliding/binding joints (curvature, keying in arches/curved surfaces); forming expansion/sliding joints (excluding their filling — separate, see below); finishing/pointing/cleaning of masonry joints/gaps/hollows/cavities/openings ≤0.1 m² each.
- Refractory/insulation brick & block types, mortars, powders fully described; auxiliary/filling materials (paper, cardboard, asbestos, mineral wool, water glass, coke pitch, carbon mass, special sands, crumbs, admixtures, plasticizers) also described.
- **Base unit: cubic metres**, inclusive of mortar/expansion/sliding joints. **Voids/openings deducted only if each exceeds 0.01 m²** (tighter threshold than the concrete/brick/stone Parts' 0.1 m²/40 cm² rules — refractory linings are billed with much less tolerance for small gaps).
- Same volumetric basis (Cl 3.3) applies to castable refractory/refractory concreting, rammed-mass filling, and loose-insulation filling (mica crumbs, slag wool, asbestos powder, fireclay mass, carbon mass).
- **Insulating separation layer** (asbestos/cardboard etc. between brick lining and shell/wall) — described and measured **separately, m²**.
- **Insulation plaster** over a refractory surface — described and measured **separately, m²**.
- **Refractory grout work** — m³, on the basis of theoretical grouted volume.
- **Filling of expansion/sliding joints** (paper, cardboard, etc.) — described and measured separately, running metres.
- **Anchors, hangers, supporting steel** for refractory brickwork — measured separately under Part VIII (steel/iron work, not yet built in this vault).
- **Painting finished masonry** (cement/fireclay mortar, water glass, etc.) — measured separately under Part XIII (plastering/pointing → [[IS 1200 Part 12 - Plastering and Pointing]]).
