# IS 1200 (Part XXI):1973 — Method of Measurement: Woodwork and Joinery

Part of the [[IS 1200 - Method of Measurement Index|IS 1200]] series. General series rules in [[IS 1200 Part 1 - Earthwork]]. Source: `...\1200_21.pdf` (11 pages). Extracted text: `<local extracted text>\IS 1200\IS-1200-Part21-fulltext.txt`. Referenced by nearly every other Part (brickwork nogging, plaster lathing, stone masonry timber framing, roof trellis, refractory brickwork) for its own timber-framing content.

## Series-rule variations for this Part
- Length/width to nearest 0.01 m; **width of single/detached planks and thickness to nearest 2 mm.** **Scantlings/battens/baulks: nearest 2 mm in each cross-sectional dimension.** *(Scantling = sawn timber, cross-section 5–20 cm both directions. Batten = sawn timber, cross-section ≤5 cm either direction. Baulk = sawn timber, cross-section >5 cm one direction and >20 cm the other.)* Cubical contents to nearest 0.001 m³ (finer than the series-default 0.01 m³).

## General (Cl 3)
- Wood type/description stated; each kind of wood measured separately.
- **"Fixed" vs "framed and fixed" measured separately** — framed = involves mortice-and-tenon or dovetail joints.
- All work includes nails. **"Secret fixed," "fixed with screws," "fixed with screws and cups," "fixed with bolts and washers"** each described and measured separately; non-ordinary-steel screws stated; screws for fixing hardware are measured *with* the hardware, not here.
- **Net-as-fixed measurement** (no extra for shape/joints) **except**: (1) non-rectangular scantlings/battens measured as the smallest enclosing rectangle; (2) varying-section scantlings/battens measured at their **largest** section; (3) mitred pieces measured along their **longest** length; (4) circular/segmental portions measured net, separately.
- Keys, wedges, dowels, wood/bamboo pins to tenoned joints, and clenching of nail heads — deemed included in the item description.
- **Extra framed-timber length for tenons/wedges** added to the site length of a framed member; extra length for embedding in walls/floors also added to site length.
- **Default tolerance: 1.5 mm per wrought (planed) face**, unless specifically stated otherwise.
- Plain woodwork items >20 cm width, if required in one width, described and measured separately.
- All lineal labours (rebates straight/splayed, beads, chamfers) — described, included with the item.
- Plugging to walls for planks etc. — running metres per row, plug spacing stated. Plugging for fittings/detached work — described, enumerated.
- **Wrought (= planed) timber** — described, measured separately, unless planing is measured separately. **Planing** — m² for all wrought surfaces, unless the timber is already described as wrought.
- **Scribing, notching >15 cm girth, circular cutting** — measured separately: m² if the timber is cubic-measured, running metres (thickness stated) if the timber is m²-measured.
- **Rebates/tongues/grooves; beads/staff-beads/flutes; mouldings** — measured separately, running metres, width/girth stated, all mitres+stops included, unless already in the main item.
- **By number:** splayed/rounded/wrought ends; wrought+pointed/rounded post ends (incl. mitred intersections); splayed/moulded rafter-foot projections; notches ≤15 cm girth (thickness stated); bored holes other than for bolts (diameter+depth stated); square-section cut holes (size+depth stated).

## Types of work (Cl 4)
- **Doors and windows (leaves):** described, fixing method stated, **m²**; each type measured separately. Composite door/window combinations: clubbed item, or split portions measured separately at the common rail's centreline. **No extra width/labour for rebated/splayed meeting stiles.**
- **Boarding** — m², thickness stated, by category: roof boarding; ceiling; floors; panelling; weather boarding; shelves/fittings/racks/bins/almirah linings. Straight+raking cutting/waste and heading joints included. Side joints described, different-joint-kind work measured separately. Rebated/tongued-and-grooved/grooved-and-filleted/secret-jointed boarding+weather boarding measured net as fixed (rebate/tongue extra width ignored). Boarding to curved surfaces in narrow widths — measured separately, incl. shooting joints to proper splay. Boarded floors state board width, jointing+nailing method, traversing/polish-finishing if applicable. Weather boarding states width/thickness/lap (feather-edged boarding's thickness = maximum thickness); edge chamfering/rebating described.
- **Batten work** — running metres.
- **Trellis work** — m², lath size+spacing stated. One-way vs two-way measured separately. Supporting posts/rails/stiles/braces (incl. for doors/windows in trellis) measured separately. Doors/windows formed in trellis work measured along with the trellis.
- **Shoring and strutting** — m³ (use+waste), incl. necessary bolts/wedges/dog-spikes/nails, erecting, maintaining in position, striking, removal.
- **Bally work** — running metres, mean diameter (average of end diameters) stated.

## Staircases (Cl 4.7) — the most detailed clause in this Part
Measured under a separate heading, in full detail per the sub-clauses below — **or**, where detailed working drawings exist, as a single omnibus item fully described, stating tread area in m².
- **Landings** (incl. bearers) — measured as boarded floors.
- **Treads and risers** — m², area = tread length × (exposed tread width + rise step-to-step). Winders+riser included with the item.
- Trimming of treads/risers/flyers/winders — included in the description, along with cross-tonguing/cross-grooving/cross-rebating/framing/gluing/wedging/blocking labour.
- **Carriages** (if provided) — measured separately.
- Returned tread ends with shaped brackets under a rounded quadrant, or curtail ends to treads/risers — enumerated.
- **Wall strings** — running metres, width+thickness stated, incl. wall plugging + tread/riser housing.
- **Outer strings** — measured as wall strings, incl. housing for treads/risers, plus **mitring/notching at newel posts, landings, etc.**
- **Cut strings** — as outer strings, incl. cutting of the upper edge for treads.
- **Ramped, circular, wreathed portions** — measured separately, described as such.
- **Handrails** — running metres along the top centreline, extreme section of straight portions stated, rounded-or-moulded stated. Circular/level/ramped/wreathed handrails measured separately; quadrants, short ramps, wreaths, scroll ends — enumerated.
- Mitres, housing joints, handrail screws/dowels at the junction of circular-to-straight sections — enumerated.
- **Balusters** — described+enumerated, size stated, incl. end framing/housing.
- **Newels** — running metres, section stated; turning length stated if turned.
- Sunk panels/mouldings planted-on or housed-in to newels — stated.
- **Newel caps and pendants** — described+enumerated.

## Miscellaneous types (Cl 4.8–4.13)
Cased frames of vertical sliding windows — running metres along the outer edge. Skirtings/cornices/picture rails/dado rails and similar mouldings — described (incl. fixing method), running metres. Trap doors — described+enumerated as extra-over the relevant item. Pelmet boxes — running metres along the sides+face planking. **Turned work** — running metres, girth stated. **Sundries** (wardrobes/cupboards, draining boards, plate racks, curtain brackets, towel rails, toilet fixtures, cistern back boards, small fittings like door/window stops) — each fully described and enumerated.
