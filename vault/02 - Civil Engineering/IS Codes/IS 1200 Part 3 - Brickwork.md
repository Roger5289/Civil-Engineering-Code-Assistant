# IS 1200 (Part III):1976 — Method of Measurement: Brickwork

Part of the [[IS 1200 - Method of Measurement Index|IS 1200]] series. General series rules in [[IS 1200 Part 1 - Earthwork]]. Source: `...\1200_3.pdf` (13 pages). Extracted text: `<local extracted text>\IS 1200\IS-1200-Part3-fulltext.txt`. Refractory brickwork is covered separately by [[IS 1200 Part 6 - Refractory Work]], not here.

## General brickwork (Cl 3)
Bricks/mortar fully described; a bond other than English bond must be stated.
- **Deemed included** in the general item: raking out joints for later plastering/pointing (or flush-finishing as work proceeds); preparing existing wall tops for raising; rough cutting/waste (gables, arch cores, eaves splays, general body cutting); plumbing to angles; forming reveals to jambs (where no fair-cut face is involved); holes for pipes; building in holdfasts/air bricks/fixing bricks; building in beam/joist/slab/lintel/sill/truss ends; forming openings/flues (no deduction made, see below); bedding wall plates/lintels/sills/roof tiles/sheets; **chases ≤50 cm girth**.
- **Included with general brickwork** (not separate items): footings; battered brickwork (measured net; battered *surfaces* separately in m² as extra-over); eaves/beam fillings (no joist/rafter deduction); brickwork in chimney breasts/stacks/smoke-or-air-flues (except independent boiler-house chimney shafts); pilasters, plain copings, sills.

## Measurement (Cl 4)
- **Base unit: cubic metres.** Walls ≤1 brick thick measured **separately in m²**, stating thickness.
- **Walls >1 to ≤3 bricks thick:** measured in **half-brick multiples** (inclusive of mortar joints). Fractional half-bricks: ≤¼ brick actual measurement; >¼ brick rounds up to a full half-brick.
- **Walls >3 bricks thick:** actual thickness measured.
- **No deduction/addition for:** dissimilar-material ends ≤0.1 m² section; openings ≤0.1 m² (lintel/sill included in opening height calc per the note, but lintel *end bearing* and rebated-reveal extra width excluded); wall plates/bed plates/slab-or-CHAJJA bearings where thickness ≤10 cm and bearing doesn't span the full wall thickness; cement concrete blocks for holdfasts/holding-down bolts; iron fixtures (wall ties, pipes ≤300 mm dia, door/window holdfasts); **chases ≤50 cm girth**.
- **Fireplaces/flues:** flues ≤0.2 m² sectional area measured solid (no extra for pargetting/coring). Flues >0.2 m²: deduct the void, measure pargetting/coring in running metres stating flue size. Fireplace apertures not deducted; no extra labour for jamb splaying/throating.
- **Pillars/columns:** m³, fully described; classified rectangular/polygonal on plan, curved on plan (any radius), or other. *Rectangular pillar = a detached masonry portion where breadth ≤3× thickness and thickness itself ≤3 brick lengths.*

## Special brickwork categories (Cl 5–22)
- **Circular brickwork:** mean radius ≤6 m — measured separately, cutting/waste/templates included. >6 m — measured separately but *included with* general brickwork.
- **Backing to masonry** — separate, average thickness stated, cutting/waste for bonding included.
- **Honeycomb walling** — m², thickness + pattern stated; honeycomb openings not deducted.
- **Independent chimney shafts** (large steam boilers) — net inclusive of cutting/waste/templates; classified rectangular/polygonal/curved on plan; height from ground/datum stated.
- **Cavity walls** — same rules as concrete cavity walls (Part 2): cavity in m² stating width, measured at cavity centre-plane with opening/solid deductions; jamb/sill/head closing separate, running metres; core/weep/vent-hole provision described.
- **Reinforced brickwork** — measured and kept separate from general brickwork; reinforcement measured separately (→ Part VIII).
- **Brick nogging:** >1 brick thick → included with general brickwork; ≤1 brick thick → per the m² wall rule above, dimensions overall. Timber framing measured separately (→ Part XXI).
- **Fair-face/architectural-appearance brickwork** — measured separately.
- **Arches/vaults/staircases** — measured separately; selected-uncut vs purpose-made/fair-cut-rubbed brick distinguished. Centering ≤2 m span included; >2 m span, centering separate (→ Part V). Skew cutting included.
- **Underpinning** — measured separately; wedging up on top of underpinning (extra labour+material) — m² = length × top-course width.
- **Fair cutting:** >10 cm width/girth (splayed angles, weatherings, cornices, quoins where purpose-made brick isn't used) — m², separate. ≤10 cm width/girth (splays, chamfers) — running metres, width/girth stated. Circular fair cutting — m², separate.
- **Brick edgings** (roads etc.) — running metres.
- **Filleting** (mortar, e.g. roof flashings) — running metres, shape+area stated.
- **Broken-glass coping** — m², mortar thickness + glass weight/m² stated.
- **DPC** — m², thickness stated; levelling-up/prep and formwork use/waste included; vertical and horizontal DPC measured separately.
- **Encasing brickwork to steel joists/stanchions** — m³; joist volume not deducted (boxed portion only, for boxed stanchions/girders); extra cutting/fitting labour around the steel — separate, m² of finished surface.
- **Sills/cornices/projections** (corbels, string courses, aprons, friezes, drip/oversailing courses) of splayed/bullnosed/purpose-made brick — running metres, depth+width stated; no deduction from wall masonry for the bearing portion.
- **Brick tile work** — measured separately, ordinary-brickwork rules apply.
- **Chases/rebates/throatings/grooves:** running metres stating girth, classified ≤10 cm and 10–20 cm girth; >20 cm girth → m² (girth × length).
- **Cutting holes:** per cm of cutting depth, classified ≤400 cm² and 400 cm²–0.1 m².
- **Cutting openings** >0.1 m²: walls ≤1 brick thick → m²; walls >1 brick thick → m³.
- **Toothing and bonding** (new-to-existing) — m² of vertical contact face, new work only.
