# IS 1200 (Part V):1982 — Method of Measurement: Formwork

Part of the [[IS 1200 - Method of Measurement Index|IS 1200]] series. General series rules in [[IS 1200 Part 1 - Earthwork]]. Source: `...\1200_5.pdf` (10 pages, incl. Amendment 1). Extracted text: `<local extracted text>\IS 1200\IS-1200-Part5-fulltext.txt`. Covers formwork *where required to be measured separately* — referenced by [[IS 1200 Part 2 - Concrete Works]], Part 3 (arches >2 m span), Part 4 (arches >2 m span).

## What's deemed included in a formwork item (Cl 3)
Splayed edges, notchings, overlap/passing allowances at angles, sheathing battens, strutting, bolting, nailing, wedging, easing, striking, removal; all supports/struts/braces/wedges/mud-sills/piles needed to support the formwork; bolts/wire-ties/clamps/spreaders/nails holding sheathing together; working scaffolds/ladders/gangways; filleting for stop-chamfered/splayed external angles ≤20 mm wide (beams/columns etc.); temporary pour/vibrator/cleaning openings; oil dressing to prevent adhesion; raking or circular cutting.

## Type of formwork — separate items by contact-surface type (Cl 4)
Wrought (planed/sawn timber sheathing); tongued-and-grooved boards; plywood lining; special lining for extra-smooth/textured/decorative architectural finish; steel sheeting/tubing; slip-form extrusion technique.

## Classification, measured separately by category (Cl 5)
Foundations/footings/column bases/mass concrete; flat surfaces (soffits of floors/roofs/landings — **>200 mm floor thickness measured separately, stating thickness**); vertical surfaces (walls/partitions incl. pilasters/buttresses/plinth-string courses); sloping/battering surfaces incl. folded plates — split at **30° from horizontal** (≤30°: underside shuttering only; >30°: both sides may be shuttered but only underside area is measured either way); arches (≤6 m span / >6 m span); cylindrical shells, underside area only (radius <3 m / >3 m); waffle/ribbed slabs (bottom inclined surface); domed vaults/shell roofs with curved-both-directions surfaces (underside area only); beam sides+soffits/haunchings/cantilevers/girders/bressumers/lintels (**beams/girders ≥1 m deep measured separately**); column/pier/pillar/post/stanchion/strut sides (square/rectangular/polygonal/circular/curved each separate); slab edges and floor/wall breaks (running metres if <200 mm width/thickness); cornices/mouldings; small surfaces (cantilever ends, brackets, step ends, pilaster/column caps+bases); CHAJJAS/chullah hoods/weather shades/corbels (incl. edges); staircases with sloping/stepped soffits (risers+stringers incl., landing excl.); spiral staircases; chimneys/shafts; elevated water reservoirs; well steining; fins.

## Measurement (Cl 6)
- **Unit: m²**, actual surface in contact with the concrete/material. Small features (per 5(p) above) — enumerated instead. Formwork left permanently in place — stated as such.
- Lining formwork with wallboard/hardboard/polyethylene/paper, or coating with mould liquid/limewhite — described and measured separately.
- **Permanent lining** (wallboard/asbestos/cork slab left in) — measured separately; description includes fixing to the concrete.
- **No deduction for openings ≤0.4 m².** No deduction at all for openings/cutouts with slip-form technique.
- Raking/circular cutting and rounded/moulded edges — running metres. Moulded stoppings — enumerated.
- **Secondary-beam formwork** measured up to the sides of the main beam, with **no deduction** from the main beam's formwork at the intersection. Same logic for beam-to-column: beam formwork measured to column sides, no deduction from column/stanchion-casing formwork at the intersection.
