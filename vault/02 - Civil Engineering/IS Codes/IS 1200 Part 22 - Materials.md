# IS 1200 (Part XXII):1982 — Method of Measurement: Materials

Part of the [[IS 1200 - Method of Measurement Index|IS 1200]] series. General series rules in [[IS 1200 Part 1 - Earthwork]]. Source: `...\1200_22.pdf` (9 pages). Extracted text: `<local extracted text>\IS 1200\IS-1200-Part22-fulltext.txt`. Referenced by [[IS 1200 Part 17 - Road Work Including Airfield Pavements]] for road-material supply measurement. Covers *supply* of raw/manufactured materials (sand, boulders, aggregates, steel, timber, sanitary fittings, etc.) — how the bulk quantity is measured, not how it's installed.

## General rules for this Part (Cl 2)
Dimensions to nearest 0.01 m, area to nearest 0.01 m², volume to nearest 0.01 m³, **weight to nearest 1 kg**.
