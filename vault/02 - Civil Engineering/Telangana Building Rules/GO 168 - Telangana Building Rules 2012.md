# G.O.Ms.No.168 — Telangana Building Rules, 2012

Structured ruleset for the Code Design Assistant, covering the base building bye-law order governing construction across Telangana (and originally all of undivided Andhra Pradesh). Built the same way as [[Fire Prevention and Protection Ruleset - Part F]]: full read, page-anchored, dense tables verified against rendered page images, ambiguities flagged rather than resolved.

**Source:** `<your codes folder>\GOMsNo168.pdf` (373 pages) + extracted text at `<local extracted text>\GO 168\GOMsNo168-fulltext.txt` (page-anchored, `===PDFPAGE N===` markers = real PDF page numbers).

**Identity:** "The Andhra Pradesh Building Rules, 2012," issued by the Municipal Administration & Urban Development (MA&UD) Department, **G.O.Ms.No.168, dated 07.04.2012**, in supersession of 19 earlier GOs (1998–2011, all now moot — listed on p.1 but not needed as sources). Issued under undivided Andhra Pradesh, before the 2014 Telangana bifurcation; Telangana inherited it as governing law and has amended it several times since. Applies to HMDA, all Urban Development Authorities, all Municipal Corporations, all Municipalities, all Nagar Panchayats, Gram Panchayat areas under Master Plans/GTP Schemes, and IALA/SEZ areas (Rule 1(b)) — extended to all Gram Panchayat areas generally by G.O.Ms.No.52/2022 (see gap list below).

## B. Applicability [Rule 1; p.2]

- Rules apply to building activity in: HMDA, all UDAs, all Municipal Corporations, all Municipalities, all Nagar Panchayats, Gram Panchayat areas under a Master Plan/GTP Scheme, and IALA/SEZ areas notified by Government.
- Supersedes all existing rules/regulations/byelaws in conflict, to the extent of the conflict.
- In force from the date of Gazette publication (09.04.2012).
- **[AMENDED BY G.O.Ms.No.52/2022, Panchayat Raj & RD Dept]** Confirmed: under Telangana Panchayat Raj & Rural Development Act 2018 §278(2), the Government adopted these Rules (as amended) for **all Gram Panchayat areas of the State**, not only those under a notified Master Plan/GTP Scheme. All Gram Panchayat building/layout applications now process through the **TSbPASS Portal** under the Telangana State Building Permission Approval and Self Certification System Act, 2020 (Act 12 of 2020) — a separate Act governing the permit *process*, not covered in this ruleset.

### The new TS-bPASS tiered system for Municipalities/Corporations other than GHMC [Rule 23(b), G.O.62/2020]

**Tier (i) — Plot ≤75 sq.yds (63 sq.m): fully EXEMPT from building permission**
- Individual residential only, height ≤7m
- Process: online registration of intent + ₹1 token fee + online self-certification of plot size/floor count
- Must still comply with Master Plan/Detailed Planning Scheme/Local Area Plan/Building Rules
- **No Completion or Occupancy Certificate required at all**
- Exemption void if the plot/site/building is subdivided from something larger than 75 sq.yds for this purpose
- **Not available** for unauthorized layouts or Rule-3 restricted areas
- Misuse (construction on Government/prohibited/disputed/Municipal land, layout open spaces, parks, playgrounds) barred
- Violation penalty: imprisonment up to **3 years**, OR fine up to **25%** of Registration-Dept land/building value, OR demolition/take-over/sealing **without prior notice**, per Telangana Municipalities Act 2019 §177 & §180

**Tier (ii) — Individual Residential, plot ≤500 sq.m, height ≤10m: INSTANT online approval**
- Self-certification + prescribed documents + fee, submitted online
- Undertaking: violation of the sanctioned plan → liable for demolition without notice (§174)
- Must conform to Master Plan/zoning/building rules/NBC norms
- Permission generated **instantly** online on document upload + fee payment
- District Level TS-BPASS Committee post-verifies all instant approvals and submits recommendations within **21 days**
- Same §177/§180 penalty regime for misrepresentation as Tier (i)

**Tier (iii) — All Residential >500 sq.m or height >10m (incl. high-rise) + all Non-Residential: SINGLE WINDOW approval**
- One common online application to the Competent Authority
- Document-completeness check within **10 days**; incomplete applications rejected back to applicant with reasons
- Sanction (or refusal) within **21 days** of a complete application
- **Deemed approval**: no decision within 21 days → approval auto-generates online; the delaying official faces disciplinary action
- Deemed approval is revocable within 21 days of issuance if based on misrepresentation, false statements, or violates building rules/Master Plan land-use

**[ADDED BY G.O.62/2020 Amdt.V]** Rule 24(c) substituted: in Municipalities/Municipal Corporations other than GHMC **and HMDA**, all High-Rise Building applications go through the web-based Single Window System (see [[#X. High Rise Building Committees|Section X]] below for the committee itself).

**[ADDED BY G.O.7/2016 Amdt.20, new Rule 23(c)]** Compliance of public safety measures: the Competent Authority must make implementation of AP Public Safety (Measures) Enforcement Act 2013 & Rules 2014 a binding condition of building permission, and must verify full compliance (no violations) before issuing the Occupancy Certificate.
