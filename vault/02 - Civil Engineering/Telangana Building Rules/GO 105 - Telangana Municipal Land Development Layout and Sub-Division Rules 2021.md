# G.O.Ms.No.105 — Telangana Municipal Land Development (Layout & Sub-Division) Rules, 2021

A separate, complete framework from [[GO 168 - Telangana Building Rules 2012]] — GO 168 governs *buildings*, this governs *layouts* (subdividing land into plots and forming roads/infrastructure before anyone builds on them). Read in full and converted to structured form the same way as GO 168. Directly relevant to the maintainer's practice since layout work is common alongside building design.

**Source:** G.O.Ms.No.105, Municipal Administration & Urban Development (Plg.III) Dept., dated 05.07.2021. Scanned CamScanner PDF (no text layer) at `<your codes folder>\GO168 Amendments\GO_105_2021.pdf` — read via 16 rendered page images.

**Legal basis:** Issued under Telangana Municipalities Act 2019 (§172, 174–178, 180, 238) and the TSbPASS Act 2020 (§6). Supersedes the old layout rules in G.O.Ms.No.62 MA (1970) and G.O.Ms.No.1095 MA (1965).

**Applicability:** All land development through layout formation/sub-division, and any land-use change involving sub-division. Applies to **all Municipalities and Municipal Corporations in Telangana except GHMC and HMDA** (those have their own separate layout rules — see G.O.Ms.No.106/2020 for HMDA's).

## A. Key definitions [Rule 2]

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Layout | Laying out land into building plots with roads/streets (formed, leveled, metalled/black-topped), plus services (water, drainage, street lighting, open space, avenue plantation) |
| Approach Road | Any existing public access (pucca or kutcha) giving way to the site, whether or not it's part of an approved layout, over which the public has right of way |
| Licensed Technical Personnel (LTP) | Architect, Engineer, Town Planner, Structural Engineer, Surveyor, or other prescribed technical person who plans/designs/supervises layout development and certifies supervision |
| District Level Layout Approval Committee | Constituted under the Telangana Municipalities Act 2019 + TSbPASS Act 2020 |
| Competent Authority | The Commissioner of the concerned Municipality/Municipal Corporation |

## B. Application for layout permission [Rule 3]

- Every owner/developer/corporate body submits an **online application** under Municipalities Act §172(1), e-signed by owner/developer + LTP.
- Required uploads: geo-location plan (500m radius, showing buildings/watercourses/permanent features), layout plan at ≥1:500 scale (drawing + PDF) showing land boundaries (survey-record-based), existing/proposed road alignment & width, plot sizes/numbers, open-space/social-infrastructure/utility/parking allocation, electric lines, water bodies/nalas/sewers, and a contour plan.

## C. Documents to be uploaded [Rule 4]

Self-Certification (₹100 non-judicial stamp paper, Appendix A) · online initial permit fee **₹10,000** · self-attested ownership + link documents · Encumbrance Certificate · Land Conversion Certificate (Telangana Non-Agriculture Land Assessment Act 2006) · Master Plan/GTP/DTP/Local Area Plan extract · Revenue Village Map extract · NOCs if site is within 200m of a river/lake/pond/cheruvu/kunta/nala (Irrigation Dept ≥Executive Engineer + Revenue Dept ≥Additional/Joint Collector) · undertaking (Appendix B) · prior Environmental Clearance from SEIAA for land development ≥50 Hectares.

## D. Approach road width & mandatory open spaces [Rule 5]

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Min. approach road to the layout | **18m**. If existing road is narrower: form the 18m road up to the site, OR leave land for widening to 18m (equal both sides) + pay a road impact fee = **100%** of development charges (min. based on existing 9m road) |
| Road impact fee reduction | **50%** of the applicable category where the layout's approved Master Plan road is ≥18m but actual width is less |
| Road impact fee treatment | Over and above regular development charges; held in a dedicated escrow account for road land acquisition/formation |

### Table I — Internal road widths by length [p.5]

| Length of road | Min. road width | Splay required | Min. carriageway width |
|---|---|---|---|
| Up to 300m | 9m | 3m | 6m |
| Above 300–500m | 12m | 3m | 8m |
| Above 500–1000m | 18m | 4.5m | 11m |
| Above 1000m | 24m | 6m | 15m |

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Open space | **≥10%** of layout area, handed to the Municipality/Corporation via registered gift deed at tentative-layout approval — **9%** for green space/lung space/parks/tree plantation, **1%** for utilities (water tank/sump, STP, transformer, solid waste mgmt, garbage bin, common parking) |
| Social infrastructure | **2.5%** of layout area for pharmacy/clinic/school/play-school/crèche/dispensary/convenient shops — saleable by the layout owner, exclusively for these uses |
| Min. plot area | **50 sq.m** |
| Min. plot width | **6.00m** |
| Mortgage | **15%** of plotted area mortgaged to the Municipality/Corporation via registered deed at tentative approval, shown on the layout plan |
| Layouts ≥50 acres | Additional Educational/Health/Commercial facilities per Appendix C (below) |

## E. Restrictions near certain features [Rule 6]

| Feature | Restriction |
|---|---|
| FTL of water bodies (river/pond/cheruvu/kunta/nala) | **No approval** for land development |
| River FTL boundary | **50m** buffer (developed as buffer zone) |
| Lake/Tank/Kunta FTL, ≥10 Ha | **30m** buffer |
| Lake/Tank/Kunta FTL, <10 Ha | **9m** buffer |
| Canal/Vagu/Nala/Storm Water Drain, width >10m | **9m** buffer |
| Canal/Vagu/Nala/Storm Water Drain, width ≤10m | **2m** buffer |
| Buffer zone use | May be used for a road ≥12m wide where feasible; reckoned as open space, not setback |
| Railways | NOC **not required**; 30m buffer from railway property is the applicant's own responsibility per Railway Authority rules |
| High Tension electricity line | **3m** min safety distance; tower-base width as green belt + 10m road either side (or per Master Plan); if road isn't feasible below the HT line, green belt alone is allowed there, additional to the 10% open space |
| Low Tension electricity line | **1.5m** min safety distance |
| Defence establishments | NOC **not required**; applicant follows Defence Authority rules independently |
| Heritage structures | **≤100m** from a protected monument: no plot permitted at all; **100–200m**: prior National Monument Authority permission; within vicinity of any notified Heritage Structure: prior NOC from the concerned Competent Authority |
| Oil/Gas pipelines | Prior clearance from the Oil/Gas Authority if a pipeline abuts/passes through the site |

## F. Approval of Tentative Layout [Rule 7]

| Step | Timeline |
|---|---|
| Application processed via self-certification, online | Approval within **21 days** |
| Shortfall/clarification notice to applicant | Within **10 days** of receipt; applicant must respond within **7 days** or the application auto-closes |
| District Collector approval after additional documents | Processed online; fee intimation letter follows |
| Fee payment + registered gift deed (roads/open spaces) + mortgage deed (15% of plotted area) | Within **30 days**; late payment allowed for another 30 days with **10%** interest; otherwise auto-closed |
| Tentative layout plan communicated | Within **3 days** of gift/mortgage deed submission + fee payment |

**Conditions of tentative layout [Rule 8]:** infrastructure/amenities (Appendix D) completed within **2 years** of tentative approval; one **+1 year** extension available at 20% of the total fee paid; failure → Commissioner executes the works itself (funded by selling mortgaged plots) and **blacklists** the owner/developer state-wide, individually or in partnership.

### Approval procedure [Rule 9]
Step 1: Online submission routes to site inspection + title/technical scrutiny officers; District Collector & Additional Collector (Local Bodies) get a dashboard of all applications.
Step 2: Site inspection by District Committee within **5 days**; simultaneous title/technical scrutiny by Municipal/Corporation Town Planning staff (sites ≤10 acres) or Director of Town Planning (>10 acres), who report within **5 days** or it's **deemed approved**. Water-body/nala proximity (200m lakes/rivers, 50m nalas) triggers Irrigation/Revenue Dept referral, **10-day** turnaround.
Step 3: District Collector approves/rejects/seeks more info within **7 days** of the reports.
Step 4: Fee intimation letter within **3 days** of approval (auto-generated if not issued); after payment + deeds submitted, tentative layout released within **3 days** (auto-generated otherwise), with the 2-year completion condition.

## G. District Level Layout Approval Committee [Rule 10]

| Role | Position |
|---|---|
| District Collector | Chairman |
| Additional Collector (Local Bodies) | Convener |
| Superintending/Executive Engineer, R&B Dept | Member |
| Superintending/Executive Engineer, Irrigation Dept | Member |
| Superintending/Executive Engineer, Panchayat Raj Dept | Member |
| Any officer nominated by District Collector | Member |
| District Town & Country Planning Officer (DTCPO) | Member (stationed at District HQ, reports to Collector/Additional Collector) |

## H. Final Layout Approval [Rule 11]

Required after infrastructure completion: layout plan as executed (signed/e-signed by owner+LTP) · final approval fee · Self-Certification (₹100 stamp paper, Appendix E) · attested registered mortgage deed copy · Encumbrance Certificate (1 day before applying, confirming the mortgaged area is unsold and vests with the Municipality/Corporation) · geo-location plan of layout+open spaces · dated photographs of completed development work.

Process: Member Convener consolidates applications for the District Committee → District Collector inspects via the Committee to verify conformity with the tentative approval → Committee (headed by District Collector) approves the final layout if standards are met.

## I. Release & revocation [Rules 12–13]

- Final layout plan/proceedings released within **3 days** of approval (auto-generated otherwise); mortgaged plots released to the owner/developer within **21 days** of final approval.
- **Revocation:** the Municipal Commissioner may revoke any permission found to rest on a false statement or misrepresentation.

## J. Modifications, user charges, penalties [Rules 14–17]

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Modification during execution | Any deviation from the tentative approved layout requires a revised layout approval; **no modification allowed once the final layout is approved** |
| User charges | **₹2/sq.m** of total site area at tentative approval, revisable by Government; held in escrow for TSbPASS implementation costs; other fees pass directly to the Municipality/Corporation/line departments |
| Penalties | Self-certification compliance liability rests on Owner, Applicant, and LTP; false declaration/misrepresentation/misuse of public-purpose land → liable under Telangana Municipalities Act 2019 §177 & §180; a layout obtained by false statement gets its permission **revoked** by the Commissioner |
| Blacklisting | Failure to execute required works (Appendix D) within the stipulated period → Commissioner executes the works via Section 172(8) of the Municipalities Act (funded by selling mortgaged plots) **and** blacklists the owner/developer |

## Appendix C — Facilities required for layouts ≥50 acres

A detailed population-linked facility schedule (educational, health, commercial, communication/essential services, social/cultural, sports, solid waste). Reference-by-page treatment rather than full transcription here — it's a lookup table triggered only for large layouts, with facility type, applicable layout-area threshold, population-based scale, and minimum area required for each of ~15 facility types (nursery/primary/high school, degree college, general hospital, shops, post office, electrical substation, police station/post, fire station, religious building, community hall, sports centres, solid waste transfer station). Pull page 14 of the source directly when sizing amenities for a layout in this size class.

## Appendix D — Layout development works & infrastructure (referenced throughout as the completion checklist)

Road formation with proper sub-surface/camber/kerbing/metalling/side drains/central medians (medians for roads ≥18m) · black-topping or cement-concrete carriageway (BIS Code) including the main approach road to the nearest public road · drainage/nala channelization for storm water, conserving/harvesting into the nearest water body or open space · street lighting/electricity · independent sewerage disposal + protected water supply (OHT/sumps) in exclusive area, not counted toward mandatory open space · underground ducting of utilities under footpaths/medians · greenery/avenue plantation in open spaces · low compound wall with iron grill around open spaces, handed to the local body free via registered gift deed.

## Related — G.O.Ms.No.106/2020 (HMDA's own Layout Rules amendment)

Not part of this GO — HMDA area layouts are governed separately (G.O.Ms.No.288/2008, 287/2008, 33/2013), but the same Telangana amendment wave touched HMDA's rules too: **G.O.Ms.No.106, MA&UD (Plg.III), dated 06.07.2020** raised HMDA's minimum layout approach road width from **30 feet (9m) to 100 feet (~30.5m)**, plus a road impact fee scale where the existing road is narrower:

| Existing road width | Additional fee (% of development charges) |
|---|---|
| 80–<100 ft | 50% |
| 60–<80 ft | 66% |
| <60 ft (min. 30 ft) | 100% |
| Non-high-rise apartments <18m height, >100 flats | 50% |

Applies to pending/new draft layout applications only (not final layouts already approved as drafts); if the Master Plan road is ≥100ft but actual width is less, fee = 50% of the applicable category above. Fee held in escrow for road land acquisition/formation, same mechanism as G.O.105/2021's own road impact fee.
