# Civil Engineering

The maintainer's day-to-day civil engineering practice and project work.

## Notes in this folder
- [[NBC 2026]] — structured, page-anchored reference for the National Building Construction Standards 2026 (SP 7:2026), built section-by-section from the full 2,999-page PDF. See its coverage table for progress.
- [[Egress Ruleset - Part F]] — Phase 1 pilot: NBC 2026 egress/staircase/exit-width clauses converted into checkable rule tables (parameter/condition/threshold/source), with exceptions kept as footnotes.
- [[Fire Prevention and Protection Ruleset - Part F]] — the rest of Part F (fire ratings, compartmentation, smoke control, gas supply, detection/alarm, protection installations, refuge, all Annexes) converted the same way. Between these two notes, Part F is now fully in ruleset form.
- [[IS Codes]] (subfolder) — Bureau of Indian Standards codes and handbooks, started 2026-09-11 with IS 456 (Plain and Reinforced Concrete). Now covers IS 456, IS 875 (all 5 parts), IS 1893, IS 13920, SP 16, SP 34, and the full 28-part IS 1200 method-of-measurement series — see [[IS Codes]] for the coverage table and audit status.
- [[Telangana Building Rules]] (subfolder) — state-level building/development orders, started 2026-09-13 with [[GO 168 - Telangana Building Rules 2012]] (Main Rules 1–29 built and verified; Annexures in progress) plus the full per-town [[GO 168 Annexure I-II - Locality Schedule]].
