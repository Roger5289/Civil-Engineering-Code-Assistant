# Part D, Section 1 — Lighting and Natural Ventilation

Requirements and design methods for natural/artificial lighting and natural/mechanical ventilation of buildings — window sizing, illuminance levels by occupancy, daylight calculation, ventilation rates, and thermal comfort. PDF pp. 1525–1640. Read together with [[Part D Sec 2 - Electrical and Allied Installations]] (artificial lighting electrical installation) and [[Part D Sec 3 - Air-Conditioning, Heating and Mechanical Ventilation]] (mechanical ventilation/AC).

## 1 Scope (p. 1531)
Covers lighting and natural ventilation requirements for buildings, including barrier-free lighting/ventilation for elderly and persons with disabilities in public buildings.

## 2 Terminology (pp. 1531–1537)

## 4 Lighting (pp. 1541–1583)
### 4.1 Principles
- 4.1.2.3: immediate-surrounding band (min 0.5 m) around task area must not fall below **Table 4** values relative to task illuminance (p. 1544): task >750 lx → surround ≥500 lx; 500→300; 300→200; 200→150; <150→equal.
- 4.1.3: Glare — minimum luminaire shielding angles by lamp luminance: 1–20 kcd/m² → 10°; 20–50 → 15°; 50–500 → 20°; >500 → 30° (p. 1544).
- **4.1.5.1 Corridors, passages and stairways (p. 1547)** — directly relevant to stair design: transitions between bright/dim zones must be uniformly lit; stairways must avoid direct glare and harsh shadows obscuring steps; tread/step-strip lighting recommended, especially for emergency egress paths.

- Daylight factor = Sky Component (SC) + External Reflected Component (ERC) + Internal Reflected Component (IRC). SC from **Table 20/21/22** (Annex B, pp. 1628–1633 — large lookup tables by l/d, h/d ratio; read directly from PDF for exact values, not reproduced here).

### 4.3–4.4 Artificial Lighting & Energy Conservation (pp. 1575–1583)
- Lumen method design steps 4.3.2.1–4.3.2.4; utilization factor depends on room index, surface reflectances (white 0.7, light 0.5, mid 0.3, dark 0.1).
- **Table 10 — Light Source Efficacy** (p. 1580): Incandescent 12-20 lm/W (500-1000h life); Fluorescent 57-67 lm/W (5000h); High-pressure sodium 90-125 lm/W (10-15k h); LED 60-100 lm/W (10 000h, best lumen maintenance).
- Fig. 3/4 (pp. 1576, 1578) — nomograms for supplementary fluorescent lighting and daylight/artificial light integration by fenestration %.
- 4.4.6.3: corridors/staircases → direct-type luminaires with wide light spread recommended; white fluorescent tubes cut energy use to 1/4.5 of incandescent for corridors/stairs.

### 4.5–4.6 Circadian & Outdoor Lighting (pp. 1583–1585)
Circadian lighting required where day-active occupants spend >4h without external window sightline. Outdoor lighting zoned urban/suburban/rural with fully-shielded, full-cutoff fixtures to limit light pollution/sky glow.

- Adaptive thermal comfort equations by building type (p. 1589–1590):
  - Naturally ventilated commercial: `T_indoor = 0.54×T_outdoor + 12.83` (±2.38°C acceptability)
  - Mixed-mode commercial: `T_indoor = 0.28×T_outdoor + 17.87` (±3.46°C)
  - Residential (all modes): `T_indoor = 0.42×T_outdoor + 17.45` (±2.15°C)
- **Table 15 — Max permissible wet-bulb temp for given dry-bulb** (p. 1598): 30°C→29.0°C WB max ... 50°C→27.0°C WB max (industrial heat-stress limit, ≤1h continuous exposure).
- 5.4.3.1: Natural ventilation design rules by wind action — orientation within 0–30° of prevailing wind; inlet at low level windward, outlet high level leeward; sill height 85% of critical occupancy height (sitting on chair 0.75m, bed 0.60m, floor 0.40m); opening area 20–30% of floor area gives ~30% of outdoor wind velocity indoors (max ~40% achievable); industrial building max effective width for natural ventilation by wind = 30 m.
- **Table 16 — Allowable Temperature Rise for industrial exhaust design** (p. 1600): outlet height 6m→3-4.5°C rise, 9m→4.5-6.5°C, 12m→6.5-11°C.
- **Table 17 — Optimum Ceiling Fan Size/Number** by room width×length (p. 1612) — e.g. 3m×4m room → one 1200mm fan; 12m×14m → nine 1400mm fans. Full grid pp. 1611–1612.
- **Table 18 — Temperature rise from fan-induced air speed** (p. 1613): fan speed I (0.6 m/s) → +1.2–2.8°C acceptable rise; speed IV (1.5 m/s) → +2.5–4.0°C.
- Design charts DC-01 to DC-04 (Figs 10–14, pp. 1605–1608; worked example Annex C pp. 1634–1638) for sizing natural-ventilation openings by buoyancy or wind-driven flow, single-sided or cross-ventilation — formulas given, chart values read directly from PDF.
- Ventilation rate formulas: wind-driven `Qw = K×A×V` (K=0.6 perpendicular, 0.3 at <45°); stack effect `QT = 7.0×A×√[h(tr−to)]`; combined `Q² = Qw² + Qt²`.
