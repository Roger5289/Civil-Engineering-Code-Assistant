# Part F — Fire and Life Safety

Scope: fire prevention (passive/constructional), life safety in relation to fire (means of egress), and fire protection (detection, alarm, suppression) of buildings — occupancy classification, construction types, egress, and protection features to minimise danger to life and property from fire. **PDF pages 2731–2974** (pages 2735 onwards are clause text; 2975–2999 are the whole-document Index of Terms, not Part F).

**Legal status:** Fire Services is a State subject (XIIth Schedule, Article 243(w)). This Part is **guidance and referral** for state governments / local authorities — actual fire NOC and enforceable rules come from the State Fire Services Act / State Building Byelaws / DCRs. Build to Part F as the best-practice minimum; the AHJ (Authority Having Jurisdiction) can be stricter.

This is the largest and most design-critical NBC section for architectural/structural checking — occupancy, egress, travel distance, fire ratings, compartmentation. Checkable numbers are in **bold**. Large per-occupancy / per-industry / per-material tables (Table 7 series, Annex B, Annex C, Annex N) are referenced by page, not transcribed.

Running header on every page: "PART F  FIRE AND LIFE SAFETY".

## 1 Scope — when Part F applies (p. 2739)

Part F's provisions apply to **high-rise and special buildings** beyond these height/area limits (unless a clause says otherwise):

| Occupancy | Height beyond which Part F applies | Floor area beyond which it applies |
|---|---|---|
| Residential | 24 m | 750 m² |
| Educational | 9 m | 500 m² |
| Institutional | 15 m | 500 m² |
| Assembly | 9 m | 750 m² |
| Business | 15 m | 750 m² |
| Mercantile | 15 m | 1 000 m² |
| Industrial low/moderate hazard (G-1/G-2) | any height, if total building area > 2 000 m² | |
| Industrial high hazard (G-3) | 15 m | 500 m² |
| Storage | 9 m | 500 m² |
| Hazardous | 9 m | 500 m² |
| Mixed use | 15 m | 1 000 m² |

- Buildings below these limits with a **single staircase** → that staircase must be firefighting-shaft type on the external periphery, lobby + stairwell both ventilated (min 1.6 m² permanently-open fixed louvre); 2 h fire door to lobby, 1 h to stairwell.
- Also applies on **alteration over an area ≥ 1 000 m²**, or on change of occupancy.
- Performance-based design (Annex M) — only for heritage buildings; other cases need state fire-authority sign-off.

## 2 Terminology — the definitions that drive checks (p. 2740–2748)

- **High-rise building = 24 m or above in height, any occupancy** (2.39). Building height for fire safety = to the floor level of the last occupiable floor from grade (where the fire tender stands); sloped terrain → from the lowest fire-vehicle-approachable level.
- **Fire resistance = R (stability) + E (integrity — no flame/hot-gas passage) + I (insulation — unexposed face ≤ 180 °C at any point, ≤ 140 °C average)** (2.28). W (radiation) for glass. Rating in minutes.
- **Firefighting shaft / fire tower (2.24):** enclosed shaft, **120 min** fire resistance = protected lobby + staircase + fireman's lift; connects to exit discharge (directly or via 120 min exit passageway); lobby accommodates ≥ 2 wheelchairs; 120 min fire doors, fireman's talk-back, wet riser + landing valve in the lobby.
- **Fireman's lift (2.27):** lift(s) with all firefighting-shaft features; serves basements; lobby pressurised; doubles as the evacuation lift for assisted evacuation. Regular lifts serving upper floors terminate at exit-discharge level. Lift bank → prefer all lifts as fireman's lifts.
- **Means of egress = exit access + exit + exit discharge** (2.44). "Exit" = protected staircase. Exits not leading to a fire exit are signed 'NOT A FIRE EXIT'.
- **Common path of travel (2.8):** the portion of exit access traversed before two separate routes to two exits become available — part of the travel distance.
- **Travel distance (2.64):** from any point to a protected exit / external escape route / final exit, along the line of travel.
- **Horizontal exit (2.40):** alternative egress from a compartment to a refuge area or another compartment at/near the same level, through a **120 min fire door in a 120 min fire-resistant wall**.
- **Fire wall / fire separating wall (2.34):** fire-rated, continuous foundation to roof (and ≥ 1 m above a combustible roof), structurally stable so either side can collapse without the wall collapsing.
- **Fire compartment (2.21):** space enclosed by fire barriers / fire-resistant walls on all sides including top and bottom.
- **Fire door in exits:** min insulation criterion 30 min; intumescent seal; self-closing (not held open); fire curtains not allowed as fire exits.
- **Refuge area (2.57):** protected staging area for temporary use during egress; firefighting-shaft lift-lobby "refuge spaces" can substitute.
- **Floor area gross (2.35)** = within outside walls, no deductions (low-density occupant-load calc). **Floor area net (2.36)** = after deducting corridors, stairs, toilets, walls, columns, shafts, services, atrium, escalator cut-out (high-density calc) — **not carpet area**.
- **Wet riser (2.70):** vertical main ≥ 100 mm nominal dia, landing valve each floor, permanently charged. **Dry riser (2.11):** ≥ 100 mm, normally dry, charged by fire-service pumping. **Down-comer (2.10):** ≥ 100 mm internal dia from a terrace tank via terrace pump.
- **Ramp (2.56):** inclined plane ≥ 1:20 (5 %) from horizontal.
- **Public way (2.55):** open to air, clear width & height ≥ 3 m.
- Fire load density by building type (wood equivalent kg/m²): Residential 25; Institutional & Educational 25; Assembly / Business 25–50; Mercantile up to 250; Industrial up to 150; Storage & Hazardous up to 500 (verify p.2743 — extraction risk).

## 3 Fire Prevention (p. 2748–2766)

### 3.1 Occupancy classification (Groups A–K)
- **A Residential** — A-I lodging/rooming, A-II dormitories, A-III apartment houses (3+ families), A-IV hotels up to 4-star, A-V starred hotels (5-star+).
- **B Educational** — school/college/training, ≥ 20 students.
- **C Institutional** — C-I hospitals & sanatoria, C-II custodial, C-III penal & mental.
- **D Assembly** — ≥ 50 persons gather: malls, theatres, auditoria, museums, restaurants/food courts, places of worship, stadia, transport terminals, underground & elevated railways.
- **E Business** — E-I human-occupied (offices, courts, libraries, labs, IT parks, call centres, TV/broadcast, ATC towers), E-II datacentres (machine environment).
- **F Mercantile** — F-I shops/stores/markets, F-II underground shopping centres.
- **G Industrial** — G-I low / G-II moderate / G-III high hazard (see Annex B).
- **H Storage** — warehouses, cold storage, garages, hangars (few persons per unit area).
- **J Hazardous** — highly combustible/explosive; e.g. pressurised gas storage > 0.1 N/mm² and > 70 m³.
- **K Mixed occupancy.**
- **Minor occupancy:** incidental to the main, **≤ 30 % of floor area** → classified as the main occupancy; over 30 % → mixed occupancy.
- **Mixed-occupancy separation matrix (3.1.11.1, p.2752):** minimum fire resistance rating for the separation between any two occupancies — most cross-pairs **120 min**; **240 min** for e.g. Business↔High-hazard-industrial, Moderate-industrial↔Hazardous, Storage↔Hazardous; **180 min** Moderate-industrial↔Storage; many pairs with Hazardous or High-hazard-industrial = NP (not permitted).
- Mixed-occupancy building governed by the **most restrictive** provisions among constituents. Change to a more hazardous group → must comply with the new group. Certificate of occupancy required before any change of occupancy.

### 3.3 Existing buildings
Need not comply unless altered or deemed a hazard. Alterations must not reduce fire/life safety below the previous level. Partitioning affecting travel distance, or densification, over floor area > 1 000 m² → local fire-authority approval.

### 3.4 Types of construction (Type 1–4)
- Non-combustible construction; **staircase-enclosure internal walls minimum 120 min rating** (brick / RCC / equivalent).
- **3.4.3 Load-bearing steel beams and columns in buildings with total covered area ≥ 500 m² must be fire-protected against collapse** (rated materials + suppression; Annex C Tables 25 & 26).
- **3.4.4 False ceiling and all its suspension fixtures must be non-combustible.**
- **Table 1 — Fire Resistance Ratings of Structural and Non-Structural Elements (minutes)** [Cl 3.4.1/3.4.2/3.5.9, p.2754–2755]:

  | Element | Type 1 | Type 2 | Type 3 | Type 4 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Exterior bearing walls (fire separation < 3.7 m) | 240 | 120 | 120 | 60 |
  | Exterior non-bearing walls (< 3.7 m) | 120 | 90 | 60 | 60 |
  | Exterior non-bearing walls (3.7–9 m) | 90 | 60 | 60 | 60 |
  | Exterior non-bearing walls (≥ 9 m) | 60 | 60 | 60 | 60 |
  | Fire separation assemblies (fire check doors) | 120 | 120 | 120 | 120 |
  | Fire enclosures of exits | 120 | 120 | 120 | 120 |
  | Shafts for services, lift hoistway, refuse chutes | 120 | 120 | 120 | 120 |
  | Vertical separation between adjacent tenant spaces | 60 | 60 | 60 | 60 |
  | Dwelling-unit separation — load bearing / non-load bearing | 120 / 60 | 120 / 60 | 60 / 30 | 60 / 30 |
  | Interior bearing walls/columns/beams/trusses supporting > 1 floor | 240 | 120 | 120 | 120 |
  | ... supporting 1 floor only / roof only | 180 | 90 | 60 | 60 |
  | Walls supporting structural members | 180 | 90 | 60 | 60 |

### 3.5 General requirements for all occupancies
- **High-rise under construction (3.5.3.2):** dry riser ≥ 100 mm dia with hydrant outlets + fire-service inlet; 2 000 litre water drums + 2 fire buckets per floor; water tank ≥ 20 000 litre.
- **Openings in fire-resistant walls/floors (3.5.4):** Types 1–3 → opening in a fire-resistant wall limited to **5.6 m² area, max height/width 2.75 m**, protected with **≥ 120 min** fire doors; floor openings in ≥ 120 min vertical enclosures. Escalator openings → **450–600 mm** smoke barrier + sprinklers all sides each floor (sprinkler-to-barrier 150–300 mm).
- **Service ducts/shafts ≥ 120 min.** Electrical shaft inspection door ≥ 120 min with 30 min insulation. Plumbing shafts in the core → inspection doors ≥ 30 min; plumbing shafts opening to wet/ventilated/external areas → no specified rating. Fire-stop material (≥ 120 min) around cable/pipe penetrations.
- **Refuse chutes:** opening ≥ 1 m above roof; non-combustible enclosure ≥ 120 min; not in staircase enclosure / service shafts; inspection panels 60 min; sprinklered; **≥ 3 m from exits**.
- **Clear height in exit access ≥ 2 100 mm** (3.5.4.6).
- **Electrical (3.5.5):** flame-retardant wiring; MV/LV in false ceiling/shafts in metal conduit; 230 V above false ceiling → 660 V grade insulation; HV/MV/LV in separate shafts; electrical cable shaft sealed at every floor with fire-stop = floor rating; no water/gas/telephone lines in the electrical duct.
- **Emergency power (3.5.5.2)** for: fire pumps; pressurisation & smoke venting (+ dampers/actuators); fireman's lifts (all lifts); exit-signage & emergency lighting; fire alarm; PA/voice evacuation; magnetic door hold-open; FCC & security-room lighting. Normal + standby (DG) with changeover; DG handles all their starting current. Cabling via fire-proof enclosures / fire-survival cables / adjoining fire compartment.
- **Substation (3.5.5.3):** independent ventilated substation + LT panel room on ground level or first basement, accessible from outside, **120 min** walls & doors. **Oil-filled substation:** utility building / outdoor only, **≥ 6 m from adjoining buildings**; equipment > 2 000 litre oil → **240 min** baffle walls between units (≥ 600 mm above equipment, ≥ 300 mm each side); **transformers > 10 MVA → HV water spray / HP water mist**. **Dry-type substation:** transformers inside buildings must be dry type; all walls/ceiling/floor/openings **120 min**; access from the nearest fire exit.
- **DG sets (3.5.5.4):** only on ground floor or first basement; DG room **120 min**; day oil tank in a dyked enclosure ≥ 110 % of tank volume with 300 mm of sand.
- Lightning down-conductors must NOT run through electrical/service shafts.
- **Escape lighting (3.5.6):** floor-centreline luminance **≥ 10 lumen/m²**; routes up to 2 m wide → 50 % of width at ≥ 5 lumen/m²; assembly during performances ≥ 2 lux. **Emergency lighting on within 5 s of failure, maintained ≥ 90 min.** Battery-pack lighting not a substitute for DG. Luminaires ≥ 2 m above floor, within ~2 m of intersections/exits/direction-changes/stairs.
- **Exit signage (3.5.6.4):** no point in exit access more than **30 m from a visible exit directional sign**; sign at every direction change; **green**; illuminated on alternative supply; floor-indicating boards on every landing.
- **AC / ventilation / smoke control (3.5.7)** — mirrors [[Part D Sec 3 - Air-Conditioning, Heating and Mechanical Ventilation]] clause 19: separate AHU per floor; ducts not through fire exits/passageways; return air not through exits/lift lobbies; duct within a compartment min 30 min, crossing without dampers → 120 min; fire/smoke dampers at fire-separation wall, shaft entry, floor penetrations, and each compartment's supply & return inlet on every floor; non-combustible duct insulation; battery rooms segregated by 120 min.
- **Heating (3.5.8):** boiler room **180 min**, entry door 120 min composite; dedicated ventilation not through electrical room or exits; oil tank dyked ≥ 110 % + 300 mm sand.
- **Glazing & glass facade (3.5.9):** glazing assembly rated to the construction type (Table 1); **glass not permitted for enclosures of exits and exit passageways**. Fully sprinklered building, fire separation **≥ 9 m** → tempered glass in non-combustible assembly, sprinklers within **600 mm** of the facade; fire separation **< 9 m** → add **≥ 900 mm spandrel panel, 1 h rating**. Floor-slab-to-facade gaps sealed at every level (equal rating to slab). **Openable fire-access panels each floor, ≤ 10 m apart** (centre-to-centre), operable at **1.2–1.5 m from floor**, **≥ 1 000 × 1 000 mm**, opening outwards, marked 'FIRE OPENABLE PANEL — OPEN IN CASE OF FIRE, DO NOT OBSTRUCT' (≥ 25 mm letters), in common areas/corridors too.
- **Surface interior finishes (3.5.10):** flame-spread Class 1 (very low) / 2 (low) / 3 (medium) / 4 (rapid). Class 1 anywhere; Class 2 anywhere except walls, building facade, staircases, corridors; Class 3 only in living/bed rooms as lining to solid walls; Class 4 (incl. untreated wood fibreboard) only as ceiling lining ≥ 2.4 m above floor below, with fire-retardant treatment, never in kitchens/corridors/staircases. **Aluminium composite panels (ACP) as external facade should be avoided; if used → ≥ 70 % mineral core + pass ISO 3957 / ISO 13785.**
- **Fire Command Centre (3.5.11):** on the entrance floor with direct access; **120 min walls + fire door**; emergency lighting; non-flammable finishes; houses main fire alarm panel, PA, all controls/monitoring of fire alarm + pressurisation + smoke management; 2 laminated bound sets of floor plans + firefighting-equipment details.

## 4 Life Safety (p. 2766–2843)

### 4.2 General exit requirements
- Lifts, escalators, moving walks, revolving doors, turnstiles are **not fire exits** and don't count toward required egress capacity.
- **Exit doors open in the direction of exit (4.2.9).** Assembly (D) and institutional (C-1): exit door must open onto a **landing**, not directly onto a flight of stairs. Other occupancies: an open door must not reduce the landing pathway by more than half the staircase width. No overhead/sliding doors.
- **At least half the required exit stairs from upper floors (rounded up) discharge directly to the exterior / via exit passageways (4.2.10).** Buildings < 60 m: firefighting shaft on an external wall discharging directly outside. Buildings ≥ 60 m: firefighting shaft(s) discharge directly to exterior via exit passageway.
- **Fire exits & exit passageways: clear ceiling height ≥ 2.4 m; fire-exit door height ≥ 2.0 m (4.2.11).**
- **Walking surfaces in exit access (4.2.18):** slope in travel direction ≤ **1:20**, cross-slope ≤ **1:48**, slip-resistant.
- **Basement (4.2.19):** not for sleeping, classrooms, hazardous industry, restaurant kitchen, battery/UPS rooms, fuel-oil storage; must have smoke ventilation + high-density sprinklers + redundant/enclosed exits; **never fewer than 2 independent basement exits**; basement car park with direct approach from above → 120 min fire doors; **minimum clear height 3.4 m**.

### 4.4 Egress components
- **Dead-end corridor ≤ 6 m for educational/institutional/assembly; ≤ 15 m for other occupancies (4.4.2.2).** Fire exits placed as remote from each other as possible.
- **Capacity: unit of exit width = 500 mm**; a clear 250 mm counts as a half-unit; < 250 mm not counted. Capacity of a component = width ÷ capacity factor (Table 3). System limited by its most restrictive component. Number of means of egress computed floor-by-floor, never decreasing along the path.
- **Table 3 — Capacity Factors (width per person, mm)** [p.2774]:

  | Occupancy | Stairways | Level components & ramps |
  |---|---|---|
  | Residential (A), Educational (B) | 10 | 6.5 |
  | Institutional (C) | 15 | 13 |
  | Assembly (D), Business (E), Mercantile (F), Industrial (G), Storage (H) | 10 | 6.5 |
  | Hazardous (J) | 18 | 10 |

  (1 000 mm door at factor 6.5 → serves ≈ 153 persons.)
- **Exit separation (4.4.2.3): minimum = diagonal of the served area ÷ 2 (unsprinklered), ÷ 3 (sprinklered).**

**4.4.2.4 Doorways:**
- **No exit doorway less than 1 000 mm wide; assembly buildings not less than 2 000 mm.** Doorway height ≥ 2 000 mm. Operable from the served side without a key. No mirrors on/in exit doors.
- Revolving door: book-fold collapse (parallel paths ≥ 1 000 mm), not within 3 m of stair/escalator, hinged door of equal capacity within 3 m; counts for only **50 persons**.
- Access-controlled/electromagnetic doors: unlock on sprinkler/fire-detection activation (until manual reset), on power loss; manual 'PUSH TO EXIT' cutting lock power.

| Institutional (C) | 30 | 45 | 22.5 |

| Datacentre building | — | 60 | — |

- **Common path of travel ≤ 50 % of the above.**
- Ground-floor ceiling ≥ 10 m (airports, exhibition/convention halls, mall atria, entrance lobbies) + sprinklered → travel distance +100 % in those areas (not to the sprinklered column); ≥ 17 m with sprinkler exemption → also +100 % with smoke/heat venting.

### 4.4.2.4.3 Staircases
- **All high-rise & special buildings: minimum 2 staircases.** Required fire-exit staircases recommended FAR-free.
- Staircases discharge (a) directly / (b) via exit passageway / (c) via a large lobby — **≥ 50 % via (a) and/or (b)**. Firefighting shafts: (a) preferred, (c) not allowed; residential & educational firefighting shafts → (a) only.
- **Building/floor with ≥ 3 compartments → at least 2 firefighting shafts.**
- **Tread width (no nosing): ≥ 250 mm residential; ≥ 300 mm** for assembly/hotels/educational/institutional/business/other. Anti-slip.
- **Riser height: ≤ 175 mm one/two-family dwellings; ≤ 150 mm other buildings.** Risers per flight recommended 12, **never more than 15**.
- Internal stairs: non-combustible, **≥ 120 min**; not around a lift shaft; no flue / electromechanical equipment / AC units / gas piping / electrical panels in the stairway; no living space / store / fire risk opening directly into staircases; lifts not opening into staircases or mid-landings; no combustible panelling.
- **Minimum staircase width by typology (4.4.2.4.3.2e):**
  - One/two-family dwelling: **1.00 m** (row housing 2 storeys: 0.75 m)
  - Residential A-I / A-II / A-III: **1.25 m**
  - Residential hotel A-IV / A-V: **1.50 m**
  - Assembly building: **2.00 m** (1.50 m if < 150 persons)
  - Educational: **1.50 m**
  - Institutional: **2.00 m** (hospital stairs max 2.00 m)
  - All other buildings: **1.50 m**
  - Width > 2.00 m → central handrail recommended.
- Handrail one side if width < 1 500 mm, both sides if ≥ 1 500 mm; handrail projection into width **≤ 90 mm**.
- **Minimum headroom under landing / under staircase: 2.2 m.** Access to fire-exit staircase via **≥ 120 min** fire door. Floor indication board inside near the fire door **≥ 300 × 200 mm**. All staircases terminate at exit-discharge level; basement access by a separate staircase. Scissor-type staircases not part of exit.
- **Curved stairs** not a means of egress; may be exit access if tread depth ≥ 280 mm at 350 mm from the narrow end and smallest radius ≥ 2 × stair width.
- **External staircases (4.4.2.4.3.4):** directly connected to ground; entrance separate/remote from the internal stair; openings within **3 m** protected with **≥ 60 min** fire doors/windows; non-combustible, doorway ≥ 120 min; **inclination ≤ 45° from horizontal**; straight flight **≥ 1 500 mm wide**; handrails both sides **1 000–1 200 mm high**, baluster gap ≤ 150 mm. Spiral staircase only for low occupant load, building ≤ 9 m, **diameter ≥ 1 500 mm**.
- **Ramps (4.4.2.4.3.5):** slope **≤ 1 in 12 (8 %)**; slip-resistant, no perforations; direction change preceded by **1.5 × 1.5 m** landings; landings at top, bottom, and at doors onto the ramp; every landing ≥ 1 500 mm long (1 250 mm if not an accessible route with a straight run); handrails both sides. (Not applicable to basement car-park ramps.)

### 4.4.2.5 Smoke control of exits / pressurisation
- **Staircase pressure difference = 50 Pa. Lobbies/corridors = 25–30 Pa.** Enclosed staircase adjacent to a lobby (pressurised or not) = 50 Pa.
- On emergency pressurisation: stop recirculation (all exhaust to atmosphere), stop supply to non-exit spaces. Activation from fire alarm panel.
- Pressurised ↔ unpressurised areas segregated by **120 min** fire-resistant wall. Fresh-air intake **≥ 4 m** from exhaust outlets.
- **Table 5 — Pressurisation of Staircases and Lift Lobbies** [p.2782–2783], by height < 15 m / 15–30 m / > 30 m:
  - Internal staircase without external wall: pressurised at all heights (except residential apartment houses / one-or-two-family dwellings below 15 m).
  - Internal staircase with external wall: naturally/cross-ventilated or pressurised (by height).
  - Lift lobby: not required at ground & above below 15 m (but segregation + pressurisation for a lift going to basement); 15–30 m naturally ventilated or pressurised; > 30 m cross-ventilated or pressurised.
  - Natural-ventilation opening per landing **0.5–1.5 m²** in external wall; cross-ventilated stair = 2 such openings in opposite/adjacent walls.
  - No lift lobby possible in AC buildings / flue-effect spaces → **hoistway pressurised at 50 Pa**; > 30 m → multi-point injection.

### 4.5 Compartmentation
- Fire-barrier types: A masonry + fire door; B fire-rated fabric dropdown curtain; C fire-rated glazed partition + door; D fire-rated gypsum/cementitious board; E masonry + fire-rated metal sliding door.
- Fire-curtain integrity (E): withstand up to 1 050 °C, resist flame/hot gas 120 min; radiation ≤ 15 kW/m² at 1 m; insulation ≤ 140 °C rise; smoke leakage ≤ 3 m³/m/h at 25 Pa.
- **Table 6 — Compartmentation (max floor area, m²)** [Cl 4.5.3, p.2784–2786]:

  | Building type | Without sprinkler | Sprinklered |
  |---|---|---|
  | Enclosed basement vehicle parking (most occupancies) | 750 | 5 000 |
  | Basements other than car parking | 750 | 2 000 |
  | Hospitals/nursing homes with sleeping patients | 750 | 2 000 |
  | Custody/care of children/aged incapable of self-preservation | 750 | 1 500 |
  | Mercantile / multistorey mercantile | 750 | not essential |
  | Assembly with/without atrium | 1 500 | not essential |
  | Business occupancy | 750 | 3 000 |
  | Datacentre buildings | (must be sprinklered) | 2 000 |
  | Hotel building | 750 | 3 000 |
  | Industrial < 6.7 m height | 1 500 | 10 000 |
  | Hazardous / high-hazard | (must be sprinklered) | 500 |
  | Warehouses < 5.0 m height | (must be sprinklered) | 5 000 |

- **4.5.3.2 Critical utility rooms → 2 h fire-rated on all sides regardless of size:** electrical room, server room, UPS, battery, lift machine rooms, IT/networking rooms, MDF/IDF/MCR, telecom equipment room, janitor storage, chemical storage, QA/QC labs.
- 4.5.3.3 Guest rooms / corridors / banquets: 60 m² fire-compartmented with 30 min fire doors.
- **4.5.3.6 All live-cooking areas (any fuel — induction, LPG, PNG) in hotels, IT parks, cafeterias, hostels, food courts, malls, commercial kitchens → 120 min fire-rated segregation.**
- 4.5.3.5 Industrial/storage hazardous areas (vehicle charging, ignitable/hazardous/flammable storage) compartmented regardless of size unless ≥ 30 m from any occupied building.
- Large-height spaces (airports, exhibition halls, mall atria, warehouses > 5 m, industrial > 6.7 m, double-height lobbies) exempt from compartmentation.

### 4.6 Smoke control
- **Above ground (4.6.1):** exit-access corridors of guest rooms / non-self-preservation patient areas / sleeping accommodation → **60 min fire wall + 30 min self-closing fire doors** + fire-stop sealing. Smoke exhaust OR pressurisation for these corridors, theatres/auditoria, large lobbies. Exit-passageway doors **120 min**. **Smoke exhaust ≥ 12 ACPH; pressurisation 25–30 Pa; smoke exhaust fans fire-rated 250 °C for 120 min.** Naturally cross-ventilated corridors exempt.
- **Below ground (4.6.2):** each basement separately ventilated; natural vents ≥ **2.5 % of floor area** round the perimeter, OR mechanical **12 ACPH in fire/distress**; multi-level → masonry/RCC intake & exhaust shafts, fans in **120 min** rooms, back-draft dampers; fans fire-rated **250 °C/120 min**; jet-fan alternative allowed with structural coordination + fire-safe power + circuit-integrity cabling; operates on sprinkler flow-switch + manual; supply air ≥ 5 m from exhaust discharge. (Same as [[Part D Sec 3 - Air-Conditioning, Heating and Mechanical Ventilation]] cl 19.1.1.)

### 4.7 Gas supply
- Gas pipes in separate exclusive shafts on external walls away from staircases; distribution pipes always below false ceiling; kept short.
- Thermal detectors in large-kitchen fume hoods (hotels/hospitals/high-rise) → auto-trip exhaust fan + shut gas.
- Seismic shut-off valve at the main distribution point for mixed-use assembly (malls) + institutional above 15 m.
- Buildings above 100 m with LPG → gas-leak detectors at usage points monitored from FCC (circuit-integrity cables).
- **Gas lines not through enclosed staircases, electrical shafts, escape routes, refuge areas. LPG-fuel kitchens not permitted in basements** (induction/PNG/electric OK for incidental basement occupancy).

### 4.8 Hazardous areas
Rooms with high-pressure boilers, refrigerating machinery, transformers, explosion-prone equipment → not directly under or adjacent to fire exits; cut off with vents to outside air; **120 min** rating. Approved outside gas shut-off valve per building.

### 4.9 Fire detection & alarm
- Requirements per occupancy in **Tables 7A–7J** (below).
- Voice evacuation in English, Hindi and vernacular, pre-recorded, integrated with fire alarm panels, alerts the fire zone + surrounding zones/floors.
- Assembly & institutional buildings, and **all buildings above 30 m**: detectors also inside electrical shafts, lift machine rooms.
- Fire alarm panels peer-to-peer or redundant cables in different shafts.
- When automatic fire alarm is provided, monitor: all tank water levels, hydrant & sprinkler pressures per zone, pump ON/OFF status, isolation valves with supervisory switches.

### Tables 7A–7J — Requirements for Firefighting Installations, by occupancy (p. 2791–2843)
Large R (Required) / NR (Not Required) matrices, one sub-table per occupancy group (7A Residential, 7B Educational, 7C Institutional, 7D Assembly, 7E Business, 7F Mercantile, 7G Industrial, 7H Storage, 7J Hazardous), covering: fire extinguisher, first-aid hose reel, down-comer, wet riser, yard hydrant, automatic wet sprinkler, automatic fire detection & alarm + LPG/PNG gas detector, PA & voice evacuation. **Look up the specific occupancy + height/area band by page.** Key rules:
- **Self-certification threshold: buildings up to 500 m² area AND up to 24 m height (both) → self-certification by a certified State-approved building professional is acceptable.**
- Building with a kitchen → min HL-2; sprinklered basement → min CL-3; **EV parking in podium/basement → sprinklers + CL-5**; EV parks get rate-of-rise heat detection, ICE-vehicle parks get CO sensors; buildings > 60 m → hydraulic calcs, multi-level pumping + storage + gravity down-comer, **pumps ≤ 12 bar shut-off**; cluster of ≤ 5 buildings (≥ 9 m apart) → common pumping 2 500 lpm at 10 bar + 2,55,000 litre; water storage added every 45 m of height. Dry riser may replace wet riser where 24×7 municipal yard-hydrant supply meets demand, or where ambient < 4 °C (buildings up to 24 m only).
- Protection-level codes: **HL-1…HL-6** (hose-based, lower tiers) and **CL-1…CL-8** (combined sprinkler + hose, higher tiers).
- **Table 7K — combined protection base calc** (p.2820): sprinkler demand + hose flow + streams + pump capacity + duration (30–120 min) by CL-1 to CL-8. e.g. CL-3 (Low Hazard): 1 892 lpm pump; CL-5 (Moderate-2): 3 785 lpm; CL-8 (High Hazard): 4 731 lpm ×2.
- **Table 7M — hose-only base calc** (p.2821): HL-1 (50 lpm, 379 lpm pump) to HL-6 (670 lpm, 5 677 lpm pump). Pump = electric + standby of equal capacity + jockey.

### 4.10 / 4.11
Qualified Fire Officer recommended on-site for large complexes / high-rise / high-risk. Fire drills & fire orders per State/Local Authority.

## 5 Fire Protection (p. 2822–2839)

### 5.1 Fire extinguishers / fixed installations
- Requirements per Table 7A–7J; base calcs 7K/7M; main/riser sizes **Table 8** (p.2827): **100 mm** (single 63 mm landing valve) for residential/educational/institutional/assembly/business up to 15 m, mercantile/industrial up to 9 m; **150 mm** for hotels/institutional/business above 15 m, industrial/storage/hazardous up to 15 m.
- Valves: supervisory switch to fire alarm panel OR chain/padlock/label/tamper tag; kept 'open'.
- **First-aid hose reel diameter ≥ 19 mm**, connected directly to the riser/down-comer main, all floors where required.
- Wet risers interconnected at terrace level to form a ring, cut-off per connection.
- **Pressure at the hydraulically remote hydrant and at the highest hydrant ≥ 3.5 bar; must not exceed 7.0 bar** (operator safety); orifice plates / pressure-reducing devices at lower levels.
- Hydrants + hose reels in the firefighting-shaft lobby; floor hydrants near a fire-exit staircase **within 5 m of the fire-exit door**; cabinet marked 'FIRE HOSE CABINET' (letters 75 mm × 12 mm), glass panel, need not be fire-rated.

### 5.1.2 Static water storage & pump house
- Underground/terrace static tank per Table 7A–7J; two or more interconnected equal compartments.
- Domestic suction tank fed only via overflow from the fire tank (keeps the fire reserve at minimum).
- **Underground fire water tank ≤ 7 m deep from the fire-brigade draw-out connection level; draw-out connection ≤ 5 m from the tank wall.**
- Tank covering slab (on a fire-apparatus driveway) withstands **45 tonne** vehicular load as a 4-point load.
- Fire-brigade collecting head: **4 × 63 mm dia** instantaneous male inlets (2 × 63 mm for pumps ≤ 1 400 lpm) in a street-level valve box; **≥ 150 mm dia** GI feed pipe, discharge 2 250 lpm if in basement / not fire-engine-accessible. Draw-out head: 63 mm dia via 100 mm GI pipe with foot valve.
- **Pump house (5.1.2.2):** directly accessible from ground level (industrial → ground level); below ground → first basement preferred, never below second basement; access via staircase or **120 min** enclosed passageway from ground, not through other basement occupancies; separated by fire walls all round, **120 min fire doors**; no other utility equipment inside; **no negative-suction arrangement, no submersible pumps**; diesel-pump exhaust insulated to a safe ground-level location; **fire pump provided with a soft starter or VFD starter**.

### 5.1.3 Automatic sprinkler installation
- Per Table 7A–7J and IS 15105 [F(20)].
- **Sprinklers in false-ceiling voids exceeding 800 mm in height.**
- May be excluded in substation and DG-set areas; areas ≥ 17 m high (atria) may omit ceiling sprinklers.
- **Sprinkler system pressure must not exceed 12 bar** (else high-pressure sprinklers).
- **Max floor area on any one floor protected by one sprinkler system riser / installation control valve = 4 500 m²** (except industrial/hazardous — consult Authorities).
- Installation control valves inside the fire pump room. Sprinkler flow switches monitored by the fire alarm panel. Prevent sprinkler/hydrant water entering lifts and electrical rooms. **Ramps at all levels sprinklered.** Not for electronics manufacturing. Sprinkler piping may be colour-matched but with a ~100 mm red band every 3 m; heads never painted. Exposed pendent sprinkler with no false ceiling → ~250 mm dia circular plate around it.

### 5.1.4–5.1.9 Other suppression
- HV/MV water spray for oil-cooled transformers (Annex D); MV spray for tankage, conveyors, cable galleries. Fixed foam for oil/furnace-oil storage; high-expansion foam for cable tunnels. Gas-based where water/foam unsuitable; **CO2 total flooding requires personnel evacuation**. **Hypoxic air technology not for occupiable areas.** Water mist (< 1 000 µm droplets); clean-agent (halon alternatives).

### 5.2 Detection & suppression system types
Descriptive (5.2.1.1–5.2.2.11): pneumatic linear heat, flame/gas/spark (UV 180–260 nm, IR 2–6 µm; spark IR ~0.8–1.1 µm, ≤ 10 ms response), video (VFD), gas detection, fuel leak detection, high-ceiling beam detector, linear heat sensing cable, probe-type heat detector, **aspiration high-sensitivity smoke detection (VESDA-type)**. Suppression: water spray, **wet/dry pre-action (for data centres/archives/museums/cleanrooms)**, modular panel/rack, nitrogen injection (oil transformers), water mist, foam sprinkler (Class B), **wet chemical kitchen (Class K, saponification)**, dry powder flooding, clean-agent total flooding, CO2 flooding, aerosol (1–10 µm).

### 5.4 / 5.5 Tables 9, 10, 11 — special / supplementary protection selection
- **Table 9** (Cl 5.4, p.2833–2835) — S (Suitable) / NS (Not Suitable) matrix of detection & suppression systems for special rooms: UPS rooms (with/without batteries, lithium-ion), server rooms/data halls, live-cooking kitchens, network/electrical rooms, MCC panels, cable cellars/trenches/trays > 1.5 m wide, dry transformers indoor, oil transformers outdoor > 2.0 MVA or 2 000 litre, DG rooms, cold/freezer rooms, MRI rooms, operation theatres, tunnels.
- **Table 10** — automatic detection systems for G–J occupancies. **Table 11** — automatic suppression systems for G–J.

## 6 Specific Occupancy-Wise Requirements (p. 2840–2851)

- **6.1 Residential:** no locking devices impeding exit; bathroom locks openable from outside; lodging houses ≥ 500 m² → 2 exits (1 with direct discharge); dormitory sleeping rooms > 10 persons → 2 separate exit accesses. **Hotels (A-IV): panic bars at 865–1 220 mm from floor; guest rooms/suites protected by extended-throw or quick-response sprinklers + audio detectors ≥ 75 dB.** Flammable-liquid stores → 120 min walls + 'FIRE DOOR — KEEP CLOSED'.
- **6.2 Educational:** no hazardous-material storage; every room > 45 persons → ≥ 2 doorways with panic bars; **pre-school/kindergarten/Grade 1 on ground floor / exit-discharge level; Grade 2 not more than one floor above**; volatile flammable liquids only in science labs.
- **6.3 Institutional (hospitals):**
  - Common path ≤ 30 m; dead-end corridor ≤ 6 m.
  - **Progressive horizontal evacuation** paramount — move from a fire-affected compartment to an adjacent one at the same level through a fire-resistant wall; compartments with self-closing fire doors + electromagnetic hold-open + double-leaf coordinator; corridor door openings ≥ 2.0 m double-swing double-leaf.
  - **Each compartment sized to hold its own + the adjoining compartment's patients: 3.5 m²/person bed-ridden, 0.6 m²/person ambulant.**
  - Critical/non-self-preservation patients preferably within 45 m height; above 45 m → refuge terraces on alternate floors with medical-gas provision, direct firefighting-shaft access via ≥ 2 h fire door, sprinklers + PA + detection + dedicated shafts + emergency power (UPS + DG) + washroom; round-the-clock firefighting team.
  - **OT / delivery rooms / ICU / recovery → 120 min fire/smoke separation.**
  - **Patient-movement aisles/corridors/ramps ≥ 2.4 m throughout; non-patient areas ≥ 1.5 m. All exits from hospital/infirmary sections ≥ 2.0 m wide.**
  - **Patient-room door widths: single/double occupancy ≥ 1.25 m; wards 3–5 beds ≥ 1.50 m; wards > 5 beds or bed-evacuation areas (ICU, recovery, delivery) 2.0 m** (may reduce to 1.5 m if two such doors). Lab rooms ≤ 100 m² (else 120 min separation). A stretcher lift in a lift bank also acts as fireman's lift.
  - Penal/mental institutions: locked doors permitted with reliable rapid-release means.
- **6.4 Assembly:**
  - Decorations non-flammable or flame-retardant treated; stage settings Class 1.
  - **Mixed-use assembly/mercantile (malls): assembly/mercantile portion preferably ≤ 30 m; above 30 m → business/residential with 240 min separation + independent exits.**
  - **Exit door width ≥ 2.0 m.** Theatres/auditoria/studios with fixed seats: **> 1 000 persons → ≥ 4 separate remote exits; 600–1 000 → ≥ 3 exits each ≥ 2.0 m; with fixed seats → ≥ 2 exits.**
  - **Clear aisles ≥ 1.2 m at right angles to seating; no seat > 7 seats from an aisle; rows opening on an aisle one end only ≤ 7 seats; cross-aisle ≥ 1 m wide, ≥ 1 per 10 rows.** No steps in aisles unless gradient > 1 in 10.
  - Box/balcony/gallery fascia railings ≥ 1 000 mm (≥ 1 100 mm at aisle ends, 1.2 m at foot of steps); cross-aisle railings ≥ 900 mm.
  - Seats fastened where assembly > 300 persons. Loose chairs in restaurants/clubs: ≤ 1 seat per 1.4 m²; **seat row spacing back-to-back ≥ 850 mm; ≥ 350 mm clear between back of one seat and front of the next.**
  - **Mall-like (6.4.2.2):** exit passageway ≥ 2.0 m wide; **50 % of common-area lifts = fireman's lift**; break-glass call points; photoluminescent tread markings; exit arrow 175 × 50 mm. **Refuge area on the floor at or immediately above 18 m ≥ 10 % of gross floor area; next above 24 m; each refuge ≥ 100 m²** (or refuge spaces = 20 % of floor occupants).
  - Fire protection: stage with fly galleries → sprinklers over/under stage + proscenium fire curtain (withstand 4 kN/m² lateral, power-free emergency closing); stage roof ventilator ≥ 1/8 of stage floor area; projection booth fire-resisting.
- **6.5 Business:** ≥ 2 exits per floor incl. basements. Labs/clinics/libraries → detection + CO2/foam. IT/telecom/broadcast/ATC → detection + clean agents / mist; electrical panels CO2/inert gas flooding.
- **6.6 Mercantile:** open-air markets/petrol stations — unobstructed travel, no dead-ends. Highly hazardous goods → Group J.
- **6.7 Industrial:**
  - Storage/manufacturing/hazardous (paint store, oil storage, spray booths) separated from non-hazardous by **120 min**; petrochemical/explosives areas **240 min**.
  - **Fire separating wall carried through the roof as a screen wall** — height so the horizontal distance at the top between segregated roofs is ≥ 6 m, minimum **600 mm** above roof; thickness ≥ **230 mm** (masonry extension) / ≥ **150 mm** (RCC extension); not needed if either roof is RCC. Fire separating walls extended outwards **≥ 450 mm** each side. Doors/windows in external walls within 3 m of a fire separating wall → **≥ 60 min** fire doors / fire-resistant glass. Moderate/high-hazard storage → **two fire doors each 180 min**.
  - Life safety: undivided floor area with travel > 45 m to outside walls → exit tunnels / overhead passageways; sprinklered single-storey may allow travel up to **65 m** with smoke venting. High-hazard: ≥ 2 exits in different directions from every point; ≥ 2 escapes per room; slide escapes permitted; automatic sprinkler + explosion venting.
- **6.8 Storage:**
  - Exit within **30 m of any point** in hazardous-commodity storage (35 m if sprinklered).
  - Storage area > **1 400 m² gross** or > 10 persons → ≥ 2 exit-access doors, not lockable while occupied.
  - Aircraft hangars: exits at ≤ 45 m intervals on all exterior walls, ≥ 2 per storage/servicing area; horizontal exits through interior fire walls at ≤ 30 m; mezzanine max travel 22.5 m.
- **6.9 Hazardous:** vapour/flame/ember/spark detectors + explosion suppression; flameproof enclosures for spark/glow devices; wall vents ≥ 100 cm² near floor line ≤ 1 800 mm apart; power exhaust producing a full air change every 3 min; dust-collecting/exhaust systems.

### Annex D — High-rise buildings (p. 2872–2878)

Gravity-fed alternative: each tank serves stories 55–60 m below; top tank +50 % to feed the one below; pump duty head 10 bar / shut-off 12 bar. Seismic bracing per region.
- **D-7 Fire & life safety audit: mandatory for all high-rise buildings, third-party auditor, once every 2 years.**

### Annex E — Atrium (p. 2881)
- **Only Type 1 / Type 2 construction.** Combustible furnishings on the atrium floor limited.
- Beam-type / aspirating smoke detectors under each protruding floor, at the atrium roof, adjacent to each return-air intake.
- Ceiling > 17 m above floor → sprinklers at the atrium ceiling not required. Sprinklers to wet adjoining glass at **450–600 mm** spacing.
- **Smoke extraction: business 6 ACPH, hotels & assembly 8 ACPH, for a height of 15 m from the top.** Fans effective at **250 °C for 120 min**. Makeup air below the smoke layer at **85–95 % of exhaust flow**, velocity **≤ 1.02 m/s** near the plume; emergency power.
- Keep smoke-layer interface **1 800 mm above the highest occupied floor** for 1.5 × egress time or 20 min (greater).

### Annex F — Data Centres (p. 2882–2887)
- Group E, sub-occupancy **E-II**. Fire risk assessment at planning + periodically.
- **No water sources or pipelines routed through or above the data centre / UPS / comms rooms.** Anti-static floor recommended.
- **Basements ≤ 2, within the building line, total area ≤ ground-floor area.**
- **Type 1 construction only; ground coverage ideally ≤ 60 %.** Parking: admin building at **1 ECS per 100 m²**; data-centre/utility parking on actual truck requirement + loading/unloading + fire-truck movement.
- Detection: aspirating smoke detection (VESDA) in server areas + HSSD + heat sensitivity.
- **Suppression: clean agent (heptafluoropropane / fluorinated ketone / equivalent), ozone-friendly, no residue**, throughout data halls including under-floor and overhead; integrated with fire alarm + BMS.
- UPS/transformer/generator rooms → fire-rated partitions + independent suppression; generators may be stacked (≤ 5). Battery rooms 120 min. Fire dampers + smoke detectors in all supply/fresh/return ducts.
- **Data-centre staircases: enclosed, fire-rated, positively pressurised, minimum 1.5 m wide, 2 required.** Admin-building staircases per clause 4 (min 2).
- Seismic bracing for MEP + firefighting per Part C Sec 1; each straight pipe/duct/cable run → min 2 transverse + 1 longitudinal brace.

### Annex G — Car Parking Facilities (p. 2885–2888)
- Parking within/above/below/attached-to/within-3-m of another-use building → separated by **120 min** walls and floors (excludes cashier/attendant/toilet spaces ≤ 200 m² total). **Vehicle ramps not exits** unless pedestrian facilities provided. No fuel dispensing in the building; car repair → 120 min separation.
- **Open parking structure: wall openings ≥ 0.4 m² per linear metre of exterior perimeter, over ≥ 40 % of perimeter or two opposing sides; interior wall lines ≥ 20 % open** (else enclosed). Stilt car park counts as open if any part within 30 m of a permanent natural-ventilation opening AND (50 % of perimeter open, OR ≥ 75 % of perimeter carries the 50 % opening). Open parking → no compartmentation.
- **Enclosed parking:** sprinklers standard-response, **min K-factor 80, 9 m² area coverage**. Smoke ventilation per 4.6. Fire-exit doors painted green + exit signage.
- **Automated / mechanical / robotic parking:** **all basements → sprinklers ≥ K 115 quick-response, ≥ 2 per vehicle, ≤ 7.2 m² coverage each, area of operation ≥ 230 m²** (ICE + electric + hybrid); sprinkler guards. Stack parking where 2 sprinklers can't fit below → K 115 extended-coverage sidewall. **Electric/hybrid cars must not be parked on stack parking at any level.** Puzzle parking without human interface → riser limited to 24 m, extra risers every 15 m. **Fire escape staircases ≥ 1 250 mm wide so no point is more than 45 m from one; horizontal walkways ≥ 1 000 mm at every level.**
- **EV car parking:** best outdoors / separate cross-ventilated building; **preferred at ground level, never below first basement; charging points not below ground (if permitted, only first basement)**, at the entrance/first opportunity of the basement; automated / ground-level isolation cut-off + fireman's emergency switch. Active: water-mist sprinkler + thermal-runaway detection + encapsulating agents. Passive: fire-rated walls (preferred over curtains) + fire doors. **Basement EV parking + charging compartment max size 1 000 m².**

### Annex H — Metro Stations (p. 2888–2921)
- Metro stations = **Assembly occupancy**; **Type 1 or Type 2 construction only**; any Fire Zone.
- **Occupant load** driven by train carrying capacity. Emergency platform load = two headway entraining loads (peak) + one (off-peak) + one crush/link train load. Peak-hour → peak-minute (÷ 60 × surge factor 1.3–1.5). Non-transit occupancy ≤ 300 m².
- **Evacuation time: clear the platform load within 4 min (enclosed) / 5.5 min (open); remotest person to a point of safety within 6 min (enclosed) / 8 min (open).**
- **Max travel distance on the platform to a means-of-egress route ≤ 100 m.**
- **Platforms/corridors/ramps: min clear width 1 200 mm**; deduct 300 mm each side wall + 450 mm at trainway-open edges; capacity 0.0820 people/mm-min; speed 38.0 m/min (60.0 for concourses).
- **Stairways: unenclosed unidirectional ≥ 1 800 mm, bidirectional ≥ 2 400 mm; enclosed ≥ 1 200 mm; capacity 0.0555 people/mm-min, speed 14.6 m/min.**
- **Escalators ≤ half the egress capacity per level; one out of service (worst case); stopped escalator = 1 m staircase; capacity 120 people/min, speed 18.5 m/min.**
- **Lifts as egress:** min 60 min fire resistance (shaft 120 min); ≤ 50 % of required capacity; one out of service + one for fire service; holding lobby ≥ 60 min smoke-tight, 1 person per 0.2 m², pressurised ≥ 25 Pa.
- **Doors/gates:** 60 people/min single-leaf; 0.082 people/mm-min bi-parting. Fare barriers release on power failure / fire alarm / manual, or open under ≤ 66 N.
- H-6 to H-11 (compartmentation, power supply, emergency lighting, fire protection facilities open vs enclosed, station smoke management) — referenced by page.

### Annex J — Metro Trainways (p. 2921–...)
- Passengers enter the trainway only to evacuate a train, under operator control.
- **Cross-passageways: min 1 200 mm clear width, 2 100 mm height.** Exit-stair width need not exceed 1 200 mm for enclosed trainways. Ancillary areas separated from trainway by 120 min. **Tunnel lighting 100 % on emergency (UPS) power.** Directional signs at ≤ 25 m intervals. Referenced by page.

### Annex K — Venting in Industrial Buildings (p. 2913–2919)
- Applies to **single-storey factories/warehouses with large undivided floor areas**.
- **Automatic fire vents required for all industrial/storage occupancies moderate hazard or above with floor area > 750 m².** Sprinklers (where provided) operate before vents. Keep combustion-product temperature preferably below ~150 °C.
- **Total vent area (effective vent opening : floor area):** low heat release (G-1) **1:150**; moderate (G-2) **1:100**; high (G-3) **1:30 to 1:50**.
- **Minimum vent opening dimension ≥ 1.25 m in any direction.**
- **Max spacing between vents:** low **45 m**; moderate **36–37 m**; high **22.5–30 m**.
- **Inlet area for cold air ≥ total vent area**, inlets as low as possible.
- **Roof screens / curtain boards** for undivided areas > 750 m²: non-combustible, gas-tight, extend down **≥ 2.2 m** (4 m around specific hazards; to within 3 m of floor where roof > 15 m). Moderate: screen spacing ≤ 75 m, curtained area ≤ 4 500 m². High: spacing ≤ 30 m, area ≤ 750 m².
- **Explosion relief vents** for combustible-dust / flammable-gas premises (ordinary walls fail below ~6.9 kPa sustained internal pressure); explosion-hazard operations not permitted in basements; doors/windows as vents swing outwards with friction/spring/magnetic latches. Vent-area-to-volume ratios by enclosure size given (K-3.4.16).

### Annex M — Performance Based Design (p. 2919–2922)
- Applies **only to heritage buildings** made public / converted where prescriptive exits would damage heritage nature (other cases need state fire-authority approval). By a registered professional; AHJ makes final determination.
- **Minimum acceptance criteria:** safe egress (**ASET > RSET** via validated evacuation modelling); controlled fire/smoke spread; suppression & detection exceeding this Part's minimums.
- **Tenability criteria at 1.8 m above floor:**
  - **FED (asphyxiant gases) < 0.3; FEC (irritant gases) < 0.3.**
  - **Convective heat: air temperature ≤ 60 °C. Radiant heat: ≤ 2.5 kW/m².**
  - **Visibility through smoke: ≥ 5–10 m.**
