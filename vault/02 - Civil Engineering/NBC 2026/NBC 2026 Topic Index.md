# NBC 2026 Topic Index (cross-cutting)

Companion to [[NBC 2026]]. That note indexes the code **by Part/Section**; this one indexes it **by topic** — for questions like "what does the code say about staircases?" where the answer is spread across structural, services and fire sections.

Each entry lists the notes that touch the topic and, in one line, what each contributes. The **master** note (bold) is where the primary requirement lives; the others add loads, ratings, services coordination, or fire provisions. Always open the master note first, then follow the page anchor into the PDF for the exact number.

Built 2026-09-05 from a full read of all 21 section notes. Sections still marked "drafted" in [[NBC 2026]] were read for topic coverage but not line-audited — verify any number from a drafted section against the PDF.

## Staircases

- **[[Part F - Fire and Life Safety]]** — the geometry and protection master: tread ≥ 250 mm (residential) / 300 mm (other); riser ≤ 150 mm (≤ 175 mm one/two-family); max 15 risers per flight; minimum width by occupancy (1.0 m dwelling → 2.0 m assembly/institutional); headroom 2.2 m; internal stairs ≥ 120 min, non-combustible, nothing else in the shaft; external staircase (≤ 45°, ≥ 1 500 mm, 60 min openings within 3 m); spiral/curved limits; pressurisation 50 Pa; at least 2 staircases for every high-rise; firefighting-shaft stairs.
- **[[Part C Sec 1 - Loads and Load Effects]]** — imposed load on stairs/landings/fire escapes 3.0–4.0 kN/m² (Table 1); horizontal load on stair handrails/balustrades 0.35–3.0 kN/m run (Table 3).
- **[[Part C Sec 8 - Glass and Glazing]]** — glass balustrades/railings mandatory laminated safety glass (Table 27 Case 5); barrier heights (stairs/landings 900 mm, Table 35); horizontal railing loads (Table 36); glazing within 1.5 m of floor = safety glazing.
- **[[Part C Sec 4 - Masonry]]** — earthquake detailing: sliding joints where stairs meet floors (so the stair doesn't act as unintended bracing); separated / built-in / sliding-joint stair types (Figs 18–19).
- **[[Part C Sec 5 - Structural Concrete]]** — tall-building stairs built integral with the structure and not confined by walls must be in the 3D model and designed for all load combinations, or given a sliding joint (E-8.1.2).
- **[[Part C Sec 7 - Prefabricated Concrete Construction]]** — precast staircase/landing: design per Sec 5, connections per clause 8 (7.9.7).
- **[[Part D Sec 1 - Lighting and Natural Ventilation]]** — stair illuminance 100–150 lux (Table 6); tread/step-strip lighting for egress; uniform lighting across bright/dim transitions.
- **[[Part A - Introduction]]** — listed only as a building part; sets no dimensions.

## Occupancy classification

- **[[Part F - Fire and Life Safety]]** — the definitive scheme: Groups A–K (A Residential, B Educational, C Institutional, D Assembly, E Business, F Mercantile, G Industrial, H Storage, J Hazardous, K Mixed) with subdivisions; minor-occupancy 30 % rule; mixed-occupancy separation matrix; occupant-load factors (Table 2).
- **[[Part A - Introduction]]** — the short list (Residential, Educational, Institutional, Assembly, Business, Mercantile, Industrial, Storage, Hazardous) plus Type 1–4 construction.
- **[[Part C Sec 1 - Loads and Load Effects]]** — imposed floor loads keyed to the same occupancy classes (Table 1).
- **[[Part E Sec 1 - Water Supply]]** / **[[Part E Sec 2 - Drainage and Sanitation]]** — water demand (Table 1, E1) and sanitary-fixture counts (Tables 1–17, E2) by occupancy.
- **[[Part D Sec 6 - Information and Communication Enabled Installations]]** — Annex C carries its own occupancy list (Groups A–J) for the connectivity template only — not the general scheme.

## Seismic / earthquake design

- **[[Part C Sec 1 - Loads and Load Effects]]** — the entry point: does not give the seismic method itself, refers to IS 1893 (Part 1) and to the material sections; load combinations, overturning/sliding factors; multi-hazard district lists (Annex M).
- **[[Part C Sec 4 - Masonry]]** — the four earthquake-resisting masonry systems (MWB/MWBR/CMW/RMW), building categories B–E, band/reinforcement tables, staircase sliding joints.
- **[[Part C Sec 5 - Structural Concrete]]** — IS 13920 ductile detailing; tall-building Annex E (base-shear coefficients, drift ≤ 1/500, cracked-section properties, near-fault exclusion).
- **[[Part C Sec 6A - Steel]]** — ordinary vs special frames; Zone III+ requires special frames; R-factors; critical-member combos 1.2DL+0.5IL±2.5E.
- **[[Part C Sec 6B - Composite Construction]]** / **[[Part C Sec 7 - Prefabricated Concrete Construction]]** — seismic elongation/ductility rules; OMRF not permitted Zone III+; Zone IV+ precast needs institute approval.
- **[[Part C Sec 2 - Soils and Foundations]]** — liquefaction susceptibility in the investigation report; foundation performance.
- **[[Part C Sec 8 - Glass and Glazing]]** — seismic design force on glazing (secondary to acceleration); inter-storey drift racking test (Zones IV/V), default drift H/500.
- **[[Part D Sec 3 - Air-Conditioning, Heating and Mechanical Ventilation]]** — seismic restraint of non-structural MEP; life-safety and critical equipment keep functioning; 2 transverse + 1 longitudinal brace per run.
- **[[Part D Sec 5 - Lifts, Escalators, Moving Walks and Power-Driven Parking]]** — lifts: protective measures where design acceleration a_d > 1 m/s² (a_d from the structural designer); escalators: movable-support compensation length = maximum storey drift.
- **[[Part D Sec 2 - Electrical and Allied Installations]]** — seismic considerations for substation siting.
- **[[Part F - Fire and Life Safety]]** — seismic bracing of firefighting risers/pumps/tanks; seismic gas shut-off valve for malls + institutional > 15 m.

## Wind load

- **[[Part C Sec 1 - Loads and Load Effects]]** — the master: design wind speed Vz = Vb·k1·k2·k3·k4, design pressure pd = Kd·Ka·Kc·pz; basic wind speed map + city list (Annex D, 33–55 m/s); pressure/force coefficient tables 7–38; gust-factor dynamic method; interference effects.
- **[[Part C Sec 8 - Glass and Glazing]]** — glass thickness/area/span selection charts by design wind pressure (Tables 17–26); empirical relation Pnet·A = 200Tk; open-out window dynamic wind restraint > 15 m.
- **[[Part C Sec 3B - Bamboo]]** — cyclone/high-wind construction recommendations (Annex B): square plan, hip roofs, overhangs ≤ 450 mm, continuous collar beams.
- **[[Part D Sec 3 - Air-Conditioning, Heating and Mechanical Ventilation]]** — tall-building facade solar-gain and air-leakage limits; loads in ~100 m sections.
- **[[Part D Sec 5 - Lifts, Escalators, Moving Walks and Power-Driven Parking]]** — super-tall lifts (> 200 m): piston effect (common shaft + vents, or +25–30 % standalone shaft), stack effect on landing doors, wind-induced building sway and rope resonance.

## Fire resistance ratings

- **[[Part F - Fire and Life Safety]]** — the master: R (stability) + E (integrity) + I (insulation, ≤ 180 °C) definition; Table 1 element ratings by construction type; exits/shafts/hoistways 120 min; compartmentation (Table 6); component data (Annex C Tables 13–29); ACP facade restriction.
- **[[Part C Sec 6A - Steel]]** — Period of Structural Adequacy ≥ required Fire Resistance Level; protected/unprotected member methods; no yield-stress reduction below 215 °C; FRL comes from Part F.
- **[[Part C Sec 5 - Structural Concrete]]** — concrete cover to reinforcement for fire (Annex C referenced); SFRC residual strength vs temperature.
- **[[Part C Sec 3A - Timber]]** — CLT charring rate 0.65 mm/min; gypsum encapsulation ≥ 13 mm; steel connectors need fire protection.
- **[[Part C Sec 4 - Masonry]]** — masonry wall fire ratings referenced to Part F / good practice.
- **[[Part C Sec 8 - Glass and Glazing]]** — fire-resistant glass types (wired, ceramic, intumescent-interlayer); rating criteria per Part F.
- **[[Part C Sec 7 - Prefabricated Concrete Construction]]** — precast joint fire rules (> 50 mm joints follow standard provisions; structural-wall joints ≤ 50 mm need grout ≥ 50 MPa).
- **[[Part B - Building Materials]]** — fire safety is a mandatory acceptance criterion for any new/alternative material (clause 4.1).

## Lifts & vertical transportation

- **[[Part D Sec 5 - Lifts, Escalators, Moving Walks and Power-Driven Parking]]** — the master (Subsection 5A): traffic analysis, car/shaft/pit/machine-room dimensions, civil & electrical, fireman's & evacuation lifts, safety devices, super-tall provisions, IS 17900 standards; 5B escalators; 5C power-driven parking.
- **[[Part F - Fire and Life Safety]]** — fireman's lift and evacuation lift (Annex D); lifts/escalators are NOT fire exits; lift-lobby pressurisation 25–30 Pa; hoistway pressurised at 50 Pa where no lobby; lift as egress in metro stations (Annex H).
- **[[Part D Sec 3 - Air-Conditioning, Heating and Mechanical Ventilation]]** — hoistway/lift-lobby pressurisation (clause 19); lift-car air-conditioning is in Sec 5, but machine-room cooling coordination sits here.
- **[[Part D Sec 2 - Electrical and Allied Installations]]** — lift electrical supply is a 100 % cumulative load (no diversity); emergency power to fireman's lifts; no meter in staircase passage.
- **[[Part D Sec 1 - Lighting and Natural Ventilation]]** — lift car air changes ~20/h; lift and lift-lobby illuminance (Table 6).
- **[[Part E Sec 3 - Solid Waste Management]]** — dumbwaiter / service lift for garbage in buildings > 5 storeys.
- **[[Part A - Introduction]]** — "lift specialist" and "utility services engineer" as registered professionals.

## Basements

- **[[Part F - Fire and Life Safety]]** — minimum clear height 3.4 m; never fewer than 2 independent basement exits; smoke ventilation (natural ≥ 2.5 % of floor area, or mechanical 12 ACPH in fire); compartmentation (Table 6); no sleeping/classrooms/LPG kitchens/battery rooms; EV parking + charging compartment ≤ 1 000 m² (Annex G).
- **[[Part C Sec 2 - Soils and Foundations]]** — foundation depth, dewatering, excavation protection and shoring.
- **[[Part C Sec 4 - Masonry]]** — basement/plinth external wall minimum thickness (Table 4), brick strength ≥ 5 N/mm², mortar ≥ M1.
- **[[Part D Sec 2 - Electrical and Allied Installations]]** — substation only in 1st/2nd basement (≤ 10 m below grade), oil-filled equipment ground floor / 1st basement only; DG not below 1st basement; EV charging up to 1st basement only.
- **[[Part D Sec 3 - Air-Conditioning, Heating and Mechanical Ventilation]]** — enclosed basement car park ≥ 6 ACH normal / 12 ACPH in fire; CO limits (≤ 35 ppm 1 h); CO-sensor coverage.
- **[[Part E Sec 1 - Water Supply]]** — underground tank sizing; **[[Part E Sec 4 - Gas Supply]]** — LPG cylinders not in basements/cellars, PNG PE pipe not through basements.

## Car parking

- **[[Part F - Fire and Life Safety]]** — Annex G is the master: 120 min separation from other uses; open-parking-structure definition (openings ≥ 0.4 m²/linear m over ≥ 40 % of perimeter); enclosed parking sprinklers K80; automated/robotic parking K115 quick-response; EV parking rules; vehicle ramps are not exits.
- **[[Part D Sec 5 - Lifts, Escalators, Moving Walks and Power-Driven Parking]]** — Subsection 5C: stack/puzzle/tower/robotic/rotary systems, design loads (2 000–3 500 kg, wheelbase 2.90 m), space widths (2.7/2.9 m), ramp slope ≤ 15 % (≤ 10 % public), CO limits, exhaust rates, > 20 spaces → sprinklers.
- **[[Part D Sec 3 - Air-Conditioning, Heating and Mechanical Ventilation]]** — clauses 14.3 and 19.1: ventilation rates, CO monitoring, smoke exhaust 250 °C/120 min fans, open vs enclosed structure definition.
- **[[Part C Sec 1 - Loads and Load Effects]]** — imposed loads for garages (2.5–5.0 kN/m²); vehicle-barrier force (default 150 kN); vehicle-impact accidental load model.
- **[[Part D Sec 2 - Electrical and Allied Installations]]** — EV charging (20 % EV-ready target, power levels, 30 mA RCD, no charging below 1st basement).
- **[[Part E Sec 2 - Drainage and Sanitation]]** — parking-floor drainage.
- Occupant load: 30 m²/person ([[Part F - Fire and Life Safety]] Table 2).

## Accessibility (persons with disabilities & the elderly)

Common external standard: **IS 4963:2025** (accessibility in the built environment for older adults and persons with disabilities). Cited by:

- **[[Part A - Introduction]]** — flagged as essential in specified public buildings and low-income housing.
- **[[Part D Sec 1 - Lighting and Natural Ventilation]]** — barrier-free lighting/ventilation for the elderly and disabled; accessible switch/socket heights.
- **[[Part D Sec 5 - Lifts, Escalators, Moving Walks and Power-Driven Parking]]** — public lifts per IS 4963; lifts for persons with disabilities per IS 15330:2020; escalators are NOT an accessible route (a lift must be provided); accessible parking bay 3 900 × 5 400 mm (5C).
- **[[Part E Sec 1 - Water Supply]]** / **[[Part E Sec 2 - Drainage and Sanitation]]** — accessible fixtures, grab bars, WC compartment sizes; low-mounted fixtures 1–10 % in high-footfall public areas.
- **[[Part F - Fire and Life Safety]]** — assisted evacuation via the fireman's/evacuation lift; refuge areas sized with wheelchair spaces (0.9 m² per 200 occupants); firefighting-shaft lobby holds ≥ 2 wheelchairs.
- **[[Part C Sec 8 - Glass and Glazing]]** — railing/barrier design for level changes.

## Ventilation (all kinds)

- **[[Part D Sec 1 - Lighting and Natural Ventilation]]** — natural ventilation design (wind- and stack-driven), minimum air changes per hour by room type, opening sizing, thermal comfort indices.
- **[[Part D Sec 3 - Air-Conditioning, Heating and Mechanical Ventilation]]** — mechanical: fresh-air/outdoor-air rates (Table 7), car-park / kitchen / lab / cleanroom / healthcare ventilation, fan energy index.
- **[[Part F - Fire and Life Safety]]** — smoke ventilation (12 ACPH basements & exit corridors, 6/8 ACPH atria); pressurisation; 250 °C/120 min fans.
- **[[Part D Sec 5 - Lifts, Escalators, Moving Walks and Power-Driven Parking]]** — lift-car air changes, escalator machinery-space ventilation, power-driven parking ventilation (0.15 m²/space fully automatic).
- **[[Part E Sec 4 - Gas Supply]]** — gas-riser-duct natural ventilation (mechanical not accepted); LPG cylinder room floor-level venting.
- **[[Part C Sec 2 - Soils and Foundations]]** — Li-ion battery storage room 18 air changes/hour.

## Electrical installations, earthing & RCD selection

- **[[Part D Sec 2 - Electrical and Allied Installations]]** — the master: wiring, substations, TN/TT/IT earthing, 30/100/300 mA RCD rules by building type, lightning protection (IEC 62305), EV charging, solar PV.
- **[[Part D Sec 3 - Air-Conditioning, Heating and Mechanical Ventilation]]** — Type F RCD for 1-phase VFD, Type B for 3-phase VFD; motor efficiency IE3+.
- **[[Part D Sec 5 - Lifts, Escalators, Moving Walks and Power-Driven Parking]]** — lift supply 415 V + 230 V; main switchgear must not cut ARD/alarm/car light; escalator MCB 'D'/'C', RCD 300/100/30 mA, earth conductor ≥ 10 mm².
- **[[Part F - Fire and Life Safety]]** — emergency/standby power list; substation & transformer fire ratings (120/240 min; > 10 MVA water spray); lightning down-conductors not through service shafts.
- **[[Part D Sec 6 - Information and Communication Enabled Installations]]** — telecom bonding busbars, TBB sizing, meshed bonding networks.
- **[[Part C Sec 1 - Loads and Load Effects]]** — impact allowance for lift/hoist frames (100 %) and machinery supports.

## Loads (dead, imposed, combinations, foundations bearing)

- **[[Part C Sec 1 - Loads and Load Effects]]** — the master for every load type and every combination; unit weights (Annex A); blast (Annex L).
- Every structural section ([[Part C Sec 4 - Masonry]], [[Part C Sec 5 - Structural Concrete]], [[Part C Sec 6A - Steel]], [[Part C Sec 6B - Composite Construction]], [[Part C Sec 7 - Prefabricated Concrete Construction]], [[Part C Sec 3A - Timber]], [[Part C Sec 3B - Bamboo]]) cites Sec 1 for loads and applies its own material resistance side.
- **[[Part C Sec 2 - Soils and Foundations]]** — allowable bearing pressure, settlement limits (Table 3), pile capacity; permissible pressure increase under wind/seismic per Sec 1.
- **[[Part D Sec 3 - Air-Conditioning, Heating and Mechanical Ventilation]]** — plant/equipment foundation loads, inertia bases, duct-support design per Sec 1; openings in structure need structural-engineer approval.
- **[[Part D Sec 5 - Lifts, Escalators, Moving Walks and Power-Driven Parking]]** — machine-room floor 10 kN/m²; pit buffer-support forces 4g(P+Q); overhead beam factor of safety.
- **[[Part D Sec 6 - Information and Communication Enabled Installations]]** — street-furniture/pole load-bearing (Annex B) — no explicit Part C cross-reference, engineer must add it.

## Refuge areas & refuge spaces

- **[[Part F - Fire and Life Safety]]** — the master (Annex D): **refuge area** in buildings > 24 m — 0.3 m²/person for two floors' occupants + wheelchair space, min 15 m², at 24 m then every 15 m, on the periphery, open to air, fully sprinklered; **refuge space** in the firefighting-shaft lift lobby — 0.45 m²/person, min 12 m² or 5 % of floor occupants; hospital refuge terraces above 45 m; mall refuge ≥ 10 % of gross floor area.
- **[[Part C Sec 1 - Loads and Load Effects]]** — refuge area / disaster shelter imposed load 3.0 kN/m² (Table 1).
- **[[Part D Sec 5 - Lifts, Escalators, Moving Walks and Power-Driven Parking]]** — note the name clash: the lift's **pit refuge space** and **car-top refuge space** (Table 12/13, 5A) are maintenance-safety volumes inside the lift well, unrelated to the building's fire refuge area.

## Foundations

- **[[Part C Sec 2 - Soils and Foundations]]** — the master: site investigation, shallow/deep/CPRF foundations, settlement limits, pile design, ground improvement, instrumentation.
- **[[Part C Sec 5 - Structural Concrete]]** — Annex E tall-building foundations (embedment 1/15–1/20 of height, SSI study > 150 m, max settlement 125 mm raft / 50 mm rock).
- **[[Part C Sec 1 - Loads and Load Effects]]** — loads and combinations for bearing-pressure checks; permissible increase under wind/seismic.
- **[[Part C Sec 3B - Bamboo]]** — bamboo-on-concrete footing details (Figs 8–9).
- **[[Part D Sec 2 - Electrical and Allied Installations]]** — foundation/column rebar as natural lightning earth electrode (coordinate before construction).
- **[[Part D Sec 3 - Air-Conditioning, Heating and Mechanical Ventilation]]** — equipment foundations, inertia blocks, seismic restraints (dynamic loading per IS 13301).

## Noise & vibration

- **[[Part D Sec 4 - Acoustics, Sound Insulation and Noise Control]]** — the master: acceptable indoor levels (Table 4), sound insulation Rw by occupancy, reverberation times, noise barriers, construction noise.
- **[[Part D Sec 3 - Air-Conditioning, Heating and Mechanical Ventilation]]** — HVAC noise control (Annex J: duct velocity ≤ 7.5 m/s near critical areas, Room Criteria method); acoustic comfort classes (Table 1).
- **[[Part D Sec 5 - Lifts, Escalators, Moving Walks and Power-Driven Parking]]** — lift ride-quality measurement (IS 17615, at the car geometric centre); escalator noise at 1.55 m above the step; quiet lift operation (references Sec 4).
- **[[Part D Sec 2 - Electrical and Allied Installations]]** — DG-set and transformer acoustic enclosures.
- **[[Part C Sec 1 - Loads and Load Effects]]** — vibration serviceability sources and verification (Annex K); machinery impact allowances.

## Glass, glazing & facades

- **[[Part C Sec 8 - Glass and Glazing]]** — the master: type selection, thickness design against wind/seismic, human-impact safety, balustrades/railings, security (bullet/blast) glazing.
- **[[Part F - Fire and Life Safety]]** — facade fire: 9 m fire-separation rule, ≥ 900 mm 1 h spandrel where < 9 m, sprinklers within 600 mm of the facade, floor-slab-to-facade gap sealing, openable fire-access panels (≤ 10 m apart, ≥ 1 000 × 1 000 mm), glass not permitted for exit enclosures, ACP facade avoided.
- **[[Part D Sec 3 - Air-Conditioning, Heating and Mechanical Ventilation]]** — tall-building facade solar gain limited to 10–20 % of incident radiation; completed-building air-leakage ≤ 2.0 l/s·m² at 75 Pa.
- **[[Part D Sec 1 - Lighting and Natural Ventilation]]** — daylighting, glazing correction factors to sky component, window-wall ratio guidance.

## Means of egress & travel distance

- **[[Part F - Fire and Life Safety]]** — the master: means of egress = exit access + exit + exit discharge; travel distance (Table 4, 30 m unsprinklered to 60–90 m sprinklered by occupancy); common path ≤ 50 %; dead-end ≤ 6 m / 15 m; exit-width unit 500 mm, capacity factors (Table 3); exit separation (diagonal ÷ 2 or ÷ 3); door widths (≥ 1 000 mm, ≥ 2 000 mm assembly).
- **[[Part D Sec 5 - Lifts, Escalators, Moving Walks and Power-Driven Parking]]** — power-driven parking: ≥ 2 independent escape routes per floor, 20 lux, 1 h emergency lighting (5C).
- **[[Part F - Fire and Life Safety]]** — metro-station egress (Annex H: platform cleared in 4/5.5 min, ≤ 100 m platform travel, stair/escalator/lift capacities); industrial travel (45 m, 65 m with venting).
- **[[Part D Sec 3 - Air-Conditioning, Heating and Mechanical Ventilation]]** — smoke control of exit corridors and passageways (clause 19.3).

## Water storage tanks

- **[[Part E Sec 1 - Water Supply]]** — the master for domestic: UGT 1.5 days + OHT 0.5 day (apartments/hotels/hospitals); hydro-pneumatic UGT 2 days; OHT clearance ≥ 2 m above highest fixture; tanks > 5 000 L need vents; annual cleaning.
- **[[Part F - Fire and Life Safety]]** — fire water storage is **additional** to domestic; underground fire tank ≤ 7 m deep, draw-out ≤ 5 m from wall, covering slab takes 45 t; static tank in ≥ 2 interconnected compartments; storage added every 45 m of height.
- **[[Part D Sec 3 - Air-Conditioning, Heating and Mechanical Ventilation]]** — cooling-tower make-up tank separate from drinking water; district cooling uses treated sewage effluent for make-up.
- **[[Part C Sec 1 - Loads and Load Effects]]** — water-tower / tank imposed loads and settlement limits.
- No common wall/slab between a water tank and a lift well, lift pit, or machine room ([[Part D Sec 5 - Lifts, Escalators, Moving Walks and Power-Driven Parking]] 5A; [[Part F - Fire and Life Safety]]).

## Emergency & standby power (DG sets)

- **[[Part D Sec 2 - Electrical and Allied Installations]]** — DG room location (ground floor / 1st basement only), room height, day-tank limits, stacking rules, diversity (lifts 100 %).
- **[[Part F - Fire and Life Safety]]** — the emergency-power load list (fire pumps, pressurisation, smoke venting, all fireman's lifts, exit signage, alarm, PA, FCC); DG room 120 min; changeover; fire-survival cabling.
- **[[Part D Sec 3 - Air-Conditioning, Heating and Mechanical Ventilation]]** — data-centre N+1 / 2N power, two independent feeds; generators may be stacked ≤ 5.
- **[[Part D Sec 5 - Lifts, Escalators, Moving Walks and Power-Driven Parking]]** — fireman's-lift backup via automatic changeover; ARD optional where a backup generator with AMF panel exists.

## Sustainability, LCA & embodied carbon

- **[[Part B - Building Materials]]** — sustainable material selection (reuse, recyclable, renewable, low embodied energy); recycled aggregates, RAP, industrial by-products.
- **[[Part C Sec 3A - Timber]]** / **[[Part C Sec 3B - Bamboo]]** — mandatory life-cycle assessment (GWP, acidification, eutrophication, ozone depletion, smog); carbon sequestration of mass timber.
- **[[Part C Sec 5 - Structural Concrete]]** / **[[Part C Sec 7 - Prefabricated Concrete Construction]]** — SCM caps (fly ash, GGBS, silica fume), geopolymer concrete, factory renewable energy.
- **[[Part D Sec 3 - Air-Conditioning, Heating and Mechanical Ventilation]]** — energy efficiency (FEI, IE3+ motors), district cooling, regenerative/heat-recovery systems; envelope numbers via ECSBC 2024.
- **[[Part A - Introduction]]** — BIM adoption; planned decommissioning/deconstruction.
- Common external references: "Handbook on Sustainability in Buildings" (BIS) and the Energy Conservation and Sustainability Building Code (ECSBC 2024) — both outside this NBCS.

## Cold storage & refrigeration

- **[[Part D Sec 3 - Air-Conditioning, Heating and Mechanical Ventilation]]** — the master (clause 13.2): storage-temperature ranges by product, insulation per IS 661, ammonia flammability/toxicity hazards, emergency ventilation, internal-release door hardware, alarms; supermarket refrigeration (13.3).
- **[[Part E Sec 2 - Drainage and Sanitation]]** — every 10 °C temperature drop doubles septic-tank capacity (cold-region design).
- **[[Part F - Fire and Life Safety]]** — cold/freezer room detection & suppression suitability (Table 9); Storage occupancy (H).
- **[[Part C Sec 1 - Loads and Load Effects]]** — low-temperature effects on materials; storage imposed loads.

## Blast & explosion resistance

- **[[Part C Sec 1 - Loads and Load Effects]]** — Annex L: blast-resistant structural design (TNT equivalence, importance categories, SDOF/MDOF, scaled distance) — only where a credible threat is determined; explosion relief in closed rooms (clause 8.6.2).
- **[[Part C Sec 8 - Glass and Glazing]]** — blast threat levels 1–6 (Table 34), blast-resistant glazing tested only by GoI-accredited labs (Annex G).
- **[[Part F - Fire and Life Safety]]** — explosion venting for combustible-dust / flammable-gas premises (Annex K); Hazardous occupancy (J) wall vents and power exhaust.
- **[[Part E Sec 4 - Gas Supply]]** — LPG/PNG installation safety, cylinder-room construction, bulk-storage safety distances.

## Building services / MEP–structure coordination

- **[[Part D Sec 3 - Air-Conditioning, Heating and Mechanical Ventilation]]** — openings in beams/slabs only with the structural engineer's approval; equipment foundations designed structurally; vibration isolation and seismic restraint of non-structural components.
- **[[Part F - Fire and Life Safety]]** — service shafts ≥ 120 min; fire-stopping at every penetration; ducts not through exits; separate AHU per floor.
- **[[Part C Sec 7 - Prefabricated Concrete Construction]]** — coordination of MEP penetrations, PPVC utility connections → Part D Sec 2 / Part E Sec 1 & 2.
- **[[Part D Sec 2 - Electrical and Allied Installations]]** — rebar as earth electrode coordinated with the structural engineer before construction; busbar trunking for floor distribution.
- **[[Part A - Introduction]]** — the multi-disciplinary team (23 roles) and BIM as the coordination vehicle.

## How to use this index

1. Find the topic. If it's not listed, check [[NBC 2026]]'s Part/Section table — the topic may sit wholly inside one section.

3. Open the supporting notes for loads, ratings and services coordination.
4. Follow the page anchor into `NBC 2026- Vol-1 & 2.pdf` (or the clean 5A extract) and read the actual clause before using any number for design.
