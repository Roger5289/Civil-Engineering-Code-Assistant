# Part B — Building Materials

Scope: governing rules for which materials are acceptable in construction, how new/alternative/recycled materials get approved, and a category-by-category index of the Indian Standards (IS) that specify each material. PDF pages 55–154.

## Clauses

**1 Scope** (p.59) — This Part covers requirements for building materials/components, and the criteria for accepting new or alternative materials/components.

**2 Materials** (p.59) — Every material used must conform to the relevant IS standard listed in the "accepted standards" list at the end of this Part, unless otherwise specified/approved. Standards are subject to revision; the Authority should apply the latest version at time of enforcement.

**3 Sustainable Materials** (p.59) — Encourages sustainable material choice (reuse of existing buildings/components, materials that are recyclable, made from renewable inputs, low embodied energy). References the BIS Handbook on "Sustainability in Buildings" for selection guidance. Ties into used materials (cl.6) and recycled/waste materials (cl.7).

**4 New or Alternative Materials** (p.59–60) — A material not covered by an IS standard can still be used if the Authority (or its appointed agency) approves it. Approval must weigh: applicability to geo-climatic conditions, general appearance, dimensional stability, structural stability/strength, **fire safety**, durability, thermal/mechanical/acoustical/optical properties, biological effect, environmental aspects, working characteristics, ease of handling, workability, UV resistance, toxicity. Examples given: decorative/polymer concrete, plastic-waste bricks, recycled plastic lumber, dry-mix mortar, non-shrink grout, blast-resistant materials, nanotech materials, pervious concrete, ACP/zinc composite panels, insulated steel water tanks, etc. Written approval (cl.4.2) must be obtained by the owner/agent before use, based on tests (cl.9.1) or submitted evidence, at the owner's expense. A material stops being "new/alternative" once an IS standard is published for it.

**5 Third Party Certification** (p.60) — Government third-party certification schemes may be used to verify conformity of both standard and new/alternative materials.

**6 Used Materials** (p.60) — Used/recycled materials are allowed provided they meet the same requirements as new materials.

**7 Recycled Materials and Waste Materials** (p.60–61) — Defines recycled building material (any product/material previously used elsewhere). Specifically covers:
- **7.1.1 Recycled Mixed Aggregate (RMA) / Recycled Concrete Aggregate (RCA)** — from construction & demolition (C&D) waste; RCA includes original aggregate plus adhered hydrated cement paste.
- **7.1.2 Reclaimed Asphalt Pavement (RAP) aggregates** — from milling/demolition of bituminous pavements, 2–7.5% bitumen by mass; coarser RAP fraction usable as 10–20% replacement of natural aggregate in non-structural lean-concrete (paver blocks, kerb stones) — strength should be evaluated before use.
- Lists other recycled/waste-derived materials usable in construction: fly ash, bottom ash, red mud, FGD/phospho/fluoro-gypsum, blast furnace slag, LD slag, textile sludge, marble/granite waste, mine/copper/iron tailings, rice husk ash, jarosite, broken glass/ceramics, kiln dust, galvanizing/tannery waste, lime sludge, vermiculite, perlite, bagasse/agro-waste.

**8 Storage of Materials** (p.61) — Materials must be stored on site to prevent deterioration/loss of properties; see the BIS Handbook on "Construction Management, Practices and Safety in Buildings."

**9 Methods of Test** (p.61) — Tests follow the relevant IS test method; where none exists, the Authority/its agent's method applies. Manufacturer/supplier must certify conformity on request (cl.9.1.1); if no certificate, the material itself is tested by a lab acceptable to the Authority.

### Commonly-used standards (from categories read in full, p.63–89)

- **Bricks/blocks (cat.3, p.69–70):** IS 1077 (common burnt clay bricks), IS 2185 Pt.1–4 (concrete masonry/hollow/lightweight/aerated blocks), IS 2691 (facing bricks), IS 3495 Pt.1–6 (brick test methods — compressive strength, water absorption, efflorescence, warpage), IS 15658 (concrete paving blocks).
- **Cement (cat.8b, p.78–79):** IS 269 (OPC), IS 455 (PSC), IS 1489 Pt.1/2 (PPC — flyash/calcined clay based), IS 8041 (rapid hardening), IS 12330 (sulphate resisting), IS 456 (plain & reinforced concrete code of practice), IS 1343 (prestressed concrete code).
- **Aggregates (cat.8a, p.77–78):** IS 383 (coarse/fine aggregate spec), IS 2386 Pt.1–8 (aggregate test methods).
- **Steel reinforcement (cat.8h, p.83–84):** IS 432 Pt.1/2 (mild/medium tensile bars, HD wire), IS 1786 (HYSD/TMT bars), IS 2090 (high tensile prestressing bars), IS 6006 (uncoated stress-relieved strand), IS 16172 (mechanical rebar couplers).
- **Concrete testing (cat.8f, p.80–82):** IS 516 (hardened concrete strength/NDT — UPV, rebound hammer, half-cell potential, carbonation depth), IS 1199 Pt.1–7 (fresh concrete sampling/testing), IS 5816 (splitting tensile strength).
- **Waterproofing compounds (cat.5b, p.72):** IS 2645 (integral waterproofing compound), IS 9103 (concrete admixtures).
