# Part E, Section 2 — Drainage and Sanitation

Covers design, layout, construction and maintenance of building drainage (foul, surface, subsoil), sanitary fixture counts by occupancy, sewage/rainwater/subsoil systems, manholes, and sewage treatment. Based on IS 1742, IS 4111, IS 5329, IS 6295. PDF pages 2571–2684.

## 1 Scope (p. 2575)
Covers drains for foul water, surface water, subsoil water and sewage, from building to public/private sewer, individual disposal system, cesspool or soakaway — including drainage peculiar to high altitude/sub-zero regions.

## 2 Terminology (p. 2575–2579)
~107 defined terms (Air Admittance Valve, Cesspool, Drainage Fixture Unit (DFU), Grease Interceptor, Sullage/Grey Water, Trap, Vent Stack, Water Seal, Yoke Vent, etc.) — full glossary at cited pages, not reproduced term-by-term here; look up by name if needed.

## 3 General — Basic Principles (p. 2584–2588)
- **3.1.2** Minimum residual water pressure at consumer's tap: **0.018 N/mm²** (some fixtures need up to 0.05 N/mm² or higher — use pumps/tanks).
- **3.1.5 / 4.2.2** Minimum amenities per dwelling: at least 1 WC, 1 kitchen sink/wash place, 1 bathing place/shower.
- **3.1.16** Accessibility: doors, fixtures, grab bars etc. for persons with disabilities per good practice [E-2(2)] = IS 4963:2025.
- **3.2** No drainage work without prior sanction; site plan (scale ≥1:500) and drainage plan (scale ≥1:100) required; completion certificate per Annex C.
- **3.3** Plumbing work executed only by licensed/registered plumber.

### 4.3 Materials for pipes (p. 2607–2609)
Salt-glazed stoneware (preferred, acid-resistant), cement concrete (>150mm, sulphate-resisting cement if needed), cast iron (unstable ground, >2.4 m/s velocity, under buildings), asbestos cement (not recommended underground), PVC/HDPE (min 3mm wall if hot water), corrugated pipes, low-noise pipes, under-slung pipes (concealed above false ceiling).

### 4.5 Planning and design
- **4.5.1.3(h)** Fixture spacing: WC/urinal/lavatory/bidet ≥380 mm from side wall centre, ≥760 mm centre-to-centre between fixtures, ≥530 mm clearance in front. WC compartment ≥760×1520 mm (floor-mounted) or 760×1420 mm (wall-hung).
- **4.5.2.2(j)** Sewer self-cleansing velocity: **0.75 m/s** minimum, generally ≤half-full flow. Sewers ≥900 mm below roads, ≥600 mm below fields/gardens.
- **4.5.2.4** Pipe systems: single stack (max 2 toilet connections/floor, min 100mm dia for ≤5 floors), one-pipe partially/fully ventilated, two-pipe, vacuum sewage system (flat terrain/high water table/seaside).
- **4.5.2.4.2** Single-stack safeguards: WC max 2400 mm horizontal from stack; other fixtures max 3500 mm; trap seal depths — WC/floor trap 50mm, branch waste ≥75mm dia 40mm seal, <75mm dia 75mm seal.
- **Table 18** Single stack sizing (p. 2616) — e.g. 100mm stack: 225 DFU (<23m height) / 24 DFU (23–49m) / not permitted ≥49m. **verify against PDF p.2616 — NOT cross-checked** (table not yet in the tables-extraction pass at time of writing).
- **4.5.3.5** Terminal velocity Vt = 3.0(Q/d)^(2/5); terminal length Lt = 0.052·Vt². Flow capacity Q = 27.8·rs^(5/3)·D^(8/3).

### 4.5.3.6 Drain appurtenances (p. 2628–2630)
Minimum waste-outlet diameters: Indian/European WC 100mm, urinal (integral trap) 75mm, wash basin 32mm, floor trap outlet 75mm. Cleanouts at every direction change >45°, base of stacks.

### 4.5.10 Manholes (p. 2633–2639)
- No manhole inside a building or passage. Min depth 800mm.
- **Spacing:** pipe ≤300mm dia → 45m; 301–500mm → 75m; 501–900mm → 90m; beyond 900mm → per local authority.
- **Size:** rectangular 900×800mm (<0.9m depth) to 1200×900mm (0.9–2.5m); arch type 1400×900mm (≥2.5m); circular 900mm dia (0.9–1.65m) up to 1800mm dia (9–14m depth).
- **Wall thickness by depth:** ≤2.25m → 200mm (1 brick); 2.25–3.0m → 300mm; 3.0–5.0m → 400mm; 5.0–9.0m → 500mm; >9.0m → 600mm.
- Bed concrete 1:4:8, min 150mm (<0.9m) to 300mm (>2.5m depth). Brickwork 1:5 cement mortar, English bond. Manhole cover clear opening ≥500mm dia for depth >0.9m.
- Figs 7, 8, 9 — manhole detail sections by depth band (p. 2635, 2636, 2637). Fig. 10 — drop manhole (p. 2640).

### 4.5.11 Storm water drainage (p. 2639–2644)
- Runoff Q = 10·C·i·A (m³/h; C=runoff coeff; i=rainfall intensity mm/h; A=hectares). Runoff coefficients: concrete roof 0.9, paved/asphalt 0.8, unpaved ground 0.3, lawns/parks 0.15 (p. 2640).
- Imperviousness factor: commercial/industrial 70–90%, residential (high density) 60–75%, (low density) 35–60%, parks 10–20% (p. 2640).
- Min design storm intensity for public storm drain connection: **50 mm/h** (4.5.11.5).
- **Table 25** Rainwater pipe sizing by roof area vs rainfall intensity (p. 2643) — e.g. 100mm dia pipe drains 168 m² at 50mm/h, 42 m² at 200mm/h. Formula RA = 0.084·d^(5/2)/I.
- **Table 26** Rainwater harvested volume by roof area × annual rainfall (p. 2647) — e.g. 100m² roof at 1000mm rainfall → 80 m³/yr.
- Fig. 11 — roof drain/weir detail (p. 2643–2644). Fig. 12 — artificial groundwater recharge structure detail, 400/450mm dia bore (p. 2645). Fig. 13 — siphonic drainage system (p. 2648).
- **4.5.12.4** Recharge wells terminate ≥5m above highest natural static subsoil water level.

### 4.5.13 Subsoil water drainage (p. 2649–2650)
Systems: natural, herringbone (branches ≤30m), grid, fan-shaped, moat/cut-off. Min pipe dia: mains 75mm, branches 65mm (100mm/75mm typical practice); laid 60–90cm depth. Fig. 14 (p. 2650).

### 4.6 Construction (p. 2652–2655)
Trench width = pipe dia + 300mm (depth ≤1200mm) or +400mm (depth >1200mm); min 750mm at top for depth >900mm. Bedding/haunching/surround concrete thickness: 100mm (pipe <150mm dia) or 150mm (≥150mm dia) — Figs 15, 16, 17.

### 4.9 Refuse chute system
Cross-referenced to Part E, Section 3 (Solid Waste Management) — see [[Part E Sec 3 - Solid Waste Management]].

### 4.10 Testing (p. 2656–2657)
Smoke test: 25mm water-column pressure, held 15 min. Water test (glazed/concrete pipes): 1.5m head, tolerance 2 L/cm-dia/km over 10 min.

### 4.12 Sewage pumping (p. 2658)
Sump retention period ~15 min; discharge pipe one size above pump outlet, velocity 1 m/s design.

### 4.13 Sewage treatment technologies (p. 2658–2661)
EAASP, SBR, MBR, RBBR/RBC, FBBR/MBBR, SAFF, trickling filters, oxidation ponds, packaged sewage treatment plants (PSTP), biodigesters, septic tanks (incl. rotationally-moulded polyethylene RMPST). Septic tanks recommended only for population ≤300. Every 10°C temperature drop → double septic tank capacity (cold-region design, 4.5.14.5.2). Figs 18, 19 (aerobic/anaerobic scheme, p. 2659).
